# Pitch Timing Calculator

## Purpose
Calculate optimal timing for each section of a pitch based on total allotted time, content type, and audience. Prevents the most common pitch failure: running out of time before the ask, or rushing through proof to hit a deadline.

---

## Standard Pitch Timing Distributions

### 5-Minute Pitch (Investor, Demo Day)

| Section | % of Time | Duration | Slides |
|---------|-----------|----------|--------|
| Hook + Problem | 25% | 75 sec | 2-3 |
| Unique Insight | 15% | 45 sec | 1-2 |
| Solution Overview | 20% | 60 sec | 2-3 |
| Proof/Traction | 20% | 60 sec | 2-3 |
| Ask + Close | 20% | 60 sec | 1-2 |

**Key rule:** No setup longer than 90 seconds. The audience decides in the first 2 minutes whether to keep listening.

### 10-Minute Pitch (Board, Partner)

| Section | % of Time | Duration | Slides |
|---------|-----------|----------|--------|
| Hook | 8% | 48 sec | 1 |
| Problem/Context | 15% | 90 sec | 2-3 |
| Unique Insight | 12% | 72 sec | 1-2 |
| Solution Deep-Dive | 20% | 120 sec | 3-4 |
| Proof/Traction | 20% | 120 sec | 3-4 |
| Vision/Future | 10% | 60 sec | 1-2 |
| Ask + Close | 15% | 90 sec | 1-2 |

**Key rule:** Solution and proof together should be 40% of the pitch. This is where credibility is built.

### 20-Minute Presentation (Keynote, Client)

| Section | % of Time | Duration | Slides |
|---------|-----------|----------|--------|
| Hook + Opening Story | 10% | 120 sec | 2-3 |
| Problem Landscape | 12% | 144 sec | 3-4 |
| Key Insight/Reframe | 10% | 120 sec | 2-3 |
| Framework/Solution | 20% | 240 sec | 5-7 |
| Proof (Case Studies) | 20% | 240 sec | 4-6 |
| Vision + Implications | 13% | 156 sec | 2-3 |
| Q&A Buffer | 5% | 60 sec | 0 |
| Close + CTA | 10% | 120 sec | 1-2 |

**Key rule:** Build in a 5% buffer. Running over time is the mark of an undisciplined presenter.

## Timing Calculation Method

### Step 1: Determine total time
Subtract 10-20% for Q&A or overflow:
- 5-min slot → 5 min (no buffer at this length)
- 10-min slot → 8-9 min usable
- 20-min slot → 16-18 min usable
- 45-min slot → 35-40 min usable

### Step 2: Assign section percentages
Use the distributions above as starting points, adjusted for:
- **Audience knowledge level:** More knowledgeable → less context, more proof
- **Decision stage:** Early stage → more problem/vision; late stage → more proof/ask
- **Complexity:** Complex solution → more solution time; simple → more story time

### Step 3: Calculate word counts per section
Speaking pace benchmarks:
- Conversational pace: 130-150 words per minute
- Energetic pace: 150-170 words per minute
- Deliberate/dramatic pace: 100-120 words per minute

**Formula:** Section words = Section duration (seconds) / 60 * words per minute

### Step 4: Build the timing script

```
PITCH TIMING SCRIPT
====================
Total allotted time: 10 minutes
Usable time: 9 minutes (1 min buffer)
Speaking pace: 140 wpm

SECTION TIMING:
00:00 - 00:48  | HOOK (112 words)
               | Open with the contrarian statement + founding moment
               | CHECKPOINT: If past 1:00, you're behind

00:48 - 02:18  | PROBLEM (210 words)
               | Three-layer problem: external, internal, philosophical
               | CHECKPOINT: Must reach insight by 02:30

02:18 - 03:30  | INSIGHT (168 words)
               | The reframe that makes your approach different
               | This is the "aha" — pause after delivery

03:30 - 05:30  | SOLUTION (280 words)
               | Framework overview + how it works in practice
               | One concrete example woven through

05:30 - 07:30  | PROOF (280 words)
               | Lead case study + 2 supporting data points
               | CHECKPOINT: Must reach close by 07:30

07:30 - 09:00  | ASK + CLOSE (210 words)
               | Specific ask + callback to opening
               | End with one memorable line
```

## Timing Red Flags

| Red Flag | Problem | Fix |
|----------|---------|-----|
| Problem section > 25% of time | Too much context, audience gets impatient | Cut background, start closer to the action |
| Solution section > 30% of time | Feature dump — losing the narrative | Focus on one core mechanism, save details for Q&A |
| No proof section | All claims, no evidence — loses credibility | Add at least one named case study |
| Ask under 10% of time | Rushed close — audience doesn't know what to do | Allocate minimum 60 seconds for the ask |
| No buffer time | Will run over — signals poor preparation | Cut 10% of content from the longest section |

---

## Rehearsal Protocol

1. Time each section independently with a stopwatch
2. If any section runs over by more than 15%, cut content (don't speak faster)
3. Rehearse the full pitch 3x with timing — consistency builds confidence
4. Record the final rehearsal and review for pacing variation
5. Mark natural pause points in the script — pauses are part of the timing
