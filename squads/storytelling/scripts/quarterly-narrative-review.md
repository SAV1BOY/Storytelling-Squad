# Quarterly Narrative Review

## Purpose

Conduct a comprehensive quarterly review of the storytelling squad's narrative health, output quality, pipeline status, and strategic alignment. This cadence ensures continuous improvement and keeps storytelling efforts connected to evolving business objectives.

## Prerequisites

- Story bank master index is up to date with all entries from the quarter
- Quality gate scores are aggregated for all deliverables from the quarter
- Metrics dashboard is populated (see `docs/metrics-and-measurement.md`)
- Narrative audit findings from the most recent audit (if conducted this quarter)
- Input from cross-squad partners on satisfaction and handoff quality
- 90-minute meeting booked with the full storytelling squad and marketing leadership
- Facilitator has prepared the Quarterly Narrative Scorecard template

## Steps

1. **Compile the Quarterly Data Pack (1 week before the review)**
   - Pull total stories produced by status: discovery, drafted, reviewed, approved, published.
   - Calculate average quality gate scores across all dimensions.
   - Gather performance metrics for published stories: engagement, conversion, share rate, time on page.
   - Collect feedback from cross-squad handoffs: Copy, Brand, Traffic satisfaction ratings.
   - Summarize the narrative audit findings if one was conducted.

2. **Prepare the Quarterly Narrative Scorecard**
   - Fill in the scorecard template with:
     - Total output volume vs. target.
     - Average quality score vs. benchmark.
     - Story bank health: entries by status, aging entries (stale for 30+ days), coverage gaps.
     - Voice consistency score from quality gates.
     - Cross-squad satisfaction average.
     - Top 3 published stories by performance metrics.
     - Bottom 3 published stories by performance metrics.

3. **Identify Wins (15 minutes of the meeting)**
   - Present the top-performing stories with data.
   - Highlight what made them effective: framework choice, emotional hook, data integration, visual execution.
   - Recognize individual contributors and collaborative efforts.
   - Discuss: "What can we replicate from these wins?"

4. **Identify Challenges (15 minutes)**
   - Present the underperforming stories with context (not blame).
   - Analyze root causes: wrong framework, weak conflict, missing data, poor distribution timing.
   - Review any quality gate failures and patterns in feedback.
   - Discuss: "What systemic issue caused this, and how do we prevent it?"

5. **Review Story Bank Health (10 minutes)**
   - Walk through the master index statistics.
   - Flag stale entries that have not progressed in 30+ days.
   - Decide for each: advance, reassign, or archive.
   - Identify coverage gaps: personas, products, or journey stages without stories.
   - Set targets for gap closure next quarter.

6. **Assess Voice and Tone Consistency (10 minutes)**
   - Review voice consistency scores from quality gates.
   - Compare this quarter to the previous quarter.
   - If scores have declined, schedule a voice recalibration session (see `scripts/calibrate-voice-and-tone.md`).
   - Note any new products, audiences, or channels that need voice guidance.

7. **Review Cross-Squad Integration (10 minutes)**
   - Share feedback from Copy, Brand, and Traffic squads on handoff quality.
   - Discuss any friction points in the handoff process.
   - Identify stories that were modified significantly after handoff and understand why.
   - Agree on process improvements for next quarter.

8. **Align with Business Objectives (15 minutes)**
   - Review the company's priorities for the upcoming quarter.
   - Map storytelling initiatives to these priorities.
   - Identify new story opportunities: product launches, market expansions, partnerships.
   - Adjust the narrative pipeline to front-load high-priority stories.

9. **Set Quarterly Targets (10 minutes)**
   - Define targets for next quarter:
     - Number of stories to publish.
     - Average quality gate score target.
     - Specific coverage gaps to close.
     - Cross-squad satisfaction target.
     - Number of customer interviews to conduct.
   - Assign owners for each target.

10. **Plan Skill Development (5 minutes)**
    - Based on challenges identified, determine skill gaps.
    - Schedule workshops: improv sessions, framework deep-dives, data storytelling training.
    - Assign reading or case study reviews for the squad.

11. **Document the Review**
    - Capture all decisions, action items, and targets in the review minutes.
    - Distribute within 24 hours to all attendees and stakeholders.
    - Store in the shared drive under `narrative-reviews/[YYYY-Q#]/`.
    - Update `docs/changelog.md` with the review date and key decisions.

12. **Update the Operating Rhythm**
    - Adjust weekly standup agendas based on new priorities.
    - Update the story bank pipeline view to reflect next quarter's focus.
    - Schedule any ad-hoc sessions: calibration, audits, cross-squad syncs.
    - Confirm the date for the next quarterly review.

## Expected Output

- Completed Quarterly Narrative Scorecard with all metrics.
- Documented wins, challenges, and root cause analyses.
- Story bank cleaned: stale entries resolved, coverage gaps identified.
- Quarterly targets set with owners and deadlines.
- Skill development plan for next quarter.
- Review minutes distributed to all stakeholders.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Metrics data is incomplete or unreliable | Use available data and note gaps; prioritize fixing the measurement pipeline as a Q+1 action item |
| Squad members become defensive about underperforming stories | Frame analysis around systemic causes, not individual performance; focus on "what can we change" |
| Leadership priorities shift mid-quarter | Build a 20% buffer into the narrative pipeline for reactive work; reprioritize in a mid-quarter check-in |
| Cross-squad feedback is not forthcoming | Send a structured survey 2 weeks before the review with a clear deadline |
| Too many action items from the review | Prioritize the top 5 using impact-effort scoring; park the rest for mid-quarter reassessment |
