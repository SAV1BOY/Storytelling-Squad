# Run Story Discovery Session

## Purpose

Facilitate a structured story discovery workshop that surfaces compelling narratives from stakeholders, subject-matter experts, and existing materials. The session produces raw story leads that feed directly into the story bank.

## Prerequisites

- A clear discovery brief stating the business objective (product launch, campaign theme, brand refresh, etc.)
- Confirmed participants: 4-8 people including at least one customer-facing team member
- Booked meeting room or video call with recording enabled
- Printed or shared copies of the Story Discovery Canvas (see templates directory)
- Facilitator has reviewed `docs/framework-selection-guide.md` to pre-select 2-3 candidate frameworks
- Whiteboard or digital collaboration board (Miro, FigJam) prepared with session template

## Steps

1. **Prepare the Room (30 minutes before)**
   - Set up the whiteboard with four zones: Context, Characters, Conflict, Change.
   - Place sticky notes, markers, and printed canvases at each seat.
   - Test recording equipment or screen-sharing software.
   - Queue up the discovery brief on the main screen.

2. **Open the Session (5 minutes)**
   - Welcome participants and state the session objective.
   - Explain the ground rules: no idea is too small, stories over statistics, specifics beat generalities.
   - Set the time box: 90 minutes total, with a 5-minute break at the midpoint.

3. **Warm-Up: Story Sparks (10 minutes)**
   - Ask each participant to write down one moment from the past quarter that surprised them about a customer or product.
   - Go around the room; each person shares their moment in 60 seconds or less.
   - Facilitator captures key phrases on the whiteboard under "Context."

4. **Deep Dive: Character Mapping (15 minutes)**
   - Identify the primary characters: customers, internal champions, partners, competitors.
   - For each character, capture: name or archetype, motivation, biggest frustration, desired outcome.
   - Place character cards in the "Characters" zone on the whiteboard.

5. **Conflict Excavation (20 minutes)**
   - Prompt: "What stands between our character and their desired outcome?"
   - Explore external conflicts (market forces, competition) and internal conflicts (doubt, inertia).
   - Use the "5 Whys" technique to dig beneath surface-level problems.
   - Capture conflict statements in the "Conflict" zone.

6. **Midpoint Break (5 minutes)**
   - Participants review the board so far and add any sticky-note additions silently.

7. **Transformation Mapping (15 minutes)**
   - For each character-conflict pair, ask: "What changed, or what could change?"
   - Document before-and-after states using the Change framework.
   - Identify proof points: data, quotes, artifacts that validate the transformation.
   - Place transformation arcs in the "Change" zone.

8. **Story Thread Synthesis (15 minutes)**
   - Facilitator reads back the strongest character-conflict-change combinations.
   - Group votes (dot voting) on the three most compelling story threads.
   - For each top thread, assign a tentative framework from the pre-selected list.

9. **Assign Story Leads (5 minutes)**
   - Each top story thread gets an owner responsible for the next step (interview, data pull, draft).
   - Set deadlines: initial story brief due within 5 business days.
   - Owners confirm they have the resources and access needed.

10. **Close and Document (10 minutes)**
    - Photograph or export the whiteboard.
    - Facilitator commits to distributing session notes within 24 hours.
    - Thank participants and share the timeline for follow-up.

11. **Post-Session: Enter Story Leads into Story Bank**
    - Within 24 hours, create entries in the story bank for each top thread.
    - Tag entries with source, framework, and status ("discovery").
    - Follow `scripts/build-story-bank.md` for entry format.

12. **Post-Session: Debrief with Squad**
    - Share session notes in the `#storytelling-squad` channel.
    - Discuss which frameworks fit and whether additional interviews are needed.
    - Update the quarterly narrative pipeline.

## Expected Output

- 3-5 prioritized story threads with assigned owners and deadlines.
- Completed Story Discovery Canvas (photographed or digital).
- Story bank entries for each thread in "discovery" status.
- Session recording available for team members who could not attend.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Participants default to listing features instead of stories | Redirect with: "Tell me about one person who experienced that feature" |
| One person dominates the conversation | Use round-robin format and written-first exercises |
| No clear conflicts emerge | Probe with: "What almost went wrong?" or "What did the customer try before us?" |
| Too many story threads to prioritize | Add a second round of dot voting with stricter criteria (business impact, feasibility) |
| Recording fails | Assign a dedicated note-taker as backup from the start |
| Remote participants have connectivity issues | Share the collaboration board link in chat; they can contribute asynchronously |
