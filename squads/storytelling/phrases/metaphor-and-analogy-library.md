# Metaphor and Analogy Library

## Purpose
100+ metaphors and analogies organized by category, ready for adaptation into
storytelling across channels and formats.

---

2. "Every pivot is a fork in the trail. You can't see where either path leads, but standing at the fork is not a strategy."
3. "Growth isn't a highway. It's a trail that you clear as you walk it."
4. "The roadmap is useful until the terrain changes. Then you need a compass."
5. "They were halfway across the bridge when they realized they were the ones building it."
6. "Launching a product is jumping off a cliff and assembling the parachute on the way down."
7. "Strategy is choosing which doors to walk through — and which to nail shut."
8. "The first mile is the hardest. Not because of the terrain. Because you haven't committed yet."

## Usage Notes
- **Adapt, don't paste.** Every metaphor should be customized to the specific context.
- **Don't mix metaphors.** One metaphor per passage. "We need to navigate these choppy waters and plant seeds for growth" is chaos.
- **Extend carefully.** A metaphor can be extended 2–3 beats. Beyond that, it collapses.
- **Test for cultural fit.** Some metaphors don't travel. Baseball metaphors mean nothing in Brazil. Cricket metaphors mean nothing in the US.
