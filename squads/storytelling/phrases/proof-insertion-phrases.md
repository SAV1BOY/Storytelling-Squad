# Proof Insertion Phrases

## Purpose
Phrases for inserting data, testimonials, case studies, and evidence naturally into
narrative flow — without breaking the story to cite a statistic.

---

## Data Integration Phrases

### Introducing a Statistic
1. "The numbers tell a clear story: [stat]."
2. "When we measured it, the result surprised us: [stat]."
3. "Here's where it gets concrete: [stat]."
4. "The data confirmed what the team suspected: [stat]."
5. "To put a number on it: [stat]."
6. "It's not just a feeling. [Stat]."
7. "We tracked this for [timeframe]. The result: [stat]."
8. "According to [source], [stat]."
9. "One number tells the whole story: [stat]."
10. "In plain terms: [stat]."

### Making Data Relatable
11. "That's the equivalent of [relatable comparison]."
12. "To put that in perspective, [comparison]."
13. "Imagine [scale analogy]. That's what [stat] looks like in practice."
14. "If you lined up every [thing], it would stretch from [A] to [B]."
15. "That's enough to fill [relatable container] [N] times."
16. "In the time it took you to read this paragraph, [thing happened N times]."

### Hedging Appropriately
17. "The data suggests — though doesn't definitively prove — that [finding]."
18. "Early results indicate [finding]. More data will confirm."
19. "In our sample of [N], [finding]. Your results may vary."
20. "Correlation isn't causation, but the pattern is hard to ignore: [finding]."

## Testimonial Integration Phrases

### Introducing a Quote
21. "One customer said it better than we ever could: '[quote].'"
22. "When we asked [Name] what changed, she didn't hesitate: '[quote].'"
23. "In [Name]'s words: '[quote].'"
24. "[Name], [role] at [company], put it this way: '[quote].'"
25. "The feedback that stopped us in our tracks: '[quote].'"
26. "Three words from a customer email summed it up: '[quote].'"

### Weaving Testimonial into Narrative
27. "She tried the old approach for two years. Then she switched. 'I got more done in the first week than in the last six months,' she told us."
28. "The skeptic in the room was [Name]. Six months later, he was the one presenting the case study."
29. "We didn't have to convince her team. She did it for us. 'Just show them what it did for my numbers,' she said."
30. "His review was one sentence: '[quote].' It became our most-shared testimonial."

## Case Study Integration Phrases

### Introducing a Case
31. "Take [Company]. They were facing [problem]."
32. "One company's experience illustrates this perfectly."
33. "[Company] is a good example. When they [situation], they [action], and the result was [outcome]."
34. "We saw this play out at [Company]."
35. "Let me show you what this looks like in practice."
36. "Here's a real example."

### Summarizing Results
37. "Within [timeframe], [Company] saw [specific result]."
38. "The result: [metric] improved by [percentage] in [timeframe]."
39. "[Company] went from [before metric] to [after metric]."
40. "Six months later, the numbers told the story: [results]."
41. "The ROI was [X]x within [timeframe]."

## Social Proof Phrases

### Scale Signals
42. "Over [N] companies have made this switch."
43. "Trusted by teams at [Company A], [Company B], and [Company C]."
44. "[N] people read this newsletter every week."
45. "Since [date], [N] customers have [action]."

### Authority Signals
46. "Featured in [Publication A], [Publication B], and [Publication C]."
47. "[Expert Name] calls this '[quote].'"
48. "The method used by [recognizable company or person]."
49. "Recommended by [N]% of users in our latest survey."

### Peer Proof
50. "Teams like yours — [size], [industry], [stage] — have seen [result]."
51. "If you're a [role] at a [type of company], this is built for you. Here's why [N] others agree."
52. "Your competitors are already using this approach. Here's what they're seeing."

## Proof Sequencing Patterns

### Single-Proof Sandwich
Story context → Proof → Story continues.
> "She rewrote the onboarding emails using the framework. Open rates jumped from
> 12% to 38% in the first month. By month three, she'd been promoted."

### Proof Stack
Three pieces of evidence in quick succession for cumulative impact.
> "The conversion rate doubled. Customer support tickets dropped 40%. And Net
> Promoter Score hit 72 — the highest in company history."

### Proof as Turning Point
The data is the narrative pivot.
> "For months, the team debated the redesign. Then the Q3 numbers came in:
> retention had dropped to 61%. The debate was over."

---

## Usage Notes
- Never interrupt the emotional peak of a story with data. Let the emotion land, then support it with proof.
- One strong proof point beats three weak ones.
- Attribute data to its source. Unattributed statistics feel made up.
- Round numbers are easier to remember. "About 3x" beats "2.87x" in narrative.
