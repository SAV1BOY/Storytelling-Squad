# Hooks and Openers

## Purpose
100+ hook patterns and story openers organized by type. Grab, adapt, and deploy.

---

## 1. Curiosity Gap Hooks
Open an information gap the reader must close.

1. "What nobody tells you about [topic] is…"
2. "The real reason [surprising outcome] has nothing to do with [obvious cause]."
3. "There's a pattern hiding in every [domain] success story. Here it is."
4. "I've studied [N] [things]. Only one insight actually matters."
5. "Everyone asks how. The better question is why."
6. "The secret isn't what you think it is."
7. "Most people look at [topic] completely backwards."
8. "Here's what [expert/competitor] doesn't want you to know."
9. "Three words changed everything: [three words]."
10. "I found the one thing that separates [winners] from [everyone else]."
11. "The answer was hiding in the data all along."
12. "There's a reason [successful people] never talk about this publicly."

## 2. Contrarian / Challenge Hooks
Disrupt expectations. Make the reader question what they know.

13. "Everything you've been told about [topic] is wrong."
14. "[Common advice] is the worst advice you'll ever get."
15. "Stop [common action]. It's killing your [desired outcome]."
16. "The best [role] I know never [expected behavior]."
17. "What if the thing you're working hardest on doesn't matter at all?"
18. "Forget [popular approach]. Here's what actually works."
19. "[Industry standard] is a lie. Here's the proof."
20. "I used to believe [common belief]. Then I saw the data."
21. "The most successful [group] do the opposite of what gurus recommend."
22. "You don't need more [popular thing]. You need less."

## 3. Specific Outcome Hooks
Lead with a concrete, quantifiable result.

23. "We went from [bad metric] to [good metric] in [timeframe]."
24. "One change. $[amount] in new revenue. Here's the change."
25. "[N]% of [group] fail at [thing]. Here's why the [100-N]% succeed."
26. "In 30 days, we [specific achievement]. It started with a single email."
27. "She turned a $500 investment into a $2M business. Step one was unexpected."
28. "After [N] failed attempts, attempt [N+1] worked. Here's what was different."
29. "The company grew 300% in one year. The strategy fits on a napkin."
30. "Our best-performing [asset] took 47 minutes to create."
31. "One slide. That's what won the $5M deal."
32. "She increased conversions by 340% by removing, not adding."

## 4. Story / Scene Hooks
Drop the reader into a specific moment.

33. "It was 3 a.m. and the server was down."
34. "She walked into the meeting with a resignation letter in her pocket."
35. "The email arrived at 6:47 on a Tuesday morning."
36. "He closed his laptop, stared at the wall, and said, 'We're done.'"
37. "I was standing in the parking lot when I got the call."
38. "The room was silent. Fourteen people, and nobody would speak first."
39. "She picked up the phone, dialed the number, and hung up. Three times."
40. "The first customer walked through the door at 4 p.m. on a rainy Thursday."
41. "I was halfway through the presentation when I realized I was wrong."
42. "The rejection letter was one sentence long."
43. "Two hours before the launch, the CTO quit."
44. "My hands were shaking when I pressed send."

## 5. Question Hooks
Engage by making the reader answer internally.

45. "What would you do with an extra $100K in revenue this year?"
46. "When was the last time a piece of content actually changed your behavior?"
47. "How many meetings did you have this week that could have been an email?"
48. "What if your biggest competitor knew something you don't?"
49. "Have you ever been completely wrong about something you were sure of?"
50. "What's the one metric you'd save if you could only keep one?"
51. "Why do some brands feel like friends and others feel like strangers?"
52. "What would happen if you deleted half your to-do list?"
53. "Do you remember the last ad that made you cry? What was it selling?"
54. "What's the story your customers tell about you when you're not in the room?"

## 6. Confession / Vulnerability Hooks
Signal honesty and earn trust through admission.

55. "I made a $50,000 mistake. Here's what it taught me."
56. "I lied to my team for three months. Here's why."
57. "The campaign everyone praised? It almost bankrupted us."
58. "I'm going to tell you something I've never shared publicly."
59. "My first business failed in four months. My second took fourteen."
60. "I spent a year giving advice I didn't follow myself."
61. "The truth is, I didn't know what I was doing."
62. "I got fired. And it was the best thing that ever happened to my career."
63. "Here's a story I'm embarrassed to tell."
64. "I was wrong. Publicly, loudly, confidently wrong."
65. "The project I'm most proud of is the one that failed."

## 7. Data / Proof Hooks
Lead with evidence that demands attention.

66. "We analyzed [N] [things]. One finding changed everything."
67. "93% of [group] say [thing]. Only 7% actually do it."
68. "The average [thing] costs [amount]. Ours costs [fraction]. Here's how."
69. "In a study of [N] companies, the top performers all did one thing differently."
70. "The data doesn't lie: [surprising finding]."
71. "[N] out of [N] [group] we interviewed said the same thing."
72. "We tracked [metric] for [timeframe]. The results were not what we expected."
73. "Last quarter, [specific data point]. This quarter? [dramatically different data point]."
74. "The correlation was 0.89. In plain English: it's almost a certainty."

## 8. "Imagine" / Future Hooks
Pull the reader into a desired future state.

75. "Imagine opening your laptop Monday morning to zero fires."
76. "Picture this: your pipeline is full and you didn't cold-call anyone."
77. "What if your content created itself? Not literally — but close."
78. "Imagine if every customer who left told you exactly why."
79. "In twelve months, you could be [desirable outcome]. Or you could be right where you are."
80. "Close your eyes. Picture your business three years from now. What's different?"

## 9. List / Framework Hooks
Promise structure and quick value.

81. "Three rules. That's all you need."
82. "The 5-step process behind every viral post."
83. "I follow one framework for every piece I write. Here it is."
84. "7 mistakes killing your conversion rate (you're probably making #3 right now)."
85. "The 2x2 matrix that explains every marketing decision."
86. "One checklist. Ten items. Zero fluff."
87. "Everything I know about [topic] fits into four categories."

## 10. Temporal / Urgency Hooks
Create time pressure or mark a moment.

88. "This changes tomorrow. Here's what you need to know."
89. "Five years ago, [thing] didn't exist. Now it's everything."
90. "You have 90 days before [industry change]. Are you ready?"
91. "Last year I ignored this trend. I won't make that mistake twice."
92. "In 2020, we almost gave up. In 2024, we hit $10M."
93. "The window is closing. Here's why this quarter matters."

## 11. Authority / Quote Hooks
Borrow credibility from a known voice.

94. "Jeff Bezos once said, 'Your brand is what people say when you're not in the room.' He was half right."
95. "A Nobel laureate told me something I'll never forget."
96. "The CEO looked at the board and said four words: 'We're changing everything.'"
97. "My mentor gave me one rule: 'Never sell to someone who doesn't have the problem.'"
98. "'That's the stupidest idea I've ever heard.' — my first investor, two months before writing the check."

## 12. Meta / Self-Aware Hooks
Break the fourth wall.

99. "This is a story about storytelling. I know. Stay with me."
100. "You've read a hundred articles like this. I promise this one is different."
101. "I almost didn't write this. Then I realized that was exactly why I should."
102. "If you're still reading, you're exactly who this is for."
103. "Another LinkedIn post about hustle culture? No. This is the opposite."
104. "I rewrote this opening seven times. This version won."
105. "The most interesting part of this story is the part I can't tell you. But I'll try."
