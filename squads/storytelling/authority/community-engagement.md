# Community Engagement Strategy

## Purpose
A community of storytelling practitioners amplifies your authority, creates a feedback loop for your frameworks, and generates organic demand. This guide covers how to build, nurture, and leverage a storytelling community.

---

## Community Types

### Type 1: Practitioner Community
A group of people who actively use storytelling in their work and want to improve.
- Format: Slack group, Discord, or LinkedIn group
- Size target: 100-500 active members
- Value proposition: peer learning, shared resources, expert access

### Type 2: Client Community
A group of current and past clients who share experiences and support each other.
- Format: Private group, quarterly meetups
- Size target: 20-100 members
- Value proposition: exclusive access, peer support, advanced content

### Type 3: Content Community
An audience that follows your content and engages with it regularly.
- Format: Newsletter, social media following
- Size target: 1,000-10,000+ subscribers/followers
- Value proposition: insights, frameworks, analysis delivered regularly

---

## Building the Community

### Phase 1: Seed (Month 1-3)
- Personally invite 20-30 people you know who care about storytelling
- Create a simple gathering space (Slack channel, monthly call)
- Provide immediate value: share one exclusive framework or tool
- Ask more than you tell — let members shape the community

### Phase 2: Grow (Month 4-9)
- Open to referrals from existing members
- Create a recurring event: monthly story critique session, framework workshop
- Start a content series exclusive to the community
- Identify and empower community champions

### Phase 3: Sustain (Month 10+)
- Establish community rituals: weekly share, monthly challenge, quarterly showcase
- Create member spotlights — feature community members and their work
- Develop peer-to-peer learning: members teach each other
- Build a resource library from community contributions

---

## Community Engagement Tactics

### The Weekly Prompt
Post a storytelling prompt every Monday. Members share their responses. Best responses are featured.
- "Rewrite this corporate mission statement as a 3-sentence story"
- "What's the best hook you've seen this week? Share it with analysis"

### The Monthly Critique
Members submit a piece of narrative work. The community provides feedback using squad rubrics.
- Rotate who submits and who critiques
- Use the story scoring rubric as a shared evaluation framework
- Celebrate improvement over perfection

### The Quarterly Showcase
Members present their best narrative work from the quarter.
- 5-minute presentations followed by discussion
- Builds pride, visibility, and motivation
- Creates content for social proof

### The Expert AMA
Invite a guest expert for a monthly Q&A session.
- Draws new members, provides fresh perspectives
- Topics: specific narrative challenges, industry trends, framework deep-dives

## Community Health Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Active members (posted in last 30 days) | 30%+ of total | Monthly |
| Event attendance | 25%+ of members | Per event |
| New member retention (active after 30 days) | 60%+ | Monthly |
| Member-generated content | 10+ posts per month | Monthly |
| Referral rate (new members from existing) | 3+ per month | Monthly |

---

## Community-to-Business Pipeline

Communities generate business naturally when they are genuinely valuable:
- Members who experience your frameworks become buyers
- Community members refer clients who trust peer recommendations
- Community contributions become content and case studies
- Community feedback improves your frameworks and services

Never sell directly in the community. Let the value create demand organically.
