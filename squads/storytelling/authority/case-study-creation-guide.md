# Case Study Creation Guide

## Purpose
Case studies of your own narrative work are the most powerful credibility tool available. They demonstrate expertise through evidence, not claims. This guide provides the process for creating case studies that showcase storytelling impact.

---

## Case Study Structure

### Section 1: The Client's World Before (20% of case study)
- Industry, size, and relevant context
- The specific narrative problem they faced
- Quantified impact of the problem (lost deals, low engagement, inconsistent messaging)
- Emotional reality: frustration, confusion, missed potential
- Quote from the client describing the before state

### Section 2: The Approach (30% of case study)
- What narrative framework was selected and why
- The discovery process: interviews, audits, research conducted
- Key insight that shaped the strategy
- The narrative architecture designed
- Specific components and patterns used (reference the squad library)

### Section 3: The Implementation (20% of case study)
- Timeline from kickoff to deployment
- Obstacles encountered and how they were resolved
- Collaboration dynamics (who was involved, how decisions were made)
- Moments of doubt or resistance and how they were overcome

### Section 4: The Results (20% of case study)
- Quantified outcomes: metrics before and after
- Timeline to results
- Unexpected benefits that emerged
- Client quote capturing the transformation

### Section 5: The Lesson (10% of case study)
- What this case study teaches about narrative craft
- The principle that others can apply to their own work
- What you would do differently knowing what you know now

---

## Metrics to Capture

### Before/After Pairs
- Pitch win rate: before vs. after narrative revision
- Content engagement: clicks, shares, time-on-page before vs. after
- Sales cycle length: average days from first contact to close
- Brand consistency score: cross-channel audit results
- Customer story retelling rate: can customers retell the brand story?

### Process Metrics
- Time from kickoff to first deliverable
- Number of narrative assets produced
- Team members trained on the framework
- Channels updated with new narrative

---

## Interview Questions for Case Study Subjects

1. "Before we worked together, how would you describe your storytelling situation?"
2. "What was the moment you realized something had to change?"
3. "What surprised you most about the process?"
4. "Can you share a specific moment when you felt the new narrative click?"
5. "What measurable results have you seen?"
6. "How would you describe the difference to a peer who hasn't done this?"
7. "What would you say to someone considering this kind of work?"

---

## Case Study Distribution Plan

Each case study should be adapted for at least 4 channels:
1. **Full case study** (800-1500 words) — website, proposals
2. **Summary version** (200-300 words) — pitch decks, email sequences
3. **Social proof snippet** (50-100 words) — LinkedIn, testimonial sections
4. **Video testimonial script** (60-120 seconds) — website, sales pages

---

## Quality Standards

Before publishing, every case study must:
- [ ] Score 80+ on the story scoring rubric
- [ ] Include at least 2 named proof blocks
- [ ] Feature at least one direct client quote
- [ ] Show measurable before/after results
- [ ] Pass the clarity linter at 85+
- [ ] Be approved by the client for public use
