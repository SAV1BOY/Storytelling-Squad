# Workshop Facilitation Strategy

## Purpose
Workshops are the highest-trust authority format. Participants experience your expertise firsthand through guided practice. A well-run workshop creates advocates, generates leads, and produces content. This strategy covers design, facilitation, and follow-up.

---

## Workshop Types

### Type 1: The Story Extraction Workshop (2-3 hours)
- Participants identify and structure their own stories
- Best for: Marketing teams, sales teams, founders
- Output: Each participant leaves with 2-3 structured story drafts
- Revenue model: Paid workshop or lead generation for consulting

### Type 2: The Narrative Audit Workshop (Half day)
- Team audits their existing narrative assets together
- Best for: Marketing teams, brand teams, agencies
- Output: Audit findings and a prioritized improvement roadmap
- Revenue model: Paid workshop leading to implementation engagement

### Type 3: The Pitch Story Workshop (Full day)
- Participants rebuild their pitch narratives from scratch
- Best for: Startup teams, sales teams, executives
- Output: Revised pitch with rehearsal
- Revenue model: Premium workshop or corporate training

### Type 4: The Improv for Storytelling Workshop (90 minutes)
- Participants practice spontaneous storytelling through improv exercises
- Best for: Team building, creativity unlocking, presentation skills
- Output: Improved confidence and narrative agility
- Revenue model: Corporate team building or conference session

---

## Workshop Design Framework

### Opening (10% of time)
- Hook: Start with a story that demonstrates the workshop's value
- Frame: Set expectations — what they will learn and produce
- Agreement: Establish participation norms (phones away, active participation)

### Core Content (60% of time)
- Alternate between teaching moments (10 min) and practice exercises (15-20 min)
- Every concept must be immediately applied — no extended lectures
- Use the squad's components and patterns as teaching tools
- Provide templates for each exercise (from lib/components/)

### Practice and Feedback (20% of time)
- Participants share their work in small groups
- Facilitator provides live feedback using squad rubrics
- Peer feedback builds community and reinforces learning

### Closing (10% of time)
- Recap the 3 most important takeaways
- Each participant states their one next action
- Provide follow-up resources and contact information

---

## Facilitation Principles

1. **Energy management:** Vary activities to maintain engagement. Sitting, standing, individual, group.
2. **Psychological safety:** Praise publicly, critique privately. Never mock a participant's work.
3. **Concrete over abstract:** Every principle must be demonstrated with a real example.
4. **Time discipline:** Start on time, end on time, keep activities within their allotted windows.
5. **Visible progress:** Participants must leave with tangible output — not just notes.

---

## Post-Workshop Follow-Up

### Within 24 hours
- Send a thank-you email with workshop materials and resources
- Include a feedback survey (3 questions maximum)
- Share a recording of any live demonstrations (if applicable)

### Within 1 week
- Send the follow-up resource pack (templates, frameworks, checklists)
- Offer a 30-minute consultation for participants who want to go deeper
- Connect participants with each other if they expressed interest

### Within 1 month
- Check in on progress — did they implement their next action?
- Share relevant content that builds on workshop themes
- Invite to the next workshop or related event
