# Thought Leadership Strategy

## Purpose
Position yourself or your brand as the recognized authority on business storytelling through strategic content creation, speaking, and community building. Thought leadership is earned through consistent, generous, insight-driven output.

---

## Core Themes

Define 3-5 themes you will own in the storytelling space:

### Theme 1: Story as Business Infrastructure
- Thesis: Story is not marketing decoration — it is the operating system of communication
- Content angles: ROI of narrative, story-driven sales, narrative as competitive advantage
- Audience: Executives, founders, CMOs

### Theme 2: The Science of Retellable Stories
- Thesis: Retellability is designable — it follows principles, not luck
- Content angles: Stickiness research, retelling test methodology, viral story mechanics
- Audience: Content creators, marketers, brand managers

### Theme 3: Narrative Consistency as Brand Asset
- Thesis: Inconsistent stories erode trust faster than bad stories
- Content angles: Cross-channel audits, consistency frameworks, BrandScript methodology
- Audience: Marketing teams, brand managers, agencies

---

## Content Calendar

### Weekly Output
- 1 LinkedIn post analyzing a real-world story or narrative technique (300-500 words)
- 1 short-form insight (tweet, thread, or reel) demonstrating a principle

### Monthly Output
- 1 long-form article or analysis (1000-2000 words) on a core theme
- 1 tool, template, or framework released publicly (builds generosity credibility)

### Quarterly Output
- 1 comprehensive piece: original research, benchmark report, or major case study
- 1 speaking engagement or guest appearance

---

## Channel Strategy

| Channel | Content Type | Frequency | Audience |
|---------|-------------|-----------|----------|
| LinkedIn | Analysis posts, frameworks | 4x/month | Executives, marketers |
| Blog/website | Long-form articles, case studies | 2x/month | Practitioners |
| Email newsletter | Curated insights + original analysis | 2x/month | Engaged subscribers |
| Podcast (own or guest) | Deep-dive conversations | 1-2x/quarter | Industry professionals |
| Speaking events | Keynotes, workshops | 1x/quarter | Conference attendees |

---

## Content Pillars

Each piece of content should fall into one of these categories:

1. **Deconstruction:** Analyze an existing story, campaign, or pitch — explain why it works (or doesn't)
2. **Framework:** Share a reusable tool, template, or process
3. **Case study:** Show your own work with measurable results
4. **Contrarian take:** Challenge conventional wisdom with evidence
5. **Prediction:** Forecast where storytelling is going and why

---

## Measurement

Track these metrics monthly:
- Content engagement rate (likes, comments, shares per post)
- Newsletter subscriber growth
- Inbound inquiries attributable to thought leadership content
- Speaking invitation frequency
- Media mentions and backlinks
- Follower growth across channels
