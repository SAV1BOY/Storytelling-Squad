# Customer Story Interview Guide

> Framework for extracting authentic transformation stories from customers

## Summary

This guide provides a structured methodology for interviewing customers to extract compelling, authentic stories for case studies, testimonials, video content, and brand storytelling. It combines techniques from StoryCorps (deep listening), Matthew Dicks (5-second moment identification), and Kindra Hall (story types and stickiness) to produce stories that are both emotionally resonant and strategically useful.

The goal is not to script a testimonial but to uncover the real transformation story that already exists in the customer's experience.

## Central Thesis

Every customer who has been genuinely helped by a product or service has a transformation story. The interviewer's job is not to create that story but to discover it through skilled questioning, active listening, and narrative pattern recognition.

## Key Concepts

- **The Before/During/After framework** — structure the interview around three phases of the customer's journey
- **Emotion before data** — capture how they felt before capturing what they achieved
- **The specific moment** — find the one moment when everything shifted (Dicks' 5-second moment)
- **Pain amplification** — don't rush past the "before" state; the depth of the problem makes the solution compelling
- **Their words, not yours** — the customer's natural language is more credible than polished marketing copy
- **Permission and safety** — create an environment where honesty is comfortable
- **Multiple story types** — recognize whether this is a Value, Founder, Customer, or Purpose story (Hall)
- **Proof extraction** — capture specific numbers, timelines, and outcomes during the interview
- **The retelling test** — a good customer story can be retold by someone who wasn't there

## Interview Flow (45-60 minutes)

### Phase 1: Warm-Up (5 minutes)
- Build rapport, explain the purpose, get consent for recording
- "Tell me a bit about yourself and what you do"
- "How long have you been using [product/service]?"

### Phase 2: The Before (15 minutes)
- "Take me back to before you found us. What was going on?"
- "What was the biggest challenge you were facing?"
- "How did that challenge affect you personally? Your team? Your business?"
- "What had you tried before that didn't work?"
- "What was the moment when you realized something had to change?"
- "How did it feel to be stuck in that situation?"

### Phase 3: The Discovery and Decision (10 minutes)
- "How did you first hear about [product/service]?"
- "What made you decide to try it? What almost stopped you?"
- "What were your biggest concerns or skepticism?"
- "What was the tipping point — the thing that made you say 'yes'?"

### Phase 4: The During (10 minutes)
- "Walk me through the early experience. What happened first?"
- "Was there a specific moment when you thought 'this is actually working'?"
- "What surprised you most about the experience?"
- "What was harder than expected? What was easier?"

### Phase 5: The After (10 minutes)
- "What's different now compared to before?"
- "Can you give me specific numbers or results?"
- "How has this changed things beyond just the immediate problem?"
- "What would you say to someone who is where you were before?"
- "If you had to describe the transformation in one sentence, what would it be?"

### Phase 6: The Essence (5 minutes)
- "What's the one thing you want people to know about this experience?"
- "Is there anything I didn't ask that you think is important?"

## Post-Interview Processing

1. **Identify the 5-second moment** — the single instant of realization or change
2. **Map the transformation** — FROM [specific state] TO [specific state]
3. **Extract proof points** — numbers, timelines, specific outcomes
4. **Note authentic language** — phrases the customer used that are naturally compelling
5. **Identify the story type** — Value, Customer, Founder, or Purpose (Hall)
6. **Draft the narrative arc** — using the material, not inventing anything
7. **Get approval** — send the story to the customer for review before publishing

## Application to Marketing

1. **Case studies** — full narrative case studies for website, sales collateral
2. **Video testimonials** — use the interview structure for on-camera interviews
3. **Social proof snippets** — extract key quotes for social media, ads, landing pages
4. **Email content** — use customer stories in email sequences
5. **Sales enablement** — arm sales team with customer stories organized by objection/industry
6. **Story bank building** — tag and store in story-bank.yaml for reuse
