# Launch Narrative Campaign

## Project Overview

Design and execute a narrative-driven campaign for a product or service launch. This project replaces the typical feature-announcement approach with a story-driven launch that builds anticipation, creates emotional connection, and drives adoption through narrative rather than hype.

A narrative launch tells the story of why this product exists, who it is for, and how it changes their world. Features are revealed within the context of that story, not as a list.

---

## Scope

### In Scope

- Launch narrative strategy (story arc from pre-launch through post-launch)
- Pre-launch teaser content series (anticipation building)
- Launch day narrative assets (announcement, hero content, social, email)
- Post-launch story continuation (customer early-adopter stories, usage narratives)
- Internal launch narrative (employee alignment and enablement)
- Sales enablement narrative materials
- PR narrative brief and media story angles
- Launch measurement framework

### Out of Scope

- Product naming and visual identity (narrative context only)
- Media buying and ad placement (narrative creative only)
- Technical product documentation
- Event production (narrative content for events, not event logistics)

## Deliverables

1. **Launch Narrative Strategy** — Overall story arc, phases, key messages, and audience segmentation

## Workflow

### Step 1: Product Story Discovery

Interview the product team, founder, and early beta users. Understand: why was this product built? What problem does it solve? What was the moment the team knew it was needed? Who benefits most?

### Step 2: Narrative Architecture

Design the launch story arc across three acts:
- **Act 1 (Pre-Launch)**: Build anticipation through the problem story. What is wrong with the world that this product addresses?
- **Act 2 (Launch)**: Reveal the solution within a transformation narrative. How does this product change the game?
- **Act 3 (Post-Launch)**: Prove the promise through early adopter stories. Real people, real results.

### Step 3: Audience Segmentation

Define 2-3 primary audience segments. For each, identify: their version of the problem, their language, their objections, and the story angle that resonates most.

### Step 4: Pre-Launch Content Production

Create teaser content that hints at the problem and solution without revealing the product. Build curiosity through narrative tension. Use countdowns, behind-the-scenes stories, and problem-focused content.

### Step 5: Launch Day Content Production

Produce all launch day assets: hero content (video or long-form), announcement post, press release, email, social media suite, and internal communication.

### Step 6: Internal Launch

Brief all employees before the public launch. Equip sales, customer success, and support teams with the product story and talking points. Everyone should be able to tell the launch narrative.

### Step 7: Launch Execution

Coordinate simultaneous publishing across all channels. Monitor real-time engagement and audience response. Engage with early reactions through the narrative lens.

### Step 8: Post-Launch Storytelling

Immediately begin gathering early adopter stories. Publish customer narratives that validate the launch promise. Continue the story arc through the post-launch period.

## Success Metrics

### Pre-Launch Metrics

- Teaser content engagement (views, shares, comments)
- Waitlist or pre-registration sign-ups
- Social mention volume and sentiment
- Media interest (journalist inquiries, coverage commitments)

### Launch Day Metrics

- Hero content reach and engagement
- Website traffic spike and session duration
- Social media mention volume and share of voice
- Email open and click-through rates
- Media coverage quantity and quality

### Post-Launch Metrics (30-90 days)

- Product adoption rate vs. target
- Customer story pipeline (early adopters willing to share their story)
- Brand awareness lift (pre/post survey)
- Sales pipeline generated from launch campaign
- Customer acquisition cost for launch cohort
