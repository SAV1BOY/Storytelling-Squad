# Podcast Narrative Format

## Project Overview

Design a podcast narrative format and produce pilot episodes that establish the show's storytelling identity. This project creates the podcast's narrative architecture, episode structure, recurring segments, and brand voice, then validates the concept through 3-4 fully produced pilot episodes.

A narrative podcast is not an interview show with a loose format. It is a carefully designed storytelling vehicle where every element, from the cold open to the closing segment, serves the narrative and the audience experience.

---

## Scope

### In Scope

- Podcast concept development (theme, narrative angle, target audience, positioning)
- Show format design (episode structure, segments, recurring elements, pacing)
- Episode narrative templates and production guides
- Host/narrator voice and persona development
- 3-4 pilot episode scripts (fully produced narrative scripts)
- Guest/subject interview frameworks
- Show branding narrative (name, tagline, description, trailer script)
- Launch strategy and promotional content
- Listener engagement narrative strategy

### Out of Scope

- Audio production and editing (scripts and direction only; production by Audio Squad or vendor)
- Podcast hosting platform setup and RSS feed management
- Music composition and sound design (direction and mood boards only)
- Ongoing episode production beyond pilots (handoff after format validation)

## Workflow

### Step 1: Audience and Market Research

Define the target listener: who they are, what they listen to, what gaps exist in their podcast diet, and what narrative experience they are seeking. Analyze competitive podcasts for format, tone, and audience response.

### Step 2: Concept Development

Define the show concept: what is the unique narrative proposition? What question does every episode answer? What is the emotional experience the listener should have? What makes this show unlike anything else in the category?

### Step 3: Format Architecture

Design the episode structure:
- **Cold Open** (1-2 min): A compelling moment from later in the episode that hooks the listener
- **Introduction** (2-3 min): Host sets the scene, introduces the theme, and makes a promise to the listener
- **Act 1** (8-12 min): The setup, the characters, the world as it was before
- **Act 2** (10-15 min): The conflict, the turning point, the struggle
- **Act 3** (8-10 min): The resolution, the insight, the transformation
- **Closing Segment** (3-5 min): Reflection, listener connection, preview of next episode

### Step 4: Host Voice Development

Define the host's narrative persona: their relationship to the material, their tone (curious, authoritative, warm, provocative), their speech patterns, and how they build intimacy with the listener.

### Step 5: Pilot Episode Development

Select 3-4 episodes that showcase the format's range. For each, develop:
- Research brief with key facts, sources, and story material
- Full narrative script with host narration, interview segments, and production notes
- Guest preparation materials and interview guides

### Step 6: Interview Execution

Conduct guest interviews using narrative interview techniques: open-ended questions, silence as a tool, follow-up on emotional moments, and story-eliciting prompts rather than opinion-seeking questions.

### Step 7: Script Finalization

Weave interview material into the narrative script. Structure each episode as a complete story with a clear arc, emotional beats, and a satisfying conclusion.

### Step 8: Launch Preparation

Produce the trailer script. Develop the launch strategy: pre-launch buzz, launch week execution, guest and partner amplification, and initial listener engagement tactics.

## Success Metrics

### Pilot Performance Metrics

- Listener completion rate per pilot episode (target: >65%)
- Listener drop-off analysis (where do listeners disengage?)
- Review and rating quality from early listeners
- Social sharing and word-of-mouth indicators

### Audience Growth Metrics

- Subscriber growth rate in first 90 days
- Download numbers per episode
- Listener retention (episode-over-episode return rate)
- Geographic and demographic reach

### Engagement Metrics

- Listener feedback volume and quality (reviews, emails, social mentions)
- Community participation (if listener engagement tactics are deployed)
- Guest inquiry rate (are potential guests approaching the show?)
- Cross-platform engagement (social, newsletter, website traffic from podcast)

### Business Impact Metrics

- Brand awareness lift attributed to podcast
- Lead generation from podcast (show note links, CTAs)
- Thought leadership positioning (media mentions, speaking invitations)
- Partnership and sponsorship interest
