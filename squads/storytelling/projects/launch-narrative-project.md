# Launch Narrative Project

## Purpose
Create the narrative arc for a product, feature, or brand launch across all channels.

---

## Project Overview
- **Duration:** 2-4 weeks
- **Agents:** Story Architect (lead), Brand Narrator, Hook Specialist, Emotional Arc Designer

---

## Phase 1: Launch Story Architecture (Week 1)
- Define the launch narrative thesis: Why does this exist now?
- Design the three-act launch arc: Anticipation → Reveal → Activation
- Create the launch BrandScript: character (customer), problem (what they lack), guide (brand), plan (how to adopt), CTA, failure (life without it), success (life with it)
- Identify 3-5 stories to weave through the launch sequence

## Phase 2: Channel Narrative Design (Week 2)
- Pre-launch teaser content (social, email): Mystery and anticipation hooks
- Launch day content: The big reveal — maximum emotional impact
- Post-launch content: Proof, stories, social proof cascade
- Create channel-specific scripts for each phase

## Phase 3: Production (Week 2-3)
- Write all content across channels with narrative continuity
- Design email sequence: 5-7 emails with escalating narrative arc
- Create social content: 10-15 posts following the launch arc
- Write landing page narrative, press release narrative, and internal comms

## Phase 4: Validation and Launch (Week 3-4)
- Score all key pieces with appropriate rubrics
- Run consistency checker across all launch content
- Rehearse any live launch events
- Execute launch sequence per the calendar

---

## Success Criteria
- Narrative consistency score across all launch channels: 85+
- Pre-launch engagement rates exceed baseline by 2x
- Launch day conversion exceeds target
- Post-launch story bank receives 2+ new customer stories within 30 days
