# Founder Story Project

## Purpose
Extract, structure, and polish the founder's personal narrative for use across pitch decks, websites, speaking engagements, and brand content.

---

## Project Overview
- **Duration:** 2-3 weeks
- **Agents:** Story Architect (lead), Character Designer, Hook Specialist, Clarity Editor
- **Deliverables:** Founder story in 4 lengths, character profile, beat sheet

---

## Phase 1: Extraction (Week 1)

### The Founder Interview (90 minutes)
Core questions:
1. What personal experience made this problem unacceptable to you?
2. What was the moment of insight that became the company's core thesis?
3. What did you sacrifice or risk to pursue this?
4. What was the closest the company came to failure?
5. What moment proved to you that the thesis was right?
6. What do you believe about this industry that most people get wrong?
7. What does the world look like when your mission succeeds?

### Supporting Research
- Review founder's public content (interviews, social media, press)
- Interview 2-3 early employees or partners for external perspective
- Identify the founder's natural storytelling moments (where they light up)

### Outputs
- Interview transcript and notes
- Key narrative moments identified
- Character architecture draft (want/need/lie/flaw)

---

## Phase 2: Architecture (Week 1-2)

### Activities
- Map the founder arc: Origin Wound, Epiphany, First Believer, Trial by Fire, Mission Crystallization
- Design the emotional arc for the story
- Select 3 hook alternatives and score them
- Create the beat sheet
- Identify proof blocks to embed

### Outputs
- Founder arc map
- Emotional arc design
- Hook alternatives with scores
- Beat sheet

---

## Phase 3: Writing (Week 2-3)

### Create Four Versions
1. **Full narrative** (800-1200 words): The complete founder story for website "About" page or keynote
2. **Pitch version** (200-300 words): For pitch decks and investor meetings
3. **Social version** (100-150 words): For LinkedIn, bios, and introductions
4. **Micro version** (50 words): For email signatures, speaker introductions, event programs

### Quality Gates
- Each version scored with story scoring rubric
- Clarity linter applied to all versions
- Hook strength scored for each version's opening

---

## Phase 4: Approval and Deployment

### Activities
- Present all versions to the founder for review and approval
- Revise based on feedback (founder must feel authentic, not polished beyond recognition)
- Create usage guidelines: when to use which version
- Add to the story bank with appropriate tags

### Outputs
- 4 approved founder story versions
- Usage guidelines
- Story bank entry
