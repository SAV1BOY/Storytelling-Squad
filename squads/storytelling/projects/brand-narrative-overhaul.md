# Brand Narrative Overhaul

## Project Overview

A full redesign of an organization's brand narrative, from foundational story architecture through channel-specific execution. This project replaces fragmented, feature-driven messaging with a cohesive, emotionally resonant brand story that aligns all communications around a single narrative thread.

This is the Storytelling Squad's flagship engagement, typically triggered by a rebrand, new leadership, market repositioning, or the recognition that current messaging is not working.

---

## Scope

### In Scope

- Brand narrative audit (current state assessment across all touchpoints)
- Competitive narrative analysis (how competitors position their stories)
- Stakeholder interviews (leadership, customers, employees, partners)
- Core narrative architecture (origin, mission, customer transformation, vision)
- Brand voice and tone framework
- Narrative guidelines document for cross-functional use
- Channel adaptation for website, sales, social, email, and PR
- Internal launch and training for key teams

### Out of Scope

- Visual identity and design system (coordinate with Design Squad)
- Full website redesign (narrative copy only, not UX/UI)
- Paid media creative production (strategy and messaging only)
- Ongoing content production (handed off after launch)

---

## Deliverables

1. **Narrative Audit Report** — Assessment of current brand storytelling across all channels with gap analysis
2. **Competitive Narrative Map** — Visual mapping of competitor narrative positions and white space
3. **Core Brand Narrative Document** — The definitive brand story: origin, mission, values, customer transformation, vision
4. **Brand Voice and Tone Guide** — Parameters for how the brand sounds across contexts
5. **Channel Narrative Playbook** — Adapted messaging frameworks for website, sales, social, email, and PR
6. **Elevator Pitches** — 10-second, 30-second, and 2-minute versions of the brand story
7. **Internal Launch Deck** — Presentation for rolling out the new narrative to internal teams
8. **Training Workshop Materials** — Facilitator guide and exercises for narrative training sessions
9. **Narrative Measurement Framework** — KPIs and tracking methodology for narrative effectiveness

## Workflow

### Step 1: Kick-Off and Alignment

Conduct kick-off meeting with key stakeholders. Align on objectives, constraints, timeline, and success criteria. Identify interview subjects and schedule sessions.

### Step 2: Narrative Audit

Review all existing brand communications: website, social media, sales materials, email campaigns, PR, internal documents. Catalog current narrative elements, inconsistencies, and gaps.

### Step 3: Stakeholder Interviews

Conduct 15-25 interviews across leadership, customers, employees, and partners. Use structured interview guides designed to surface story material, not just opinions.

### Step 4: Competitive Narrative Analysis

Map competitor narratives across positioning, voice, story types, and themes. Identify narrative white space where the brand can own a distinct story.

### Step 5: Narrative Architecture

Synthesize research into the core narrative: origin story, mission narrative, customer transformation story, and vision narrative. Present to leadership for alignment.

### Step 6: Voice and Tone Development

Define the brand's voice parameters: personality traits, vocabulary preferences, formality range, humor approach, and channel-specific adaptations.

### Step 7: Channel Adaptation

Translate the core narrative into channel-specific messaging: website copy, sales talking points, social media narratives, email sequences, and PR positioning.

### Step 8: Internal Launch

Present the new narrative to internal teams. Conduct workshops for sales, marketing, customer success, and leadership. Equip teams with tools and training to deliver the narrative consistently.

## Success Metrics

### Leading Indicators (0-3 months post-launch)

- Internal narrative adoption rate (% of teams using new messaging)
- Brand voice consistency score across channels
- Employee ability to articulate the brand story (survey-based)
- Website engagement metrics on narrative-driven pages

### Lagging Indicators (3-12 months post-launch)

- Brand recall improvement (pre/post survey)
- Conversion rate change on key pages
- Sales cycle length change
- Net Promoter Score movement
- Media coverage quality and narrative alignment
