# Keynote Presentation Project

## Purpose
Create a keynote narrative for conferences, company events, or public presentations.

---

## Project Overview
- **Duration:** 2-4 weeks
- **Agents:** Story Architect (lead), Emotional Arc Designer, Hook Specialist, Scene Builder
- **Deliverables:** Full keynote script, slide narrative, emotional arc map, rehearsal timing

---

## Phase 1: Concept and Research (Week 1)
- Define the single core message the audience will take away
- Research the audience: who they are, what they already know, what they need
- Study the event: theme, other speakers, audience size, format
- Identify 2-3 signature stories that illustrate the core message
- Select a structural framework (typically Duarte sparkline + Hero's Journey elements)

## Phase 2: Architecture (Week 1-2)
- Design the full narrative arc (beginning, middle, end)
- Plan the emotional arc: where are the peaks, valleys, and rest points?
- Build the beat sheet with 6-10 major beats
- Design the callback pattern (opening and closing)
- Identify the 1-2 "retellable moments" — the parts the audience will share

## Phase 3: Writing (Week 2-3)
- Write the full script (speaking pace: 130-140 wpm)
- Build 2-3 complete scenes using five commandments
- Design transitions between major sections
- Insert proof blocks where credibility is needed
- Apply clarity and trim rules

## Phase 4: Rehearsal and Refinement (Week 3-4)
- Time the full keynote — target 90% of allotted time
- Rehearse 5+ times with timing
- Record one rehearsal and review for pacing, energy, and clarity
- Get feedback from 1-2 trusted advisors
- Final script polish and memorization of key transitions

---

## Success Criteria
- Story quality score: 85+
- Emotional arc has at least one peak of 8+
- Callback pattern successfully connects opening and closing
- Delivery time within allotment
- At least one retellable moment designed
