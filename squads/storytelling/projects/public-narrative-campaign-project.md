# Public Narrative Campaign Project

## Purpose
Design a narrative-driven campaign for public audiences — brand awareness, social impact, or cultural positioning.

---

## Project Overview
- **Duration:** 4-8 weeks
- **Agents:** Brand Narrator (lead), Story Architect, Emotional Arc Designer, Character Designer

---

## Phase 1: Campaign Narrative Strategy (Week 1-2)
- Define the campaign thesis: What truth must the audience accept?
- Identify the cultural tension the campaign addresses
- Select the campaign genre (from genres taxonomy)
- Design the campaign character: real person, composite, or brand-as-character
- Map the target emotional journey: what does the audience feel at each stage?

## Phase 2: Story Development (Week 2-4)
- Write the anchor story: the centerpiece narrative (video script, long-form article, or keynote)
- Create 5-10 supporting micro-stories that extend the anchor from different angles
- Design the social narrative: how does the story spread on social platforms?
- Build proof blocks: research data, testimonials, third-party validation

## Phase 3: Channel Adaptation (Week 4-6)
- Video script(s) for anchor and supporting content
- Social media content series (15-25 pieces)
- Email sequence for engaged audience
- PR narrative for media outreach
- Landing page or microsite narrative

## Phase 4: Launch and Measurement (Week 6-8)
- Execute campaign launch per distribution plan
- Monitor narrative reception: comments, shares, sentiment
- Collect audience stories that emerge from the campaign
- Measure against success criteria

---

## Success Criteria
- Reach target (audience-specific)
- Engagement rate exceeds industry benchmark by 2x
- Earned media coverage
- Audience-generated content incorporating campaign narrative
- Brand sentiment shift (measured via survey or social listening)
