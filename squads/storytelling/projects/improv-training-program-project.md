# Improv Training Program Project

## Purpose
Design and run an improv-based training program that develops spontaneous storytelling, active listening, and narrative agility in participants.

---

## Project Overview
- **Duration:** 8-12 weeks (1 session per week)
- **Format:** 60-90 minute sessions, in-person or virtual
- **Group size:** 8-16 participants

---

## Program Structure

### Module 1: Foundations (Weeks 1-3)
**Session 1: Yes, And**
- The core improv principle: accept and build on what's offered
- Exercise: One-word story circle, yes-and scene building
- Application: How "yes, and" improves brainstorming and narrative collaboration

**Session 2: Active Listening**
- Exercise: Repeat-back storytelling (tell me what you heard)
- Exercise: Last-word-first-word (next person starts with the last word said)
- Application: Listening is the foundation of customer story extraction

**Session 3: Specificity**
- Exercise: Five senses description (describe a scene using all senses)
- Exercise: "Be specific" challenge (replace every vague word with a specific one)
- Application: Specific details make stories vivid and retellable

### Module 2: Story Building (Weeks 4-6)
**Session 4: Character Creation**
- Exercise: Character hot seat (audience interviews a character you create)
- Exercise: Status exercises (play high/low status in conversations)
- Application: Building dimensional characters for case studies and brand stories

**Session 5: Conflict and Stakes**
- Exercise: Escalation scenes (each line raises the stakes)
- Exercise: Dilemma scenes (character must choose between two goods)
- Application: Designing conflict that creates narrative tension

**Session 6: Structure Under Pressure**
- Exercise: Two-minute story challenge (tell a complete story in 2 minutes)
- Exercise: Story spine (once upon a time, every day, until one day, because of that, until finally)
- Application: Rapid narrative construction for pitches and presentations

### Module 3: Performance (Weeks 7-9)
**Session 7: Hook Workshop**
- Exercise: Hook battle (pairs compete to create the most compelling opening)
- Exercise: Contrarian hook generator
- Application: Writing hooks that stop scrolling and start listening

**Session 8: Emotional Range**
- Exercise: Same scene, different emotions (play the same scene 5 ways)
- Exercise: Emotional arc in 90 seconds
- Application: Designing emotional variation in presentations and content

**Session 9: Pitch Improv**
- Exercise: Pitch anything (receive a random product, pitch it in 60 seconds)
- Exercise: Objection handling through story (respond to objections with scenes)
- Application: Fluid, confident pitching under pressure

### Module 4: Integration (Weeks 10-12)
**Session 10-11: Story Slam Practice**
- Participants prepare and perform 5-minute personal stories
- Peer feedback using squad scoring rubrics
- Revision and re-performance

**Session 12: Showcase and Graduation**
- Each participant performs their best story
- Group reflection on growth
- Certificates and next steps

---

## Facilitation Notes
- Create psychological safety first — no one is judged for improv attempts
- Warm up every session with a physical/vocal exercise (even virtual)
- Debrief every exercise: "What did that teach us about storytelling?"
- Record sessions (with permission) for participant review
