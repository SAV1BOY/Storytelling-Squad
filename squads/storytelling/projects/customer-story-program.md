# Customer Story Program

## Project Overview

Build a systematic customer story program from the ground up. This project creates the infrastructure, processes, and templates needed to continuously gather, produce, and distribute customer stories at scale. The goal is to turn customer stories into the organization's most valuable and prolific content type.

Unlike one-off case studies, this program creates a repeatable engine that produces 2-4 customer stories per month across multiple formats and channels.

---

## Scope

### In Scope

- Customer story program strategy and editorial calendar
- Interview framework and question guides
- Story production templates (written, video script, social)
- Customer recruitment and consent process
- Story asset management system
- Distribution and amplification strategy
- Quality standards and review process
- Program measurement framework

### Out of Scope

- Video production (provide scripts and direction; production by Video Squad)
- Paid media distribution (strategy only; execution by Performance Squad)
- CRM integration (requirements only; implementation by Ops Squad)
- Legal review of consent forms (draft provided; legal review by client legal team)

## Deliverables

1. **Program Strategy Document** — Vision, goals, target cadence, audience segmentation, and editorial approach
2. **Customer Recruitment Playbook** — How to identify, approach, and secure customer story participants
3. **Interview Guide Kit** — Question frameworks for different story types (transformation, ROI, innovation, partnership)
4. **Consent and Release Templates** — Standardized consent forms with tiered permission levels
5. **Story Production Templates** — Written case study, short-form story, social snippet, video script, and podcast segment templates
6. **Editorial Calendar** — 6-month forward-looking story calendar with themes and targets
7. **Distribution Playbook** — Channel-specific distribution strategy for each story format
8. **Story Asset Library Structure** — Taxonomy, tagging, and storage system for story assets
9. **Quality Review Checklist** — Standards and review criteria for all published stories
10. **Program Dashboard** — Metrics tracking and reporting framework

## Workflow

### Step 1: Define Story Types and Audience Segments

Establish which types of customer stories serve which business objectives and audience segments. Map story types to funnel stages and use cases.

### Step 2: Build Recruitment Pipeline

Work with sales, customer success, and account management to identify story candidates. Create a nomination process and scoring criteria for prioritization.

### Step 3: Design Interview Process

Develop interview guides for each story type. Train interviewers on empathetic listening, follow-up questioning, and story identification techniques.

### Step 4: Establish Consent Process

Create tiered consent forms that cover all anticipated uses. Build a tracking system for consent status, expiration, and renewal.

### Step 5: Produce Pilot Stories

Select 4-6 diverse customers for the pilot batch. Interview, draft, review, and publish across primary channels. Document lessons learned.

### Step 6: Refine and Systematize

Based on pilot results, refine templates, process, and quality standards. Create standard operating procedures for ongoing production.

### Step 7: Launch Full Program

Activate the editorial calendar. Begin recruiting at steady-state cadence. Implement distribution playbook. Start tracking program metrics.

### Step 8: Optimize and Scale

Review monthly performance data. Optimize story types, channels, and production process. Scale production cadence based on results.

## Success Metrics

### Program Health Metrics

- Stories produced per month (target: 2-4 at steady state)
- Customer recruitment success rate (target: >40% of approached customers agree)
- Time from interview to publication (target: <3 weeks)
- Consent renewal rate (target: >80%)
- Story asset utilization rate (% of stories used across 3+ channels)

### Performance Metrics

- Story content engagement rate vs. non-story content
- Conversion rate on pages featuring customer stories
- Sales team usage rate of story assets
- Social sharing rate of customer stories
- Pipeline influence attributed to customer story content

### Business Impact Metrics

- Influenced pipeline value from story-exposed prospects
- Customer story contribution to close rate improvement
- Brand trust score change correlated with story program
- Customer advocacy increase (NPS, referral rate)
