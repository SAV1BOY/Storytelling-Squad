# Brand Story Creation Project

## Purpose
Create or refresh the core brand narrative — the foundational story that all marketing, sales, and communications are built upon.

---

## Project Overview
- **Duration:** 3-4 weeks
- **Agents:** Brand Narrator (lead), Story Architect, Character Designer, Emotional Arc Designer, Clarity Editor
- **Deliverables:** Master brand narrative, BrandScript, channel-adapted versions, consistency guidelines

---

## Phase 1: Discovery (Week 1)

### Activities
- Founder interview (60-90 minutes): origin wound, epiphany, mission, values
- Customer interviews (3-5 customers, 30 minutes each): how they describe the brand
- Competitor narrative audit: how competitors tell their stories
- Internal stakeholder interviews (3-5): what the team believes the brand stands for
- Existing asset review: current website, pitch deck, marketing materials

### Outputs
- Discovery summary document
- Key themes and narrative tensions identified
- Raw story material collected

---

## Phase 2: Architecture (Week 2)

### Activities
- Complete BrandScript (all 7 elements)
- Define brand archetype (primary + secondary) from Campbell's 12
- Design the brand transformation arc (founder story or brand evolution)
- Select narrative framework (StoryBrand + supporting framework)
- Draft story beat sheet for the master narrative

### Outputs
- Completed BrandScript
- Brand archetype definition
- Story arc outline with beat sheet
- Framework selection rationale

---

## Phase 3: Creation (Week 3)

### Activities
- Write master brand narrative (1000-1500 words)
- Design emotional arc using emotion mapping rubric
- Insert proof blocks at key credibility moments
- Apply clarity linter and trim rules
- Create channel-adapted versions: website (300 words), pitch (150 words), social (75 words)

### Outputs
- Master brand narrative
- Emotional arc map
- Channel-adapted versions (4-6 formats)

---

## Phase 4: Validation and Deployment (Week 4)

### Activities
- Score master narrative with story scoring rubric (target: 80+)
- Score clarity with clarity score calculator (target: 85+)
- Run BrandScript completeness check
- Present to stakeholders for feedback
- Create consistency guidelines for cross-channel deployment
- Brief all content creators on the new narrative framework

### Outputs
- Quality scores and validation report
- Consistency guidelines document
- Team briefing materials
- Final approved brand narrative

---

## Success Criteria
- BrandScript: 7/7 elements complete
- Story quality score: 80+
- Clarity score: 85+
- Stakeholder approval achieved
- All content creators briefed
