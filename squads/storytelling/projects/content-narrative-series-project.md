# Content Narrative Series Project

## Purpose
Design a multi-part content series (blog, email, video, podcast) with a cohesive narrative arc that builds audience engagement progressively.

---

## Project Overview
- **Duration:** 2-3 weeks for design; 4-12 weeks for production
- **Agents:** Story Architect (lead), Hook Specialist, Proof Curator

---

## Phase 1: Series Architecture (Week 1)
- Define the series thesis: What transformation does the audience undergo?
- Design the series arc: each installment is one beat in a larger story
- Plan the cliffhanger strategy: how does each piece drive to the next?
- Determine cadence: weekly, bi-weekly, or daily?

## Phase 2: Installment Design (Week 1-2)
- Outline each installment with:
  - Core topic/lesson
  - Story or example used
  - Hook type
  - Emotional target
  - Cliffhanger or callback to next installment
  - CTA (transitional for early installments, direct for final)

## Phase 3: Production (Week 2-3 for initial batch)
- Write the first 3-4 installments before publishing (build a buffer)
- Score each with story scoring rubric
- Ensure narrative continuity: callbacks, recurring characters or themes
- Format for target channel

## Phase 4: Publication and Optimization (Ongoing)
- Publish per cadence
- Monitor engagement per installment — which topics/hooks perform best?
- Adjust future installments based on data (without breaking the arc)
- Collect audience feedback and incorporate

---

## Series Structure Templates

### The Teaching Series (6-8 installments)
1. The Problem (why this matters)
2-6. One principle per installment with story illustration
7. The Integration (how it all fits together)
8. The Challenge (audience applies what they learned)

### The Story Series (4-6 installments)
1. Setup: introduce the character and world
2-4. Rising action: complications and discoveries
5. Climax: the turning point
6. Resolution and lessons

### The Case Study Series (4-5 installments)
1. The Before: the client's world before
2. The Approach: what was done and why
3. The Journey: implementation and obstacles
4. The Results: measurable outcomes
5. The Lessons: what this means for the reader
