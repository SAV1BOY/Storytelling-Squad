# Employer Brand Story

## Project Overview

Build a comprehensive employer brand storytelling program that attracts, engages, and retains talent through authentic employee narratives. This project creates the strategy, infrastructure, and content engine for telling the organization's culture story through the voices of its people.

In a competitive talent market, the organizations that tell the most authentic and compelling culture stories win the best candidates. This is not about polished recruitment marketing. It is about real stories from real employees that reveal what it is actually like to work here.

---

## Scope

### In Scope

- Employer brand narrative audit (current EVP, careers page, Glassdoor, social)
- Employee interview program (20-30 interviews across roles, levels, and locations)
- Employer brand narrative framework (culture story, values in action, growth stories)
- Content production across written, video, and social formats
- Careers page narrative redesign (copy and structure)
- Recruitment marketing content package
- Employee advocacy storytelling toolkit
- Employer brand measurement framework

### Out of Scope

- Careers page design and development (copy only)
- ATS or recruitment platform setup
- Compensation and benefits strategy
- Employee engagement survey design (we use results, not design the survey)

## Workflow

### Step 1: Employer Brand Audit

Assess current employer brand touchpoints: careers page, job descriptions, Glassdoor reviews, LinkedIn presence, recruitment marketing, onboarding materials, and internal communications. Identify narrative strengths and gaps.

### Step 2: Employee Interview Program

Conduct 20-30 interviews across a representative sample of the organization. Interview for stories, not opinions. Focus on: why they joined, a moment they felt proud, a time they were challenged, what they would tell a friend considering applying.

### Step 3: Competitive Employer Narrative Analysis

Review how competitors position their employer brand. Identify narrative themes in the space and find the differentiated story the client can own.

### Step 4: Employer Narrative Framework

Define the core employer brand story: what is it like to work here, told through the lens of growth, impact, culture, and belonging. Establish 3-4 narrative pillars that organize all employer brand content.

### Step 5: Story Production

Produce employee stories across written profiles, social posts, video scripts, and quote graphics. Each story demonstrates a narrative pillar through a specific person's experience.

### Step 6: Careers Page Narrative

Rewrite the careers section with story-driven copy. Replace generic claims with specific employee stories. Structure the page as a narrative journey from "who we are" through "what it is like here" to "your place in the story."

### Step 7: Employee Advocacy Launch

Equip employees with a storytelling toolkit: guidelines for sharing their experience, templates for LinkedIn posts, approval process for company mentions, and examples of great employee stories.

### Step 8: Measurement and Iteration

Track employer brand storytelling metrics. Gather feedback from recruiting teams and candidates. Identify which stories resonate most and produce more in that vein.

## Success Metrics

### Recruitment Metrics

- Qualified application volume increase (target: 25-40% within 6 months)
- Application completion rate improvement
- Candidate quality score from hiring managers
- Time to fill for key roles
- Offer acceptance rate

### Engagement Metrics

- Careers page traffic and time on page
- Employee story content engagement (views, shares, comments)
- Employee advocacy participation rate (% of employees sharing stories)
- Glassdoor rating trend
- LinkedIn follower growth and engagement rate

### Retention Metrics

- New hire 90-day retention rate (are the stories attracting the right people?)
- Employee engagement survey scores related to culture and belonging
- Internal referral rate (employees referring their network)
- Regrettable turnover trend
