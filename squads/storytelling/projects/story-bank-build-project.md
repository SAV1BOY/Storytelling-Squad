# Story Bank Construction Project

## Purpose
Build a comprehensive, tagged, searchable story bank from scratch or from existing scattered assets.

---

## Project Overview
- **Duration:** 4-6 weeks for initial build; ongoing maintenance
- **Agents:** Story Bank Manager (lead), Story Architect, Proof Curator

---

## Phase 1: Inventory Existing Stories (Week 1)
- Audit all locations where stories currently live: website, sales decks, case studies, email templates, social media archives, meeting recordings
- Extract every identifiable story (even fragments and anecdotes)
- Create a raw inventory spreadsheet: source, format, subject, approximate quality

## Phase 2: Define the Taxonomy (Week 1-2)
- Establish tagging dimensions per story-bank-tagger.md
- Define priority industries, channels, and emotions based on business needs
- Set minimum coverage targets per the gap detector framework
- Create the tag vocabulary document

## Phase 3: Tag and Assess (Week 2-4)
- Tag each story across all dimensions
- Score each story using the story scoring rubric (at minimum: quick assessment)
- Classify: ready to deploy, needs revision, needs rewrite, archive
- Identify the strongest stories per category

## Phase 4: Fill Critical Gaps (Week 4-6)
- Run the gap detector against the tagged bank
- Prioritize gaps by business impact
- Create a collection plan for missing stories
- Produce 5-10 new stories to fill the most critical gaps

## Phase 5: Organize and Deploy (Week 5-6)
- Set up the story bank in the chosen platform (database, shared drive, CMS)
- Create retrieval guides for sales, marketing, and leadership teams
- Train stakeholders on how to find and use stories
- Establish the maintenance cadence (monthly spot-check, quarterly full review)

---

## Success Criteria
- Minimum 20 tagged stories in the bank at launch
- Coverage across all primary story types (8 types)
- Coverage in at least 3 priority industries
- At least 5 stories scored 75+
- Stakeholders trained and accessing the bank within 2 weeks of launch
