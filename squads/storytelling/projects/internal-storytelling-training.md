# Internal Storytelling Training

## Project Overview

Design and deliver an internal storytelling training program that builds narrative capability across teams. This project equips employees in marketing, sales, customer success, leadership, and HR with the skills to find, craft, and deliver stories that advance business objectives.

Storytelling capability should not be confined to a single team. When every department can tell the brand story and gather customer stories, the entire organization becomes a storytelling engine.

---

## Scope

### In Scope

- Training needs assessment (current storytelling capability across teams)
- Curriculum design for 3-4 role-specific training tracks
- Workshop materials (facilitator guides, participant workbooks, exercises)
- Core storytelling skills module (universal for all tracks)
- Role-specific modules (sales storytelling, leadership storytelling, customer story gathering)
- Practice exercises with real brand stories and scenarios
- Certification criteria and assessment rubrics
- Train-the-trainer materials for internal facilitators
- Post-training reinforcement program

### Out of Scope

- LMS platform setup or e-learning production (content only)
- Ongoing coaching beyond the program (recommendations for continued development)
- External customer-facing content production (training focus only)
- Performance management integration (recommendations only)

---

## Deliverables

1. **Training Needs Assessment** — Current capability baseline, skill gaps, and priority areas by team
2. **Curriculum Architecture** — Program structure, learning tracks, module sequence, and time requirements
3. **Core Module: Storytelling Fundamentals** — Universal module covering narrative structure, story finding, audience-first thinking
4. **Sales Storytelling Module** — Story-driven selling techniques, customer scenario narratives, objection handling through story
5. **Leadership Storytelling Module** — Vision stories, change narratives, culture stories, meeting storytelling
6. **Customer Story Gathering Module** — Interview techniques, consent process, story extraction and drafting
7. **Facilitator Guide** — Complete guide for internal trainers to deliver each module
8. **Participant Workbook** — Exercises, templates, and reference materials for all participants
9. **Assessment and Certification Rubric** — Criteria for evaluating storytelling capability and certifying completion
10. **Reinforcement Program** — 90-day post-training practice plan with prompts, challenges, and peer review

## Workflow

### Step 1: Needs Assessment

Survey and interview representatives from each team to understand current storytelling practices, skill levels, and pain points. Identify the highest-impact skill gaps.

### Step 2: Capability Baseline

Establish a measurable baseline: can team members articulate the brand story? Can sales reps tell a customer story in a meeting? Can leaders use narrative in all-hands presentations? Score each team against the storytelling maturity model.

### Step 3: Curriculum Architecture

Design the program structure:
- **Core Module (4 hours)**: Required for all participants. Covers: why stories work, narrative structure, finding stories in everyday experience, audience-first thinking, the brand story.
- **Sales Track (4 hours)**: Story-driven discovery calls, customer scenario narratives, closing with story, objection handling through narrative.
- **Leadership Track (4 hours)**: Vision storytelling, change narratives, culture stories, storytelling in meetings and presentations.
- **Story Gathering Track (4 hours)**: Customer interview techniques, consent and ethics, story extraction, drafting customer stories.

### Step 4: Module Development

For each module, develop: learning objectives, facilitator guide with timing and talking points, participant workbook with exercises, practice scenarios using real brand examples, and assessment criteria.

### Step 5: Exercise Design

Create hands-on exercises that build genuine skill:
- **Story Mining**: Participants find stories from their own work experience
- **Story Crafting**: Participants structure a story using the narrative arc template
- **Story Telling**: Participants practice delivering stories to small groups with peer feedback
- **Story Gathering**: Participants practice interview techniques in role-play scenarios

### Step 6: Pilot Delivery

Run pilot workshops with representative groups from each track. Observe participant engagement, exercise effectiveness, and skill development. Collect structured feedback.

### Step 7: Refinement

Revise modules based on pilot feedback. Adjust timing, clarify instructions, strengthen weak exercises, and add or remove content based on what worked.

### Step 8: Train-the-Trainer

Prepare internal facilitators to deliver the program ongoing. Provide the facilitator guide, conduct observation sessions, and establish a feedback loop for continuous improvement.

## Success Metrics

### Learning Metrics

- Pre/post knowledge assessment score improvement (target: >30% improvement)
- Participant confidence in storytelling ability (self-assessment, pre vs. post)
- Exercise completion quality (assessed by facilitators)
- Certification pass rate (target: >80% of participants)

### Application Metrics (30-90 days post-training)

- Frequency of story use in sales conversations (self-reported and manager-observed)
- Quality of customer stories submitted by trained teams
- Leadership use of narrative in presentations and communications
- Brand story articulation accuracy (spot-check assessments)

### Business Impact Metrics (3-12 months post-training)

- Sales win rate change for trained reps vs. untrained
- Customer story production volume from trained teams
- Employee engagement scores related to company culture and mission
- Internal narrative consistency across departments

### Program Health Metrics

- Participant satisfaction score (target: >4.2/5)
- Internal facilitator confidence and delivery quality
- Program completion rate (target: >90% of enrolled participants)
- Demand for additional training sessions
