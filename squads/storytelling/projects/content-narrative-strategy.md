# Content Narrative Strategy

## Project Overview

Develop a comprehensive content marketing narrative strategy that transforms disconnected content production into a cohesive, story-driven content ecosystem. This project creates the narrative framework that turns individual blog posts, videos, podcasts, and social updates into chapters of a larger brand story.

Most content marketing programs produce content without a narrative spine. The result is a library of disconnected pieces that fail to build compounding brand equity. This project fixes that.

---

## Scope

### In Scope

- Content audit with narrative lens (current content assessed for story coherence)
- Audience narrative research (what stories the audience needs at each funnel stage)
- Master narrative framework (the overarching story that all content serves)
- Content pillar definition with narrative themes
- Editorial calendar with narrative arc planning (6 months)
- Content templates for each format with built-in narrative structure
- Distribution narrative strategy (how stories flow across channels)
- Measurement framework for narrative content performance

### Out of Scope

- Content production beyond templates and pilot pieces (ongoing production is separate)
- SEO technical audit (narrative-informed keyword strategy only)
- Marketing automation setup (strategy and content flow only)
- Social media management (narrative guidelines and calendar only)

## Workflow

### Step 1: Content Inventory and Audit

Catalog all existing content assets. Evaluate each for narrative quality, audience relevance, funnel alignment, and performance. Identify the strongest narrative pieces and the largest gaps.

### Step 2: Audience Narrative Research

Map the audience's information journey: what questions do they ask at each stage, what stories do they need to hear, what objections must be addressed through narrative, and where do they consume content.

### Step 3: Competitive Content Landscape

Analyze competitor content strategies through a narrative lens. Where are they telling strong stories? Where is there narrative white space the brand can own?

### Step 4: Master Narrative Definition

Define the overarching content narrative: the single story that all content serves. This is not a tagline. It is a narrative about the audience's transformation and the brand's role in enabling it.

### Step 5: Content Pillar Architecture

Define 4-6 content pillars, each representing a major theme within the master narrative. For each pillar: narrative rationale, key themes, example topics, target audience, and funnel alignment.

### Step 6: Editorial Calendar Design

Build a 6-month editorial calendar with narrative arcs. Each month or quarter has a narrative theme. Individual pieces advance the theme while standing alone as valuable content.

### Step 7: Template Development

Create narrative-structured templates for each content format. Each template includes story structure prompts, voice guidelines, and quality criteria.

### Step 8: Pilot Production

Produce 4-6 exemplar pieces that demonstrate the new narrative strategy in action. Use these as benchmarks and training materials for the content team.

## Success Metrics

### Content Performance Metrics

- Average engagement rate increase on narrative-structured content vs. prior content
- Content completion rates (read, view, listen through rates)
- Organic traffic growth attributed to narrative content
- Social sharing rate per content piece
- Email click-through rate on narrative content

### Narrative Coherence Metrics

- Audience message recall (can the audience articulate the brand's story?)
- Content pillar coverage balance (are all pillars being served?)
- Cross-content journey completion (do readers follow from one piece to the next?)
- Brand voice consistency score across content pieces

### Business Impact Metrics

- Lead generation from content (downloads, sign-ups, demo requests)
- Content-influenced pipeline value
- Customer acquisition cost trend (should decrease over time)
- Content-attributed revenue
