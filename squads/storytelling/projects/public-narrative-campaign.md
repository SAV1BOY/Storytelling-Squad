# Public Narrative Campaign

## Project Overview

Design and execute a public narrative campaign for a cause, movement, or social initiative. This project uses storytelling to shift public perception, build coalitions, and mobilize action around a shared cause. It applies the principles of public narrative, combining the story of self, the story of us, and the story of now.

Public narrative campaigns differ from brand campaigns in a critical way: the goal is not to sell a product but to change beliefs and behaviors on a societal level.

---

## Scope

### In Scope

- Public narrative strategy (issue framing, audience mapping, narrative arc)
- Story collection from affected communities and advocates
- Core campaign narrative and messaging framework
- Multi-channel content production (written, video scripts, social, email)
- Coalition storytelling toolkit (enabling partners to tell aligned stories)
- Media narrative strategy and earned media plan
- Campaign launch and sustained engagement plan
- Impact measurement framework

### Out of Scope

- Policy development or advocacy strategy (narrative support only)
- Event production and logistics (content for events, not event management)
- Paid media buying (creative and strategy only)
- Legal review of claims (coordinate with legal advisors)

## Deliverables

1. **Narrative Strategy Document** — Issue framing, audience segments, narrative arc, and theory of change
2. **Story Collection Report** — 15-25 stories gathered from affected community members and advocates
3. **Core Campaign Narrative** — The foundational story that anchors all campaign communications
4. **Message Framework** — Key messages, proof points, and counterarguments for each audience segment
5. **Content Production Package** — Hero video script, 6-8 written stories, social content suite, email series
6. **Coalition Storytelling Toolkit** — Messaging guide, story templates, and social media kit for partners
7. **Media Narrative Plan** — Story angles, press materials, op-ed drafts, and journalist outreach strategy
8. **Campaign Calendar** — 3-6 month phased calendar with content, moments, and milestones
9. **Impact Measurement Framework** — Narrative shift metrics, engagement tracking, and behavior change indicators

## Workflow

### Step 1: Issue Landscape Research

Understand the current state of public discourse on the issue. Map existing narratives: who is saying what, which frames dominate, what counter-narratives exist, and where there is narrative opportunity.

### Step 2: Community Story Gathering

Partner with affected communities to gather authentic stories. This is not extractive: it requires trust, consent, collaboration, and community ownership of their narratives. Follow the ethical storytelling guidelines rigorously.

### Step 3: Public Narrative Architecture

Build the campaign narrative using the public narrative framework:
- **Story of Self**: Why the narrator (organization or spokesperson) is personally called to this cause
- **Story of Us**: The shared experience and values that connect the community of supporters
- **Story of Now**: The urgent challenge that demands action right now

### Step 4: Audience Mapping and Message Development

Identify 3-5 audience segments: allies to mobilize, persuadables to convert, opposition to neutralize, and media to inform. Develop segment-specific messages grounded in the core narrative.

### Step 5: Coalition Narrative Alignment

Work with coalition partners to ensure narrative alignment. Each partner tells the story from their unique perspective while reinforcing the shared narrative. Provide toolkits that make this easy.

### Step 6: Content Production

Produce the full content suite: hero video script, individual stories, social content, email sequences, and media materials. Every piece should be usable independently while serving the larger campaign arc.

### Step 7: Campaign Launch

Execute a coordinated launch across owned, earned, and partner channels. Synchronize messaging for maximum impact. Lead with the strongest story, not the policy position.

### Step 8: Sustained Engagement

Maintain narrative momentum through regular story releases, community moments, media placements, and coalition activities. Adapt the narrative based on public response and evolving circumstances.

## Success Metrics

### Narrative Shift Metrics

- Public opinion change on the issue (pre/post survey)
- Media narrative frame analysis (are journalists using our framing?)
- Social conversation volume and sentiment on the issue
- Counter-narrative prevalence (is opposition narrative declining?)

### Engagement Metrics

- Campaign content reach and engagement across channels
- Story completion rates (are people watching/reading to the end?)
- Social sharing rate and viral coefficient
- Coalition partner amplification reach
- Email subscription and action completion rates

### Behavior Change Metrics

- Petition signatures, pledges, or commitment actions
- Event attendance or participation
- Policy engagement (letters to representatives, public comments)
- Donation or volunteer activity
- Sustained engagement beyond initial campaign burst
