# Founder Story Series

## Project Overview

Create a multi-part founder story series that humanizes the brand, builds trust, and differentiates through authentic origin narrative. This project transforms the founder's journey into a strategic storytelling asset delivered across written, video, and audio formats in a serialized structure.

The series goes beyond a single origin story. It unpacks the key chapters of the founding journey, each revealing a different facet of the brand's values, mission, and character.

---

## Scope

### In Scope

- Founder interview series (4-6 deep-dive sessions)
- Narrative architecture for the series (chapter structure, themes, arc)
- 6-8 episode series across written and video/audio formats
- Supporting social content for each episode
- Series launch strategy and promotional plan
- Companion email sequence for subscribers
- Series landing page copy and structure

### Out of Scope

- Video production and editing (provide scripts; production by Video Squad)
- Podcast production and hosting (provide scripts and direction)
- Paid media spend (strategy and recommendations only)
- Website development (copy and wireframe only)

## Workflow

### Step 1: Pre-Interview Research

Research the founder's public history, previous interviews, social media presence, and company milestones. Identify known story elements and gaps to explore.

### Step 2: Founder Interviews

Conduct 4-6 structured interviews (90 minutes each) covering: early life influences, the founding moment, key struggles, pivotal decisions, failures and lessons, vision for the future.

### Step 3: Theme Extraction

Analyze transcripts to identify recurring themes, emotional peaks, surprising revelations, and values demonstrations. Map these to potential episode structures.

### Step 4: Series Architecture

Design the overall series arc: opening hook, rising action across episodes, climax, and forward-looking resolution. Assign themes and key story beats to each episode.

### Step 5: Episode Production

Write each episode in long-form and script formats. Produce accompanying social content and email copy. Review each episode with the founder for accuracy and comfort.

### Step 6: Founder Review Cycle

Each episode goes through a structured review: factual accuracy check, emotional comfort check, brand alignment check, and legal sensitivity review.

### Step 7: Launch Execution

Publish the first 2 episodes simultaneously to establish the series. Release subsequent episodes weekly. Activate promotional plan across owned, earned, and paid channels.

### Step 8: Performance Monitoring

Track per-episode performance. Adjust promotion and distribution based on engagement patterns. Gather audience feedback for potential follow-up content.

## Success Metrics

### Engagement Metrics

- Average read/view completion rate per episode (target: >60%)
- Series subscription rate (email sign-ups for new episodes)
- Social sharing rate per episode
- Episode-over-episode retention (% of audience that follows the full series)
- Comment and response quality (substantive engagement vs. superficial)

### Brand Impact Metrics

- Brand trust score change (pre/post series survey)
- Brand recall improvement for origin story elements
- Media pickup and earned coverage of the series
- Founder's personal brand visibility increase

### Business Metrics

- Website traffic driven by series content
- Lead generation attributed to series (downloads, sign-ups)
- Sales team usage of founder story in prospect conversations
- Investor/partner engagement with series content
