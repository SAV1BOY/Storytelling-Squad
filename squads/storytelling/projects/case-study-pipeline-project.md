# Case Study Pipeline Project

## Purpose
Establish a systematic pipeline for producing case studies on an ongoing basis, ensuring the story bank always has fresh, relevant customer stories.

---

## Project Overview
- **Duration:** Ongoing (initial setup: 2 weeks; production: continuous)
- **Agents:** Story Bank Manager (lead), Story Architect, Proof Curator, Clarity Editor
- **Output:** 2-4 case studies per month at steady state

---

## Phase 1: Pipeline Setup (Week 1-2)

### Define Criteria
- Which customers qualify for case studies? (satisfaction score, results achieved, willingness)
- Which industries/verticals are priority? (cross-reference with gap detector)
- What channels will case studies serve? (determines format and depth)

### Build the Interview Kit
- Standard interview questionnaire (10 questions)
- Consent and approval form template
- Recording setup guide (Zoom, in-person, phone)
- Story brief template (filled out before the interview)

### Create the Production Workflow
- Step 1: Identify candidate (sales/CS team nomination or gap detector trigger)
- Step 2: Schedule and conduct interview (30-45 minutes)
- Step 3: Draft the case study (Story Architect + Proof Curator)
- Step 4: Edit and score (Clarity Editor + story scoring rubric)
- Step 5: Client approval
- Step 6: Format for target channels
- Step 7: Tag and add to story bank
- Step 8: Distribute through active channels

---

## Phase 2: Candidate Sourcing (Ongoing)

### Sources
- Customer success team nominations (monthly request)
- NPS survey high scorers (automated trigger)
- Renewal or expansion events (proactive outreach)
- Conference attendees who mention results publicly
- Social media mentions of positive experiences

### Candidate Evaluation
| Criterion | Weight | Threshold |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Measurable results | 30% | Must have before/after metrics |
| Willingness to be named | 25% | Must agree to public attribution |
| Industry relevance | 20% | Priority industries scored higher |
| Story quality potential | 15% | Interesting journey, not just results |
| Relationship health | 10% | Must be in good standing |

---

## Phase 3: Production (Ongoing)

### Monthly Targets
- Interview 4-6 candidates
- Produce 2-4 finished case studies
- Format each for 3+ channels
- Add all to story bank with tags

### Quality Standards
- Story scoring rubric: 75+ minimum
- Clarity score: 80+ minimum
- At least 2 proof blocks per case study
- At least 1 direct client quote
- Client approval before publication

---

## Phase 4: Optimization (Quarterly)

- Review pipeline metrics: interviews conducted, studies produced, approval rate
- Identify bottlenecks: where does production slow down?
- Update interview questions based on what produces best stories
- Run gap detector to reprioritize candidate sourcing
