# Video Testimonial Series

## Project Overview

Produce a professional video testimonial series that captures authentic customer stories on camera. This project goes beyond standard "talking head" testimonials to create narrative-driven video content that combines customer interviews with visual storytelling, resulting in a library of video assets usable across sales, marketing, and social channels.

Great video testimonials feel like short documentaries, not commercials. The customer is the star, the story is real, and the production quality builds credibility without sacrificing authenticity.

---

## Scope

### In Scope

- Video testimonial strategy (story types, customer selection, narrative approach)
- Customer recruitment, preparation, and consent management
- Interview script development for 8-12 customer stories
- Production direction (creative briefs, shot lists, visual storytelling notes)
- Post-production narrative supervision (edit notes, pacing, story arc guidance)
- Multi-format adaptation (full-length, social cuts, audio extracts, quote graphics)
- Distribution strategy across sales, website, social, and email
- Series landing page narrative copy

### Out of Scope

- Video filming and on-site production (provide direction; execution by Video Squad or vendor)
- Video editing and post-production (provide narrative edit notes)
- Paid video advertising (strategy and creative direction only)
- Video hosting and platform setup

9. **Series Landing Page Copy** — Narrative copy for the testimonial series hub page

## Workflow

### Step 1: Define Testimonial Strategy

Determine what types of stories the series needs: transformation stories, ROI stories, implementation stories, relationship stories, or innovation stories. Map each type to a business objective and funnel stage.

### Step 2: Customer Selection

Work with sales, customer success, and account management to identify candidates. Score customers on: story potential, diversity of representation, willingness to participate, relationship strength, and strategic value.

### Step 3: Customer Recruitment and Preparation

Approach selected customers with a clear value proposition for participation. Explain the process, time commitment, and how the content will be used. Secure formal consent. Provide preparation guidelines to help them feel comfortable.

### Step 4: Interview Script Development

Create narrative interview guides for each customer. Structure questions to elicit story, not testimonial: "Tell me about the moment you realized something had to change" rather than "Why do you like our product?" Include follow-up prompts and emotional hooks.

### Step 5: Creative Brief Development

For each shoot, produce a creative brief covering: the narrative arc, visual style, b-roll needs, environment notes, and mood references. The brief ensures the production team captures the footage needed to tell the story.

### Step 6: Production Direction

During filming, the Storytelling Squad provides narrative direction: guiding the interview conversation, identifying powerful moments, requesting additional takes or follow-up questions, and ensuring the emotional truth of the story is captured.

### Step 7: Post-Production Supervision

Provide detailed edit notes for each video: recommended structure, pacing guidance, music mood, title card text, and which moments to prioritize. Review rough cuts for narrative coherence and emotional impact.

### Step 8: Multi-Format Adaptation

Define cut specifications for each format: full-length (2-4 minutes for website and sales), social cuts (30-60 seconds for social platforms), and micro-clips (15 seconds for ads and stories). Each cut must tell a complete micro-story.

## Success Metrics

### Production Metrics

- Number of completed testimonials (target: 8-12)
- Average production timeline per testimonial (target: consistent with plan)
- Customer satisfaction with the process (post-shoot survey)
- Multi-format utilization (number of derivative assets per testimonial)

### Engagement Metrics

- Video completion rate by format (full-length target: >50%, social target: >65%)
- Social sharing and engagement rate
- Website page engagement when testimonials are present
- Email click-through rate on testimonial content

### Business Impact Metrics

- Sales team usage rate (% of reps actively using testimonials in deals)
- Conversion rate lift on pages with video testimonials
- Deal influence (testimonials cited as factor in closed deals)
- Pipeline value influenced by testimonial content
- Customer referral rate increase among testimonial participants
