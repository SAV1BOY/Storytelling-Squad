# Story Types Selection Guide

## Purpose
Choosing the wrong story type wastes time and produces narratives that miss the mark. This guide helps you select the right story type for any business situation, based on Kindra Hall's framework extended with operational decision criteria.

---

## The Decision Framework

### Question 1: What is the business objective?

| Objective | Primary Story Type | Why |
|-----------|-------------------|-----|
| Close a sale | Value Story | Prospects need to see outcomes |
| Raise funding | Founder Story | Investors bet on people and vision |
| Build brand awareness | Purpose Story | Audiences connect with mission |
| Reduce churn | Customer Story | Existing clients see peer success |
| Recruit talent | Founder + Purpose | Candidates want meaning and culture |
| Launch a product | Vision Story | Market needs to see the future |
| Recover from crisis | Purpose + Turning-Point | Stakeholders need transparency and direction |

### Question 2: Who is the audience?

| Audience | Preferred Story Type | Notes |
|----------|---------------------|-------|
| C-suite executives | Value Story (data-rich) | They want outcomes and ROI |
| Practitioners/operators | Customer Story (detailed) | They want to see implementation reality |
| Investors | Founder Story + Vision | They want conviction and market insight |
| General public | Purpose Story | They want to feel something and share it |
| Existing customers | Behind-the-Scenes | They want insider access and community |
| Prospective employees | Founder + Purpose | They want meaning and culture signals |

### Question 3: What channel will it live on?

| Channel | Best Story Types | Length Constraint |
|---------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Website homepage | Customer Story, Value Story | Short (150-300 words) |
| Blog | Lesson-Learned, Customer Story | Medium (800-1500 words) |
| Email sequence | Value Story, Turning-Point | Short per email (100-200 words) |
| Pitch deck | Founder Story, Value Story | Integrated across slides |
| Keynote | Turning-Point, Founder Story | Long (10-30 minutes of narrative) |
| Social media | Lesson-Learned, Behind-the-Scenes | Micro (50-150 words) |
| Video | Customer Story, Purpose Story | 60-180 seconds |
| Case study | Value Story, Customer Story | Medium-long (800-2000 words) |

## Common Selection Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Using a founder story for a sales meeting
The prospect does not care about the founder's journey — they care about their own outcomes. Use a value story or customer story.

### Mistake 2: Using a value story for a keynote
Keynote audiences want to be inspired, not sold to. Use a turning-point or founder story with universal themes.

### Mistake 3: Using a purpose story when you need proof
Purpose stories build affinity but do not close deals. If the prospect is in the decision stage, they need a value story with proof blocks.

### Mistake 4: Using the same story type everywhere
Each channel and audience requires a different story type. A story bank needs diversity, not depth in one category.

---

## Story Type Combinations

For complex projects, combine two story types:
- **Pitch deck:** Founder Story (opening) + Value Story (middle) + Vision Story (close)
- **Website:** Customer Story (social proof) + Purpose Story (about page) + Value Story (product page)
- **Email sequence:** Lesson-Learned (email 1) + Value Story (emails 2-3) + Customer Story (email 4) + Turning-Point (email 5)
- **Brand campaign:** Purpose Story (primary) + Customer Stories (supporting)

## Quick Selection Flowchart

```
START: What do you need?
  |
  +--> Sell something? --> Value Story or Customer Story
  |
  +--> Build trust? --> Founder Story or Customer Story
  |
  +--> Inspire action? --> Purpose Story or Vision Story
  |
  +--> Teach a lesson? --> Lesson-Learned Story
  |
  +--> Humanize the brand? --> Behind-the-Scenes Story
  |
  +--> Create urgency? --> Turning-Point Story
```
