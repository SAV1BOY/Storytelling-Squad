# Workflow Execution Guide

## Purpose

Explain how to run end-to-end storytelling workflows from initial request through publication. This guide connects the scripts, agents, templates, frameworks, and quality processes into complete workflows for the most common project types.

## Workflow Principles

1. **Every workflow starts with a brief.** No production begins without a documented request.
2. **Every workflow passes through a quality gate.** No asset ships without a review.
3. **Every workflow ends with a handoff or publication.** Work is not done until it reaches the audience.
4. **Workflows are sequential but allow iteration.** Steps build on each other, but you can return to a prior step when needed.
5. **Track progress in the story bank.** Every workflow produces a story bank entry that advances through status stages.

## Workflow 1: Customer Story (End-to-End)

**Timeline:** 2-3 weeks
**Agents:** Story Architect, Character Designer, Proof Curator, Clarity Editor
**Framework:** Before-After-Bridge or STAR

### Steps

| Step | Action | Script/Reference | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receive and triage the story brief | Squad lead reviews in `#storytelling-requests` | Day 1 |
| 2 | Research the customer | Background research: CRM, usage data, support tickets | Day 1-2 |
| 3 | Conduct the customer interview | `scripts/conduct-customer-interview.md` | Day 3-5 |
| 4 | Transcribe and highlight key quotes | Post-interview processing | Day 5-6 |
| 5 | Select the framework | `docs/framework-selection-guide.md` | Day 6 |
| 6 | Draft the story using the template | `templates/customer-story.md` with agents | Day 7-10 |
| 7 | Self-review using the checklist | `docs/checklist-usage-guide.md` | Day 10 |
| 8 | Submit for quality gate | `scripts/story-quality-gate-process.md` | Day 10-13 |
| 9 | Revise based on feedback | Address reviewer notes | Day 13-14 |
| 10 | Customer review and approval | Share draft with the customer for sign-off | Day 14-17 |
| 11 | Final quality gate (if revised) | Re-score flagged dimensions | Day 17 |
| 12 | Cross-squad handoff | `scripts/cross-squad-handoff.md` | Day 17-18 |

## Managing Workflow Exceptions

### When Deadlines Shift
- If a deadline moves up, negotiate scope reduction (fewer proof points, shorter format).
- If a deadline moves out, use the extra time for additional interviews or data.
- Always update the story bank entry and notify cross-squad partners.

### When the Framework Does Not Fit Mid-Draft
- Pause drafting. Revisit `docs/framework-selection-guide.md`.
- Switch frameworks before investing more time in the wrong structure.
- Document the switch in the story bank entry for learning.

### When Quality Gate Fails After Multiple Revisions
- After 3 cycles, escalate to the squad lead.
- Options: restructure with a different framework, reassign to a different writer, or archive.
- Document the decision and rationale.
