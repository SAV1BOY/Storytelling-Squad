## Core Concepts from the Story Grid

### 1. Genre
Every story (including business stories) operates within a genre that carries
audience expectations. Understanding genre means understanding what the audience
needs to feel satisfied.

| Business Context | Story Grid Genre | Core Emotion |
|-----------------|-----------------|-------------|
| Case study | Performance (success/failure) | Respect, admiration |
| Founder origin story | Status/admiration | Inspiration |
| Brand manifesto | Worldview/revelation | Awakening |
| Fundraising appeal | Morality/altruism | Compassion |
| Sales pitch | Performance (challenge) | Excitement, desire |

### 2. The Five Commandments of Storytelling
Every effective scene or story contains:
1. **Inciting Incident** — An event that upsets the balance.
2. **Progressive Complication** — Things get harder.
3. **Crisis** — A choice must be made (dilemma).
4. **Climax** — The choice is made (action).
5. **Resolution** — The consequence of the choice.

**Applied to a case study:**
1. Inciting incident: the client's problem reaches a breaking point.
2. Progressive complication: previous solutions fail; the problem worsens.
3. Crisis: the client must choose — try something new or accept the status quo.
4. Climax: they implement the solution.
5. Resolution: the results, quantified and felt.

### 3. Value Shifts
Every story moves a character along a value spectrum. The beginning state must be
different from the ending state.

| Value | Negative Pole | Positive Pole |
|-------|--------------|--------------|
| Success | Failure | Achievement |
| Trust | Suspicion | Confidence |
| Knowledge | Confusion | Clarity |
| Connection | Isolation | Belonging |
| Safety | Danger | Security |

A flat value (same at start and end) means no story happened.

## The Foolscap Method (One-Page Story Plan)
Shawn Coyne's tool for planning any story on a single sheet:

```
GLOBAL GENRE: [e.g., Performance/Business]
GLOBAL INCITING INCIDENT: [The event that starts everything]
PROGRESSIVE COMPLICATIONS: [Key escalation points]
CRISIS: [The dilemma — what choice must be made?]
CLIMAX: [What action is taken?]
RESOLUTION: [What is the result?]
BEGINNING HOOK: [First 25% — setup]
MIDDLE BUILD: [50% — escalation]
ENDING PAYOFF: [Final 25% — resolution]
```

**Applied to a brand story:**
```
GLOBAL GENRE: Worldview/Revelation
INCITING INCIDENT: Founder discovers the industry is broken
PROGRESSIVE COMPLICATIONS: Failed solutions, wasted money, industry resistance
CRISIS: Accept the status quo or build something new?
CLIMAX: The founding moment — building the product
RESOLUTION: Customers respond; the market shifts
```
