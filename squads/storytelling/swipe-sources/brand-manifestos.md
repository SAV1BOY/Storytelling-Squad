# Brand Manifestos — Swipe Source Guide
> Notable Brand Manifestos and Where to Find Them

## Source Overview

A brand manifesto is a declaration of beliefs, values, and purpose — the narrative DNA of a
brand expressed in its most concentrated form. The best manifestos are pure storytelling:
they establish stakes, declare values, name an enemy (the status quo), and invite the audience
into a movement. Studying manifestos reveals how brands compress their entire narrative
identity into a single document.

## What to Study

- **Voice and Conviction** — How manifestos establish a distinctive, unmistakable voice
- **Enemy Naming** — How they define what they stand against (not just what they stand for)
- **Movement Invitation** — How they make the reader feel like part of something bigger
- **Values as Story** — How abstract values are expressed through narrative language
- **Rhythm and Cadence** — How manifestos use poetic techniques (repetition, parallelism,
  short sentences) for emotional impact

## Key Manifestos to Study

1. **Apple — "Think Different"** — The manifesto behind the campaign. Study how it positions
   the audience as the creative rebel. Uses "the crazy ones" as hero archetype.

2. **Nike — Internal Manifesto** — Nike's internal brand principles. Study how "If you have
   a body, you are an athlete" reframes the entire brand promise.

3. **Patagonia — Environmental Mission** — "We're in business to save our home planet."
   Study how purpose-driven narrative creates brand loyalty beyond product.

4. **Holstee Manifesto** — "This is your life. Do what you love." Study how a simple poster
   became a viral brand-building tool through pure narrative conviction.

5. **Lululemon Manifesto** — Collection of life principles printed on shopping bags. Study
   how lifestyle values replace traditional product messaging.

6. **TED — Ideas Worth Spreading** — TED's mission as manifesto. Study how three words
   contain an entire narrative worldview.

7. **Charity: Water — Origin Story as Manifesto** — Scott Harrison's personal transformation
   story serves as the organization's manifesto. Study narrative-as-mission.

8. **Dove — Real Beauty Pledge** — Manifesto that challenged beauty industry norms. Study
   how naming the enemy (unrealistic beauty standards) creates movement energy.

## Narrative Techniques to Watch For

- Repetition as conviction tool ("We believe..." repeated structures)
- Short, declarative sentences that read like truths, not arguments
- Contrast between the old world (what we reject) and new world (what we create)
- Second person "you" that makes the reader the protagonist
- Present tense that creates immediacy and permanence
- Specific enough to be meaningful, universal enough to include everyone
- Emotional escalation from statement to movement to call to action

## How to Use in Marketing

- **Brand Foundation**: Study manifestos before crafting brand narrative strategy
- **Voice Development**: Use manifestos as voice calibration references
- **Workshop Material**: Manifestos are excellent examples for brand storytelling workshops
- **Public Narrative**: Study how manifestos use Marshall Ganz's Story of Self/Us/Now
- **Content Inspiration**: Manifesto principles can drive content calendar themes
