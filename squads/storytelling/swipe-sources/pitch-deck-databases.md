# Pitch Deck Databases — Swipe Source Guide
> Curated Sources for Pitch Deck Narrative Study

## Source Overview

Pitch decks are concentrated storytelling artifacts — they must convey a complete narrative
arc (problem → solution → transformation → proof → call to action) in 10-15 slides. Studying
great pitch decks reveals how master storytellers compress narrative into visual-spatial format.
These databases collect decks from successful startups, providing a rich swipe file for
narrative structure.

## What to Study

- **Narrative Arc in Slides** — How the best decks follow a clear story structure (not just
  information dumps) with problem → solution → traction → vision flow
- **Visual Storytelling** — How images, charts, and design amplify or replace text narrative
- **Opening Hooks** — How the first 1-3 slides create urgency and curiosity
- **Proof Placement** — Where and how traction/evidence slides appear in the narrative flow
- **The Ask** — How the closing slides frame the investment as a story climax

## Key Sources

1. **Pitch Deck Hunt** — Curated collection of startup pitch decks with analysis. Study the
   variety of narrative structures across industries. Best for seeing current trends.

2. **SlideBean's Pitch Deck Collection** — Analyzed decks from funded startups with
   commentary on what worked. Study the annotations for insight into investor psychology.

3. **Alexander Jarvis Collection** — One of the largest collections of startup pitch decks
   (400+). Study patterns across successful raises at different stages.

4. **Sequoia Capital Pitch Template** — The gold standard pitch format. Study how Sequoia's
   recommended structure maps to Oren Klaff's pitch methodology.

5. **First Round Capital Blog** — Detailed breakdowns of successful pitch strategies with
   founder commentary. Study the thinking behind narrative choices.

6. **Y Combinator Application Examples** — YC application narratives show how to tell a
   startup story in extremely compressed format. Study radical reduction.

7. **TechCrunch Pitch Deck Teardowns** — Published analyses of real pitch decks. Study the
   critique methodology for understanding what investors look for narratively.

## Narrative Techniques to Watch For

- How problem slides create emotional resonance (not just market data)
- Use of customer stories/quotes embedded in deck narrative
- "Before/After" slide pairs showing transformation
- How "Why Now" slides create urgency through narrative framing
- Demo slides as "proof of concept" story beats
- Team slides as "mentor/guide" positioning (why this team can deliver)
- Competition slides framed as "landscape of the quest" not just feature matrices
- Financial projections as "vision of the promised land"

## How to Use in Marketing

- **Deck Building**: Study top decks before building any presentation narrative
- **Pitch Training**: Use analyzed decks as training materials for pitch workshops
- **Framework Application**: Map Klaff's frame control to real deck examples
- **Visual Narrative**: Study how design choices support or undermine story
- **Compression Skills**: Practice telling stories in 10-slide format
