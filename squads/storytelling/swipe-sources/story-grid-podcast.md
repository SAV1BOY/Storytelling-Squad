# Story Grid Podcast — Swipe Source Guide
> Hosted by Shawn Coyne and Tim Grahl

## Source Overview

The Story Grid Podcast is the audio companion to Shawn Coyne's Story Grid methodology — the
most rigorous analytical framework for understanding why stories work or fail. Each episode
applies Story Grid's editorial principles to specific stories, genres, and writing challenges.
For marketing storytellers, this podcast provides the analytical toolkit to diagnose narrative
problems and engineer narrative solutions.

## What to Study

- **Five Commandments of Storytelling** — How every scene must contain an inciting incident,
  progressive complication, crisis, climax, and resolution
- **Genre Conventions and Obligatory Scenes** — Every genre has specific elements the audience
  expects, and missing them causes narrative failure
- **Value Shifts** — How every scene must shift a value from positive to negative or vice
  versa. Scenes without value shifts are narrative dead weight.
- **Controlling Idea** — How the single governing theme drives every narrative choice
- **Global Story Analysis** — How to analyze an entire narrative's arc using the Story Grid
  spreadsheet methodology

## Key Episodes to Study

1. **"Five Commandments Deep Dive"** — Foundation episodes explaining each commandment.
   Study how these five elements appear in scenes of any length, from 30-second ads to
   feature films.

2. **"Genre Analysis" episodes** — Episodes analyzing specific genres (thriller, love story,
   action, status). Study how genre conventions map to marketing content types.

3. **"Beginning Hook, Middle Build, Ending Payoff"** — How the three-part macro structure
   works. Study how this maps to marketing funnels and content sequences.

4. **"The Editor's Six Core Questions"** — The diagnostic framework for any narrative.
   Study these questions as a quality checklist for marketing content.

5. **"Obligatory Scenes" series** — What every genre must include. Study how "obligatory
   scenes" in marketing stories (proof, transformation, CTA) parallel fiction requirements.

6. **"Value Shifts and Progressive Complications"** — How to create narrative momentum.
   Study how value shifts apply to case studies and customer stories.

7. **"Controlling Idea" episodes** — How a single idea governs the entire narrative. Study
   how this applies to brand messaging and content strategy.

## Narrative Techniques to Watch For

- Analytical rigor applied to creative work — bringing structure to intuition
- How to diagnose "why this story isn't working" using specific criteria
- The relationship between genre expectations and audience satisfaction
- How value shifts create emotional engagement at the scene level
- The difference between story and discourse (what happens vs. how it's told)
- How controlling idea prevents narrative drift in long-form content

## How to Use in Marketing

- **Quality Assurance**: Use Five Commandments as a checklist for every piece of narrative
  content (does it have an inciting incident? crisis? climax?)
- **Genre Mapping**: Map marketing content types to Story Grid genres for structural guidance
- **Diagnostic Tool**: When a story "isn't working," use Story Grid analysis to find the
  specific structural failure
- **Training Material**: Episodes provide excellent examples for team storytelling education
- **Editorial Process**: Apply Story Grid's editorial methodology to content review workflows
