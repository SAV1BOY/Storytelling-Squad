# Building a StoryBrand Podcast: Key Episodes Index

Curated index of episodes from Donald Miller's *Building a StoryBrand* podcast
(now *Business Made Simple*) most relevant to the storytelling squad's work.

---

## About the Podcast
**Host:** Donald Miller (author of *Building a StoryBrand*)
**Format:** Short solo episodes (10–20 min) + interviews with business leaders
**Focus:** Applying the SB7 framework to marketing, sales, and leadership
**Key framework:** StoryBrand 7-Part Framework (SB7)

---

## The SB7 Framework (Quick Reference)

1. **A Character** — The customer is the hero. Not you.
2. **Has a Problem** — External problem, internal problem, and philosophical problem.
3. **And Meets a Guide** — The brand is the guide (empathy + authority).
4. **Who Gives Them a Plan** — Three clear steps.
5. **And Calls Them to Action** — Direct CTA and transitional CTA.
6. **That Helps Them Avoid Failure** — What happens if they don't act?
7. **And Ends in Success** — What does the happy ending look like?

## The Three Problem Levels

The most actionable concept from the StoryBrand framework for storytelling:

| Level | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **External** | The tangible problem | "I can't find a project management tool that works." |
| **Internal** | How the problem makes the character feel | "I feel overwhelmed and out of control." |
| **Philosophical** | Why this shouldn't be happening | "Managing projects shouldn't require an engineering degree." |

**The best stories address all three levels.**
- External: names the problem.
- Internal: creates empathy.
- Philosophical: creates moral urgency.

---

## StoryBrand One-Liner Template
```
[Problem]: Most [customers] struggle with [external problem].
[Solution]: [Brand] provides [solution/product].
[Result]: So you can [result that addresses internal and philosophical problems].
```

**Example:**
> "Most marketing teams waste months creating content that nobody reads.
> We help you build a storytelling system that turns content into pipeline.
> So you can stop guessing and start growing — with confidence."
