## The ABT Framework (Core Concept)
Park Howell frequently uses and teaches the ABT structure:
- **And** — Establish agreement (the setup, the world as it is).
- **But** — Introduce conflict (the problem, the contradiction).
- **Therefore** — Resolve with action (the solution, the next step).

**Example:**
> "We help companies tell their stories **AND** our clients love the results.
> **BUT** most companies struggle because they don't have a storytelling framework.
> **THEREFORE** we created a system that any team can learn in one workshop."

This structure replaces the deadly AAA (And, And, And) pattern:
> "We do this AND this AND this AND this." (No tension. No reason to care.)

## The Story Cycle System (Howell's Framework)

1. **Backstory** — What's the context? Where did we come from?
2. **Hero** — Who is the customer? What do they want?
3. **Stakes** — What happens if they don't get it?
4. **Disruption** — What changed? What's the inciting incident?
5. **Antagonist** — What stands in the way?
6. **Mentor** — How does the brand guide the hero?
7. **Journey** — What's the path forward?
8. **Victory** — What does success look like?
9. **Moral** — What's the lesson?
10. **Ritual** — What repeatable action keeps the audience engaged?
