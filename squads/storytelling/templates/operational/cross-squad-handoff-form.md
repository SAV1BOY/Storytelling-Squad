# Cross-Squad Handoff Form

## Name
Cross-Squad Narrative Handoff Form

## Purpose
Ensure that when the storytelling squad delivers narrative assets to another squad, the receiving team has everything they need to use the asset correctly. A story without context gets misused — the wrong tone, the wrong channel adaptation, the wrong audience targeting. This form packages the story asset with the instructions, context, and guardrails that protect narrative quality across squad boundaries.

## Instructions
1. Complete this form every time a narrative asset is handed off to another squad.
2. Attach or link the actual asset (story document, script, narrative brief, etc.).
3. Walk the receiving squad through the form in a brief handoff call (15-30 minutes) or async message. Do not simply send the form — context gets lost in text.
4. The receiving squad confirms receipt and understanding by signing off in the acknowledgment section.
5. File the completed handoff form in the project record for future reference.

## Example

**Handoff ID:** HANDOFF-2026-009
**From:** Maya Torres, Storytelling Squad
**To:** James Park, Copy Squad
**Asset:** Meridian Health customer transformation story (video script + email adaptation brief)
**Status:** Final — video script is locked. Email adaptation brief is a framework for the copy squad to build from.
**Key guardrail:** The customer's quote about "finally being able to see patients instead of paperwork" is the emotional anchor. It must appear in every adaptation.
**Adaptation note:** The video script is 90 seconds. For the email version, the copy squad should extract the core transformation arc (before state, turning point, after state) and write it in first-person email voice. Target 300-400 words.
