# Story Version Log
> Use this template to track the revision history of any narrative asset. Documents what changed, who changed it, why, and who approved it. Essential for compliance, collaboration, and learning from the editing process.

## Instructions
Create one version log per story asset. Update it every time a meaningful revision is made. "Meaningful" means any change that affects the message, structure, proof, or approval status — not typo fixes. Keep the log in the same folder as the story asset. Link to the actual draft files when possible.
