# Narrative Review Request

## Name
Narrative Review Request Form

## Purpose
Provide a standardized way for team members and cross-squad partners to request a narrative review of existing or in-progress content. A narrative review assesses whether a piece of content tells a coherent, effective story — not whether the grammar is correct or the design is polished. This form ensures the reviewer has enough context to provide actionable feedback.

## Instructions
1. Complete this form when you have a draft, a piece of live content, or a planned narrative that you want the storytelling squad to evaluate.
2. Attach or link the content being reviewed. The reviewer must be able to see the actual material.
3. Specify what kind of feedback you need — structural, emotional, voice/tone, audience fit, or general narrative quality.
4. Submit to the storytelling squad's review channel. Reviews are typically completed within 3-5 business days.
5. Urgent reviews (needed within 24-48 hours) require prior agreement with the squad lead.

---

### 3. What Kind of Review Do You Need?

Check all that apply:

- [ ] **Structural review** — Does the story have a clear arc? Is the structure sound (hook, tension, resolution)?
- [ ] **Emotional review** — Does the content create the intended emotional response? Where does it fall flat?
- [ ] **Voice/tone review** — Does the content match the brand voice guide? Where does it drift?
- [ ] **Audience fit review** — Will this resonate with the target audience? Is it calibrated for their level?
- [ ] **Clarity review** — Is the message clear? Can the audience state what this is about after reading/watching?
- [ ] **General narrative quality** — Overall assessment: is this a good story, well told?

---

### 4. Specific Concerns

**Is there a reference piece that represents the quality or style you are aiming for?** [LINK OR DESCRIBE]
