# Cross-Squad Handoff Template
> Use this template when passing a narrative asset or project from the Storytelling Squad to another squad (or vice versa). Ensures nothing gets lost in translation — context, requirements, constraints, and expectations travel with the asset.

## Instructions
Complete this form before transferring any work. Both the sending and receiving squad leads should review and confirm understanding. Attach the actual asset and all supporting documents. The handoff is not complete until the receiving squad confirms receipt and comprehension.

## Example

**From:** Storytelling Squad (Jamie Torres)
**To:** Design Squad (Li Wei)

**Asset:** BrightPath Case Study v2.1 — approved narrative copy (1,050 words), 3 approved customer quotes, 1 data table (before/after metrics), 1 approved customer headshot.

**Context:** This is a customer case study for the website and sales enablement. It follows the Five Commandments structure with a clear turning point at the "dashboard reveals the truth" scene. The core message: predictive analytics cuts response time and reduces churn.

**Must Preserve:** The narrative arc (don't break the story into disconnected blocks), all customer quotes verbatim, the before/after metrics table.

**Must Avoid:** Competitor mentions, stock photography (use only the approved customer headshot), any metrics not in the approved data table.

**Flexibility:** Layout, typography, color, number of pages/sections, supporting illustrations, and iconography are all open to the Design Squad's judgment.

**Timeline:** Design first draft by March 15. Joint review March 17. Publish March 20.
