# Story Brief Intake Form

## Name
Story Brief Intake Form

## Purpose
Standardize how new story requests enter the storytelling squad's pipeline. Every story request — whether from an internal team, a cross-squad partner, or a new project — must pass through this intake form to ensure the storytelling squad has enough context to produce the right story, for the right audience, in the right format, on time.

## Instructions
1. The requester completes this form as thoroughly as possible. Incomplete forms will be returned with questions.
2. Submit the completed form to the storytelling squad's intake channel or project management system.
3. The squad lead reviews the intake within 48 hours and assigns it to the appropriate task and agent.
4. If the request is unclear or underdefined, a 15-minute clarification call is scheduled before work begins.
5. Do not begin story development without a completed and reviewed intake form.

## Template Body

---

### Story Brief Intake

**Request ID:** [AUTO-GENERATED OR MANUAL — e.g., STORY-2026-042]
**Date submitted:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
**Requester name:** [FULL NAME]
**Requester squad/team:** [SQUAD OR DEPARTMENT]
**Priority:** [LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / URGENT]
**Requested delivery date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]

---

### 1. What Do You Need?

**Story type requested:** [SELECT: Customer story / Founder story / Brand origin / Case study / Testimonial / Campaign narrative / Pitch narrative / Thought leadership / Other: ___]

**Brief description:** [2-3 SENTENCES — what is this story about and why do you need it?]

**Desired outcome:** [WHAT SHOULD THE AUDIENCE THINK, FEEL, OR DO AFTER ENCOUNTERING THIS STORY?]

---

### 2. Who Is It For?

**Target audience:** [DESCRIBE THE PRIMARY AUDIENCE — role, industry, pain points, sophistication level]

**Audience's current state:** [WHAT DOES THE AUDIENCE CURRENTLY BELIEVE OR FEEL ABOUT THIS TOPIC?]

**Audience's desired state:** [WHAT SHOULD THEY BELIEVE OR FEEL AFTER THE STORY?]

---

### 3. Where Will It Live?

**Primary channel:** [SELECT: Email / Blog / Social media / Video / Deck / Pitch / Webinar / Ad / Podcast / Print / Website / Other: ___]

**Secondary channels:** [LIST ANY ADDITIONAL CHANNELS WHERE THIS STORY WILL BE ADAPTED]

**Format requirements:** [WORD COUNT / VIDEO LENGTH / SLIDE COUNT / OTHER SPECS]

---

### 4. What Do You Already Have?

**Existing materials:** [LIST ANY EXISTING CONTENT — interviews, data, testimonials, previous drafts, brand guidelines]

**Source contacts:** [IF THIS STORY REQUIRES INTERVIEWS — who should we talk to? Provide names and availability]

**Brand/voice guidelines:** [LINK TO CURRENT BRAND VOICE GUIDE, OR NOTE IF THIS IS A NEW BRAND]

---

### 5. Context and Constraints

**Campaign or project context:** [IS THIS PART OF A LARGER CAMPAIGN? WHAT ELSE IS HAPPENING?]

**Competitive context:** [ARE COMPETITORS TELLING A SIMILAR STORY? HOW SHOULD WE DIFFERENTIATE?]

**Regulatory or legal constraints:** [ANY CLAIMS THAT NEED LEGAL REVIEW? INDUSTRY-SPECIFIC COMPLIANCE?]

**Stakeholder approvals required:** [WHO NEEDS TO APPROVE THE FINAL STORY? LIST NAMES AND ROLES]

---

### 6. Success Metrics

**How will you measure success?** [ENGAGEMENT / CONVERSION / SHARES / QUALITATIVE FEEDBACK / OTHER]

**What does "great" look like?** [REFERENCE EXAMPLE — a story, ad, or piece of content that represents the quality or style you want]

---

### For Storytelling Squad Use Only

**Reviewed by:** [SQUAD LEAD NAME]
**Review date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
**Assigned task:** [TASK ID FROM PIPELINE]
**Assigned agent:** [AGENT NAME]
**Estimated delivery:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
**Notes:** [ANY CLARIFICATIONS OR FLAGS]

## Example

**Request ID:** STORY-2026-042
**Date submitted:** 2026-03-09
**Requester name:** Sarah Chen
**Requester squad/team:** Traffic Squad
**Priority:** HIGH
**Requested delivery date:** 2026-03-23

**Story type requested:** Customer story
**Brief description:** We need a customer transformation story for our Q2 ad campaign. The customer (Meridian Health) saw a 40% reduction in patient onboarding time after implementing our platform. We want to use their story as the anchor for video and social ads.
**Desired outcome:** Prospective healthcare clients should feel that our platform delivers measurable results and that implementation is less painful than they fear.

**Target audience:** Healthcare operations directors, mid-size hospital systems, skeptical about new technology
**Primary channel:** Video (30-second and 90-second cuts)
**Secondary channels:** LinkedIn social, email nurture sequence
**Existing materials:** Raw interview transcript with Meridian's COO, internal case study draft, usage data from analytics team
**Success metrics:** Ad click-through rate, demo request conversion
