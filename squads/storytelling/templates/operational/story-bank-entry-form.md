# Story Bank Entry Form

## Name
Story Bank Entry Form

## Purpose
Standardize how stories are added to the story bank. Every entry must include complete metadata so the bank remains searchable, classifiable, and useful. A story without metadata is a story that will never be found when someone needs it. This form ensures consistency across all entries regardless of who adds them.

## Instructions
1. Complete this form for every new story being added to the bank.
2. All required fields must be filled. Optional fields should be completed when the information is available.
3. Classify the story using the Hall Story Selection Matrix (story type) and applicable taxonomies (channel, industry, emotion).
4. Include the story itself — either the full text, a link to the source document, or both.
5. Submit the completed form to the story bank maintainer for review and indexing.
6. Stories that do not meet the minimum quality threshold (see quality checklist below) are returned for revision, not added to the bank.
