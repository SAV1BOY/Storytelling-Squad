# Customer Story
> Use this template to write a transformation-focused customer story. Unlike a full case study, a customer story is shorter, more emotional, and centers on the human experience of change. Ideal for social media, email, landing pages, and sales conversations.

## Instructions
Interview the customer or pull from existing testimonial data. Focus on the emotional journey: what life was like before, what triggered the change, what the experience was like, and who they are now. Use the "Before/After" contrast as the structural engine. The best customer stories feel like the customer is telling them.

## Example

**Before:** Maria Santos spent every Monday morning wrestling three spreadsheets to figure out which students were at risk of dropping out. By the time she had answers, it was too late. "I felt like I was always a step behind," she said.

**Trigger:** When student churn hit 22%, her CEO gave her a deadline: fix it or cut the program. Maria started looking for a better way.

**During:** She started a trial on a Tuesday. By Thursday, the platform had flagged 40 students her spreadsheets had missed. "I literally gasped," she said.

**After:** Six months later, churn dropped from 22% to 11%. Maria's Monday reporting went from 4 hours to 15 minutes. "I went from chasing data to actually helping students. That's what I signed up for."

**Transformation:** Maria went from reactive, spreadsheet-bound reporting to proactive, data-informed student support in under a week.
