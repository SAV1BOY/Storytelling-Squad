# One-Liner Template
> Use this template to craft a single sentence that clearly communicates your brand's story. The one-liner is used everywhere: website headers, email signatures, networking events, social bios, and pitch openings.

## Instructions
Follow the [Problem] + [Solution] + [Result] formula from StoryBrand. Keep it under 30 words. Test it by saying it out loud to someone unfamiliar with your business — if they say "tell me more," it works. If they nod politely and change the subject, revise.

## Template Body

### One-Liner Metadata
- **Brand / product:** [NAME]
- **Audience:** [WHO THIS IS FOR]
- **Context:** [WHERE THIS WILL BE USED — bio, header, intro, pitch opener]
- **Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
- **Version:** [v#]

---

### The Formula

**Part 1 — The Problem**
*Name the pain your customer faces. Start here because the problem opens a story loop in the listener's brain.*

- **Customer's problem in their words:** [HOW THEY WOULD DESCRIBE IT]
- **Draft problem clause:** "Most [AUDIENCE] struggle with [PROBLEM]..."

**Part 2 — The Solution**
*Describe what you offer as the bridge from problem to result. Keep it concrete.*

- **What you provide:** [YOUR SOLUTION — product, service, method]
- **Draft solution clause:** "...we provide [SOLUTION]..."

**Part 3 — The Result**
*Paint the outcome. Make it specific and desirable.*

- **Tangible result:** [MEASURABLE OUTCOME]
- **Emotional result:** [HOW THEY FEEL]
- **Draft result clause:** "...so they can [RESULT]."

---

### Complete One-Liner
> [PROBLEM CLAUSE] + [SOLUTION CLAUSE] + [RESULT CLAUSE]

---

### Refinement Checklist
- [ ] Under 30 words
- [ ] Starts with the problem (not "We are..." or "We help...")
- [ ] Contains a specific, desirable result
- [ ] Uses plain language (no jargon)
- [ ] Works when spoken aloud
- [ ] Creates curiosity (the listener wants to know more)
- [ ] Could be understood by someone outside your industry

### Variations

**Version B (email signature):**

**Version C (verbal / networking):**
> [ONE-LINER — conversational tone]

## Example

### Draft Process

**Problem:** Most product leaders can't get feature-level retention data without waiting weeks for their data team.
**Solution:** We built a self-serve analytics platform purpose-built for product teams.
**Result:** They get answers in minutes and make confident feature decisions.

### Complete One-Liner
> "Most product leaders wait weeks for retention data. Our self-serve analytics platform gives them feature-level answers in minutes — so they can build what actually keeps customers."

### Variations
**Website header:** "Stop guessing which features matter. Get retention answers in minutes."
**Email signature:** "Helping product teams understand retention — in minutes, not months."
**Verbal:** "You know how product teams have to wait weeks to find out which features actually keep customers? We built a tool that answers that in minutes."
