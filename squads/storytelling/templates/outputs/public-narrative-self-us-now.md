# Public Narrative: Self, Us, Now
> Use this template to write a public narrative speech or essay following Marshall Ganz's framework. For advocacy, leadership, campaigns, fundraising, and movement building.

## Instructions
Write each section as spoken prose — this is meant to be delivered aloud. The three stories must connect: Self reveals values through personal choice, Us connects those values to shared experience, Now channels shared values into urgent action. Each section should take roughly equal time. Practice aloud; this is a performance, not a reading.

## Example

**Self:** "When I was 14, I got suspended for fighting. I was angry all the time — at school, at home, at the world. A teacher named Ms. Davis started an after-school coding club and invited me. I remember thinking, 'Why would she want me there?' I showed up because I had nowhere else to go. That club was the first place anyone treated me like I was capable of something. That's when I decided: if someone believes in you, everything changes."

**Us:** "Every person in this room knows what it's like to watch a kid with nothing to do between 3 and 6 PM. We've seen them on the corner. We've worried about our own children. We're here because we believe every kid deserves a place where someone sees them as capable — not at risk."

**Now:** "The city budget vote is in 10 days. Right now, line item 4.7 allocates zero dollars to new after-school programs in our district. Zero. We have 3 programs for 12,000 students. Last year, District 5 showed up at their budget hearing with 200 signatures and won funding for 4 new programs. We can do the same. Tonight, sign the petition at the table by the door. Then show up at City Hall on March 19 at 6 PM. Bring a neighbor. This is how it changes."
