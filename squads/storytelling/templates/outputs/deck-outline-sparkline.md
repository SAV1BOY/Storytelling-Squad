# Deck Outline — Sparkline Structure
> Use this template to plan a presentation deck that alternates between "what is" and "what could be," building to a call to action. Based on Nancy Duarte's Sparkline. Works for keynotes, sales decks, board presentations, and all-hands.

## Instructions
Plan every slide before opening your slide tool. Each row is one slide. The Sparkline alternation creates tension that carries the audience forward. Proof slides anchor the vision in reality. The CTA slide should be the strongest slide in the deck.

### Slide-by-Slide Outline

| Slide # | Type | Headline / Title | Content Notes | Speaker Notes |
|---------|------|------------------|---------------|---------------|
| 1 | Title | [DECK TITLE] | [Subtitle, presenter name, date] | [Opening line — spoken, not on slide] |
| 2 | Hook | [PROVOCATIVE QUESTION or STAT] | [Single image or number, minimal text] | [The story or context behind the hook] |
| 3 | What Is #1 | [CURRENT REALITY — the problem] | [Data, screenshot, or quote showing the status quo] | [Describe the pain — make them nod] |
| 4 | What Is #1 | [DEEPER EVIDENCE of the problem] | [Supporting data or anecdote] | [Build the case — they should feel the weight] |
| 5 | What Could Be #1 | [THE POSSIBILITY — first glimpse] | [Contrasting image, aspirational stat, or example] | [Shift tone — lighter, forward-looking] |
| 6 | Proof #1 | [EVIDENCE that the possibility is real] | [Case study snippet, metric, or demo screenshot] | [Ground the vision in fact] |
| 7 | What Is #2 | [DEEPER PROBLEM — root cause or systemic issue] | [Why the status quo persists — structural barrier] | [Go deeper — surprise them with insight] |
| 8 | What Could Be #2 | [CLEARER VISION — how the solution works] | [Process diagram, demo frame, or 3-step plan] | [Make it tangible and simple] |
| 9 | Proof #2 | [STRONGER EVIDENCE] | [Customer quote, before/after data, ROI calculation] | [Stack credibility] |
| 10 | What Is #3 | [THE STAKES — cost of inaction] | [Trend line, competitive data, or cautionary example] | [Create urgency — this can't wait] |
| 11 | What Could Be #3 | [FULL VISION — the transformed future] | [Vivid description, multiple proof points] | [Peak inspiration — highest emotional moment] |
| 12 | Proof #3 | [BEST EVIDENCE — strongest case] | [Headline metric, customer video clip, or live demo] | [This is the "believe it" moment] |
| 13 | CTA | [CLEAR ASK — what to do next] | [One action, one button, one link] | [Be direct, be specific, be confident] |
| 14 | Close | [MEMORABLE CLOSING LINE] | [Callback to the opening hook — full circle] | [End with emotion, not logistics] |
| 15 | Appendix | [BACKUP SLIDES] | [Detailed data, objection answers, bios] | [For Q&A only — don't present these] |

### Emotional Arc
| Slide Range | Audience Emotion | Intensity |
|-------------|------------------|-----------|
| 1–2 | Curious | Low |
| 3–4 | Recognizing the problem | Medium |
| 5–6 | Hopeful | Medium |
| 7 | Concerned | High |
| 8–9 | Convinced | High |
| 10 | Urgent | High |
| 11–12 | Inspired | Peak |
| 13–14 | Motivated to act | Peak |

## Example

| Slide # | Type | Headline |
|---------|------|----------|
| 1 | Title | "The Retention Blind Spot" |
| 2 | Hook | "70% of product teams can't answer: which feature keeps customers?" |
| 3 | What Is | "The average time to get a retention answer: 5 business days." |
| 4 | What Is | "By then, the sprint is over. The decision was already made on gut feel." |
| 5 | What Could Be | "What if you could answer that question in 4 minutes?" |
| 6 | Proof | "BrightPath did. Churn dropped from 22% to 11%." |
| 7 | What Is | "The problem isn't laziness. It's infrastructure. BI tools weren't built for product teams." |
| 8 | What Could Be | "A platform built for product people: connect, ask, act. Three steps." |
| 9 | Proof | "200+ companies. NPS of 72. Average onboarding: 10 minutes." |
| 10 | What Is | "Companies that don't fix this lose 15% of customers to invisible churn annually." |
| 11 | What Could Be | "Companies that fix this make confident product decisions every sprint." |
| 12 | Proof | "Acme Corp: 'We shipped 3 fewer features last quarter — and grew retention 18%.'" |
| 13 | CTA | "Start a 14-day free trial. See your retention data by Friday." |
| 14 | Close | "Stop guessing. Start knowing." |
