# Social Media Story Series
> Use this template to plan a connected series of 5-7 social media posts that tell a story across multiple installments. Each post stands alone but together they build a narrative arc. Works for LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, and other platforms.

## Instructions
Plan the full series before writing any single post. Each post must be engaging on its own (most people won't see all of them) but reward those who follow the arc. Use a consistent visual and thematic thread. Tag each post with a series identifier so followers can find the full set.

### Post 1 — The Setup
*Introduce the theme with a story, question, or bold claim. Open the loop.*

- **Hook (first line):** "[FIRST SENTENCE — must earn the click to 'see more']"
- **Body:**
  > [STORY, OBSERVATION, or QUESTION — 3-6 sentences that establish the theme]
  > [END WITH A QUESTION or TEASER — "More on this tomorrow" or "Here's what I've learned (thread starts tomorrow)"]
- **Visual:** [IMAGE, GRAPHIC, or SELFIE description]
- **CTA:** [ENGAGEMENT — "Have you experienced this? Comment below."]
- **Series tag:** [POST 1 of 7 | #HASHTAG]

### Post 2 — The Deepening
*Go deeper into the problem or theme. Add a personal angle.*

- **Hook:** "[FIRST SENTENCE]"
- **Body:**
  > [EXPAND on the setup — share a personal experience, data point, or deeper observation]
  > [CONNECT to the audience — "You've probably felt this too"]
- **Visual:** [DESCRIPTION]
- **CTA:** [ENGAGEMENT — "What's your version of this?"]
- **Series tag:** [POST 2 of 7 | #HASHTAG]

### Post 3 — The Proof
*Share evidence — a customer story, data, or concrete example.*

- **Hook:** "[FIRST SENTENCE — lead with the result or surprise]"
- **Body:**
  > [TELL A SPECIFIC STORY — customer transformation, case study, or real-world example]
  > [INCLUDE A METRIC or QUOTE]
- **Visual:** [BEFORE/AFTER, DATA CHART, or CUSTOMER PHOTO]
- **CTA:** [SHARE — "Tag someone who needs to hear this"]
- **Series tag:** [POST 3 of 7 | #HASHTAG]

### Post 4 — The Challenge
*Challenge a common belief or practice. Create tension.*

- **Hook:** "[CONTRARIAN or MYTH-BUSTING first line]"
- **Body:**
  > [STATE THE COMMON BELIEF]
  > [EXPLAIN WHY IT'S WRONG or INCOMPLETE]
  > [PRESENT THE ALTERNATIVE VIEW with evidence]
- **Visual:** [COMPARISON GRAPHIC or BOLD TEXT IMAGE]
- **CTA:** [DEBATE — "Agree or disagree? Tell me why."]
- **Series tag:** [POST 4 of 7 | #HASHTAG]

### Post 5 — The Turn
*Share the insight or framework that changes everything.*

- **Hook:** "[FIRST SENTENCE — the 'aha' moment]"
- **Body:**
  > [PRESENT THE FRAMEWORK, INSIGHT, or METHOD]
  > [MAKE IT ACTIONABLE — steps, checklist, or template]
  > [SHOW HOW IT CONNECTS to everything shared so far]
- **Visual:** [FRAMEWORK DIAGRAM, CAROUSEL, or INFOGRAPHIC]
- **CTA:** [SAVE — "Save this for later. You'll need it."]
- **Series tag:** [POST 5 of 7 | #HASHTAG]

### Post 6 — The Lesson
*Reflect on the journey. Share the meta-lesson.*

- **Hook:** "[FIRST SENTENCE — reflective, personal]"
- **Body:**
  > [WHAT YOU LEARNED from exploring this theme]
  > [WHAT CHANGED in your thinking or practice]
  > [WHAT YOU HOPE the audience takes away]
- **Visual:** [PERSONAL PHOTO or SIMPLE TEXT IMAGE]
- **CTA:** [REFLECT — "What's your biggest takeaway from this series?"]
- **Series tag:** [POST 6 of 7 | #HASHTAG]

### Post 7 — The CTA
*Close the series with a clear ask. Reward followers with something.*

- **Hook:** "[FIRST SENTENCE — closure and energy]"
- **Body:**
  > [SUMMARIZE the arc in 2-3 sentences]
  > [OFFER SOMETHING — resource, template, link, next step]
  > [THANK the audience for following along]
- **Visual:** [RESOURCE PREVIEW, LINK CARD, or SUMMARY GRAPHIC]
- **CTA:** [ACTION — "Download the template," "Join the waitlist," "DM me 'STORY'"]
- **Series tag:** [POST 7 of 7 | #HASHTAG]

## Example

**Series:** "The 7 Stories Every Brand Needs" | #7BrandStories

**Post 1 (Setup):** "Most brands have a product story. Very few have a customer story. Even fewer have an enemy story. In the next 7 posts, I'm going to walk you through the 7 stories every brand needs — and most are missing at least 4. Let's start with the one that matters most..."

**Post 3 (Proof):** "BrightPath had zero customer stories on their website. Their homepage was 100% product features. In 90 days, they replaced every feature section with a customer transformation. Result: 34% increase in demo requests. The product didn't change. The story did."

**Post 5 (Turn):** "Here's the framework: The Story Bank. 7 categories, each with a template. Founder story. Customer story. Product story. Enemy story. Culture story. Vision story. Proof story. (Carousel with each template preview.)"

**Post 7 (CTA):** "That's the full framework. If you want the templates, I put them all in a free Notion doc. DM me 'STORIES' and I'll send the link."
