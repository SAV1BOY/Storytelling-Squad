# Testimonial Interview Script
> Use this template to conduct customer interviews that extract story-worthy material. The question flow is designed to elicit narrative — not just satisfaction statements. Works for case studies, video testimonials, and story banking.

## Instructions
Send questions to the customer 24 hours before the interview so they can reflect. During the interview, follow the flow but let the conversation breathe — the best stories come from follow-up questions like "Tell me more about that" and "What did that feel like?" Record with permission. Flag quotable moments in real time.

### Interview Flow

**Section 1 — Warm-Up and Context (5 min)**
*Build rapport. Get them comfortable talking.*

1. "Thanks for making time. Before we dive in — can you tell me a bit about your role and what your team is responsible for?"
   - **Listen for:** [TEAM SIZE, SCOPE, GOALS]

2. "What does a typical week look like for you?"
   - **Listen for:** [ROUTINES, PAIN POINTS embedded in daily life]

---

**Section 2 — The Before State (10 min)**
*Understand their world before your product/service. This is the "ordinary world" of the story.*

3. "Take me back to before you started using [PRODUCT]. What was the situation like?"
   - **Listen for:** [SPECIFIC WORKFLOWS, FRUSTRATIONS, TOOLS THEY USED]

4. "What was the biggest challenge your team was facing at that time?"
   - **Listen for:** [THE CORE PROBLEM — get them to be specific]

5. "Can you describe a specific moment when that problem was really painful? Like, a day where it hit hardest?"
   - **Listen for:** [A SCENE — time, place, what happened, how it felt. This is gold.]

6. "What had you tried before to solve this?"
   - **Listen for:** [PREVIOUS SOLUTIONS AND WHY THEY FAILED]

7. "How was this problem affecting the broader team or business?"
   - **Listen for:** [BUSINESS IMPACT — numbers, morale, downstream effects]

---

**Section 3 — The Turning Point (10 min)**
*How did they discover and decide on your solution?*

8. "How did you first hear about [PRODUCT]?"
   - **Listen for:** [DISCOVERY CHANNEL — referral, search, event]

9. "What made you decide to try it? Was there a specific moment?"
   - **Listen for:** [THE DECISION TRIGGER — what tipped them over]

10. "Were you skeptical at first? What were your concerns?"
    - **Listen for:** [OBJECTIONS — these become objection-handling material]

11. "What was the onboarding or setup experience like?"
    - **Listen for:** [EASE, SUPPORT, SURPRISES — good and bad]

12. "Was there an 'aha' moment — a point where you thought, 'This is working'?"
    - **Listen for:** [THE TURNING POINT — the narrative climax. Press for detail.]

---

**Section 4 — The After State (10 min)**
*The transformation. Get specific results and emotional payoff.*

13. "What's different now compared to before?"
    - **Listen for:** [CONTRAST — before vs. after, specific and vivid]

14. "Can you share any specific numbers — time saved, revenue impact, efficiency gains?"
    - **Listen for:** [METRICS — push for precision: percentages, dollar amounts, hours]

15. "How has this changed things for your team — not just the metrics, but how people feel about their work?"
    - **Listen for:** [EMOTIONAL TRANSFORMATION — confidence, relief, pride]

16. "Is there anything that surprised you — a benefit you didn't expect?"
    - **Listen for:** [UNEXPECTED VALUE — often the most interesting angle]

17. "If you had to describe the experience to a peer in one sentence, what would you say?"
    - **Listen for:** [THE NATURAL ONE-LINER — often becomes the pull quote]

---

**Section 5 — The Quote and Close (5 min)**
*Get the soundbite. Close with gratitude.*

18. "If someone in a similar role asked you, 'Is this worth it?' — what would you tell them?"
    - **Listen for:** [THE RECOMMENDATION — authentic, peer-to-peer]

19. "Anything else you'd want people to know about your experience?"
    - **Listen for:** [UNPROMPTED INSIGHTS — sometimes the best material]

20. "Thank you. We'll share a draft for your review before anything goes live. Is there anything you'd prefer we not include?"
    - **Listen for:** [RESTRICTIONS, SENSITIVITIES]

---

### Post-Interview Checklist

- [ ] Highlight top 3-5 quotable moments
- [ ] Identify the primary narrative arc (before → trigger → after)

## Example

**Question 5 response (the scene):** "I remember this one Monday — I came in at 7 AM to get the spreadsheet ready before our 9 AM meeting. I was manually vlookup-ing three different sheets. At 8:55, my laptop crashed and I lost 2 hours of work. I walked into that meeting with nothing. That was the moment I thought, 'There has to be a better way.'"

**Question 12 response (the aha):** "On day 3 of the trial, I asked the platform a question my data team had been working on for two weeks. I had the answer in four minutes. I literally called my director and said, 'You need to see this.'"

**Question 17 response (the one-liner):** "We stopped reacting to problems and started predicting them. That changed everything."
