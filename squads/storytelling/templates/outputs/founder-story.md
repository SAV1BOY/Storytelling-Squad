# Founder Story
> Use this template to write the origin story of a company, product, or initiative. Founder stories build trust, humanize brands, and give audiences a reason to care beyond features and pricing.

## Instructions
Interview the founder(s) to fill in each section. Focus on the specific moment of insight — not the resume. The best founder stories have a visible wound (the pain), a turning point (the insight), and a mission that feels inevitable given the journey. Keep it honest; audiences detect performative vulnerability.

### The Story Arc (Summary)
> [FOUNDER] was [DOING WHAT] when they experienced [PAIN]. They realized [INSIGHT]. Despite [OBSTACLES], they built [SOLUTION]. Today, [IMPACT]. Their mission: [MISSION].

## Example

**The Pain:** In 2021, Lena Torres was a product manager at a Series B startup. Every Monday, she waited 3-5 days for her data team to answer a simple question: "Which features are driving retention?" By the time she got answers, the sprint was over. She'd already shipped based on gut feel.

**The Insight:** After her third product bet failed in a row, Lena realized: "Product leaders don't need more data. They need faster answers to specific questions — and they need to ask those questions themselves."

**The Journey:** Lena quit her job, maxed out a credit card, and built a prototype in her apartment. The first version was ugly. The first 10 demos were rejections. Then customer #11 — a VP of Product at a 200-person SaaS company — said, "I've been waiting for someone to build this."

**The Mission:** Every product leader deserves to make data-informed decisions without waiting for someone else to pull the data.

**Current Impact:** Today, 200+ SaaS companies use the platform. Average time-to-insight: 4 minutes. Customer NPS: 72.
