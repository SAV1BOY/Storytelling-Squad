# Product Story
> Use this template to craft a narrative around a product — its origin, the need it fills, how it works, the transformation it enables, and where it's headed. Use for product pages, launch content, sales decks, and internal alignment.

## Instructions
Fill in each section collaboratively with product, marketing, and customer-facing teams. The product is not the hero — the user is. The product is the tool the hero uses to overcome their challenge. Frame every feature as a benefit to the user's story.

### Part 1 — The Origin (Why This Exists)
*Every product is a response to a frustration. Start there.*

- **The problem we witnessed:** [WHAT WE SAW HAPPENING — to users, to the market]
- **Who was suffering?** [SPECIFIC PERSON / ROLE / TEAM]
- **What were they using before?** [EXISTING SOLUTIONS AND THEIR FAILURES]
- **The moment of clarity:** [WHAT MADE US SAY "SOMEONE NEEDS TO BUILD THIS"]
- **Origin paragraph:** [2-3 SENTENCES — write it as a narrative, not a feature list]

### Part 2 — The Need (The World Without This Product)
*Paint the "before" picture. Make the reader recognize their own situation.*

- **The user's daily pain:** [SPECIFIC WORKFLOW OR EXPERIENCE that's broken]
- **Time wasted:** [HOW MUCH TIME THE OLD WAY COSTS]
- **Money wasted:** [FINANCIAL COST OF THE STATUS QUO]
- **Emotional cost:** [HOW IT FEELS — frustration, self-doubt, anxiety]
- **What they've tried:** [ALTERNATIVES AND WHY THEY FELL SHORT]
- **The unspoken need:** [WHAT THEY REALLY WANT — beyond the functional fix]

### Part 3 — How It Works (The Guide's Plan)
*Explain the product as a 3-step plan. Simplicity builds trust.*

- **Step 1:** [ACTION] — [WHAT THE USER DOES]
  - What happens: [WHAT THE PRODUCT DOES IN RESPONSE]
  - User feels: [EMOTION — e.g., "That was easy"]

- **Step 2:** [ACTION] — [WHAT THE USER DOES]
  - What happens: [WHAT THE PRODUCT DOES IN RESPONSE]
  - User feels: [EMOTION — e.g., "I can see the answer"]

- **Step 3:** [ACTION] — [WHAT THE USER DOES / GETS]
  - What happens: [THE RESULT]
  - User feels: [EMOTION — e.g., "I'm in control"]

### Part 4 — The Transformation (Before and After)
*Contrast the user's life before and after adopting the product.*

| Dimension | Before | After |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Workflow | [OLD WAY] | [NEW WAY] |
| Time spent | [OLD DURATION] | [NEW DURATION] |
| Confidence | [OLD FEELING] | [NEW FEELING] |
| Results | [OLD OUTCOME] | [NEW OUTCOME] |
| Identity | [WHO THEY WERE] | [WHO THEY ARE NOW] |

- **Transformation statement:** "[USER] goes from [BEFORE] to [AFTER]."

### Part 5 — The Proof (Why This Is Real)
*Evidence that the transformation is not theoretical.*

- **Key metric 1:** [RESULT + NUMBER — from real customer data]
- **Key metric 2:** [RESULT + NUMBER]
- **Customer quote:** "[QUOTE — specific, enthusiastic, credible]"
- **Logos / social proof:** [NOTABLE CUSTOMERS, AWARDS, PRESS]

### Part 6 — The Vision (Where This Is Going)
*Connect the product to a larger mission. Products with a story about the future attract believers, not just buyers.*

- **Current state:** [WHAT THE PRODUCT DOES TODAY]
- **Next milestone:** [WHAT'S COMING — upcoming feature, capability, expansion]
- **Long-term vision:** [WHAT THE WORLD LOOKS LIKE IF THIS PRODUCT SUCCEEDS AT SCALE]
- **Vision statement:** "[ONE SENTENCE — aspirational but grounded]"

### Product Story Summary
> [PRODUCT] was born because [ORIGIN PAIN]. For [USER], it replaces [OLD WAY] with [NEW WAY], delivering [KEY RESULT] in [TIMEFRAME]. Today, [PROOF]. Tomorrow, [VISION].

## Example

**Origin:** Our founders spent years as product managers, waiting days for simple retention data. They watched teams ship features based on gut feel because the data pipeline was too slow and too complex.

**Need:** Product leaders spend an average of 4 hours per week waiting for data requests. By the time answers arrive, the sprint is over.

**How It Works:** 1) Connect your data source in 10 minutes. 2) Ask a question in plain English. 3) Get a visual answer with actionable recommendations.

**Transformation:** Product leaders go from "I think this feature matters" to "I know this feature drives 23% of our retention" — in minutes instead of days.

**Proof:** 200+ SaaS companies. 80% reduction in time-to-insight. NPS of 72. "I made my first confident product decision in years." — VP Product, Acme Corp.

**Vision:** A world where every product decision is informed by evidence, and no product leader has to guess.
