# Webinar Narrative Script
> Use this template to script a webinar that weaves storytelling through education and offer. Structured as Teach-Tension-Offer with story integration at every stage.

## Instructions
Complete the Webinar Narrative Brief first. This script template turns that brief into a deliverable script with speaker notes, timing, and story beats. Write for speaking, not reading — use short sentences, conversational language, and natural transitions. Practice the story sections aloud.

## Template Body

### Script Metadata
- **Webinar title:** [TITLE]
- **Host:** [NAME]
- **Guest(s):** [NAME(S) — if any]
- **Duration:** [MINUTES]
- **Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
- **Platform:** [TOOL]

---

### Opening (0:00–0:05) — Hook and Welcome

**Slide:** [TITLE SLIDE — title, host name, date]

**Script:**
> "Welcome, everyone. I'm [NAME], and today we're going to talk about [TOPIC]. But first, let me tell you a quick story..."

**Opening story (90 seconds):**
> [TELL A SHORT STORY that illustrates the core problem the audience faces. End with a question or tension that the webinar will resolve.]
> "[STORY — 4-6 sentences, specific, personal or customer-based]"

**Agenda preview:**
> "In the next [DURATION], you'll learn:
> 1. [LESSON 1 — benefit framed]
> 2. [LESSON 2 — benefit framed]
> 3. [LESSON 3 — benefit framed]
> And I'll show you [TOOL/RESOURCE/OFFER] that puts all of this into practice."

**Housekeeping:**
> "Drop your questions in the chat — we'll answer them at the end. [POLL/INTERACTIVE ELEMENT if applicable]."

---

### Teach Section (0:05–0:30) — Deliver Value

**Lesson 1: [TOPIC]** (0:05–0:15)

**Slide:** [KEY CONCEPT or FRAMEWORK visual]

**Teach point:**
> [CORE LESSON — explain the concept clearly, as if to a smart friend]
> [WRITE 3-5 SENTENCES of instruction]

**Story illustration:**
> "Let me show you what this looks like in practice. [CUSTOMER/PERSONAL STORY that demonstrates Lesson 1 in action — 3-4 sentences]"

**Actionable takeaway:**
> "Here's what you can do with this right now: [SPECIFIC ACTION the attendee can take today]."

---

**Lesson 2: [TOPIC]** (0:15–0:22)

**Slide:** [KEY CONCEPT or EXAMPLE visual]

**Teach point:**
> [CORE LESSON — build on Lesson 1]
> [WRITE 3-5 SENTENCES]

**Story illustration:**
> "[STORY that demonstrates Lesson 2 — different customer or angle — 3-4 sentences]"

**Actionable takeaway:**
> "Try this: [SPECIFIC ACTION]."

---

**Lesson 3: [TOPIC]** (0:22–0:30)

**Slide:** [KEY CONCEPT, DATA, or DEMO visual]

**Teach point:**
> [CORE LESSON — the most advanced or impactful point]
> [WRITE 3-5 SENTENCES]

**Story illustration:**
> "[STORY — 3-4 sentences, strongest example]"

**Actionable takeaway:**
> "[SPECIFIC ACTION — this one should be slightly harder, planting the seed for the tension section]."

---

### Tension Section (0:30–0:40) — Reveal the Gap

**Slide:** [CONTRAST visual — DIY vs. Supported]

**Transition:**
> "Now, everything I just taught you is real and actionable. You can go do it today. But here's what I've noticed..."

**The gap:**
> "[DESCRIBE the limitation of doing this alone — time, consistency, scale, expertise]"
> [WRITE 3-5 SENTENCES that make the audience feel the gap without shaming them]

**Story bridge:**
> "I want to tell you about two people. [PERSON A] tried to do this on their own. [DESCRIBE their experience — partial success, inconsistency, burnout]. [PERSON B] used [SYSTEM/TOOL/SUPPORT]. [DESCRIBE their experience — faster, more consistent, better results]. The difference wasn't talent — it was the system."
> [WRITE 4-6 SENTENCES]

**Tension question:**
> "So the question is: do you want to keep doing this manually, or do you want a system that makes it repeatable?"

---

### Offer Section (0:40–0:50) — Present the Resolution

**Slide:** [OFFER visual — name, components, CTA]

**Transition:**
> "That's exactly why we built [PRODUCT/OFFER NAME]."

**Offer description:**
> "Here's what it includes:
> - [COMPONENT 1 — and how it solves a specific pain]
> - [COMPONENT 2 — and the benefit]
> - [COMPONENT 3 — and the benefit]"

**Proof at point of offer:**
> "[TESTIMONIAL or METRIC — e.g., '200 teams use this. Average time to first result: 3 days.']"

**Bonuses / urgency:**
> "[BONUS — e.g., 'Everyone on this live call gets an extended 30-day trial']"
> "[URGENCY — e.g., 'This offer expires at midnight Friday']"

**CTA:**
> "Go to [LINK] right now. [DESCRIBE what happens when they click]. If you have questions, drop them in the chat."

---

### Q&A (0:50–0:60) — Answer and Reinforce

**Slide:** [Q&A slide with CTA link still visible]

**Approach:**
- Answer questions directly
- Weave in additional proof when relevant
- Repeat the CTA naturally: "Great question — that's actually one of the things [PRODUCT] handles. You can check it out at [LINK]."

**Closing:**
> "Thank you all for being here. Remember: [RESTATE KEY LESSON]. And if you want [OUTCOME], [CTA] is the fastest way to get there. See you next time."

## Example

**Opening story:** "Six months ago, a content manager named Raj told me he'd published 200 blog posts that year. Traffic was great. Pipeline from content? Zero. He said, 'I'm a content machine with no engine.' That's the problem we're solving today."

**Lesson 1:** How to identify which customer moments are storyworthy (not just which customers are happy).

**Tension:** "You now know the framework. But I've watched dozens of teams try to do this manually — they get 2-3 great stories, then it falls apart. The bottleneck isn't skill. It's the system."

**Offer:** "That's why we built the Customer Story Engine — templates, interview scripts, approval workflows, and a story bank. 200 teams use it. Start your 30-day trial at storysquad.co/trial."
