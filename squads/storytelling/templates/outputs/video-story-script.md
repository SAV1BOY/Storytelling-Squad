# Video Story Script
> Use this template to write a video script with narrative structure and timing marks. Works for brand videos, explainers, testimonials, ads, and social clips. Every second counts — no wasted frames.

## Instructions
Complete the Video Narrative Brief first. Write this script for the ear and eye simultaneously — what viewers hear and see must work together. Include timing marks so editors and producers know pacing. Read aloud and time with a stopwatch before finalizing.
