# Case Study Story
> Use this template to write a narrative-driven case study. Goes beyond "challenge/solution/result" by adding story structure, emotional beats, and proof. Turns a flat testimonial into a compelling transformation story.

## Instructions
Fill in every section after completing the Case Study Brief. Write in narrative prose, not bullet points — this template provides the scaffolding, but the final output should read as a story. Use the Five Commandments to ensure the story turns. Get customer approval before publishing.

## Example

**Title:** "How BrightPath Education Cut Student Churn in Half in 6 Months"
**Subtitle:** A student success team replaced manual tracking with predictive analytics — and saved an estimated $600K annually.

**Context:** BrightPath Education serves 50,000 students across 12 campuses. Their student success team was experienced and dedicated, running weekly check-ins and maintaining careful records.

**Challenge:** But all that care was manual. Maria Santos, VP of Student Success, spent every Monday morning wrestling three spreadsheets to identify at-risk students. By the time she had answers, students had already dropped off. Churn hit 22% — costing an estimated $1.2M per year.

**Results:** Within six months, student churn dropped from 22% to 11%. The team identified at-risk students 3 weeks earlier on average. Maria's Monday reporting went from 4 hours to 15 minutes. "We stopped reacting and started predicting," Maria said. "That changed everything."
