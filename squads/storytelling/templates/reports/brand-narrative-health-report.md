# Brand Narrative Health Report
> Use this template to assess the overall health of a brand's narrative ecosystem — consistency across channels, audience recall, sentiment trends, and alignment between what the brand says and what the audience hears. Run semi-annually or annually.

## Instructions
This report combines quantitative data (analytics, surveys) with qualitative assessment (content review, stakeholder interviews). It's a strategic document, not a performance dashboard — focus on patterns and recommendations, not individual asset metrics.

## Example

**Overall score: 6.2/10.** Consistency is strong on website and email (8/10) but weak in sales collateral (4/10) — reps are using outdated decks with different messaging. Recall is moderate: 62% of surveyed prospects could name one thing that differentiates us, but only 24% could articulate our story. Differentiation is a concern: our homepage messaging is nearly identical to Competitor A's. Recommendation: Update sales collateral to match current BrandScript, and develop a unique "enemy" narrative that competitors can't copy.
