# Story Bank Status Report
> Use this template to track the health of your organization's story bank — the centralized repository of narrative assets. Identifies coverage, gaps, freshness, and upcoming needs. Run monthly.

## Instructions
Review the story bank and fill in each section. Cross-reference with sales requests, campaign plans, and upcoming launches to anticipate needs before they become urgent. This report is the bridge between the story audit (strategic) and daily content operations (tactical).
