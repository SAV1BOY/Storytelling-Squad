# Narrative Performance Report
> Use this template to measure how well narrative assets are performing against business goals. Tracks retention, conversion, recall, and engagement metrics for story-driven content. Run monthly or quarterly.

## Instructions
Pull data from analytics, CRM, and survey tools. Compare story-driven content against non-narrative controls where possible. This report should answer: "Is our storytelling actually working?" Not all metrics apply to every asset — fill in what's relevant and mark others [N/A].

## Example

**Executive Summary:** Measured 12 story-driven assets in Q1. Story-driven blog posts had 2.3x longer time-on-page than feature-focused posts. The BrightPath case study was the #1 converting asset on the website (6.2% conversion rate vs. 2.1% site average). Customer story emails had 34% higher click-through than product announcement emails. Recommendation: Double the customer story publishing cadence and retire 3 stale product-focused blog posts.

**Key Finding:** Video testimonials had a 72% average watch-through rate when they opened with the customer's pain. Videos that opened with company background had a 31% watch-through rate. The hook matters more than the production value.
