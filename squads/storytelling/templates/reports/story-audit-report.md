# Story Audit Report
> Use this template to assess the current state of narrative assets across a brand or organization. Identifies what stories exist, what's missing, what's working, and what needs attention. Run quarterly or before major campaigns.

## Instructions
Inventory all existing narrative assets, evaluate their quality and coverage, and identify gaps. This report should be completed by the storytelling lead with input from marketing, sales, product, and customer success. Use the Story Bank categories as the organizing framework.

**Executive Summary:** Audited 34 narrative assets across brand, customer, product, and sales categories. 22 are in active use, 8 are stale (not updated in 6+ months), and 4 critical gaps were identified. The most urgent gap: zero customer stories for the enterprise segment, which represents 40% of pipeline.

**Top Gap:** No customer stories from enterprise clients (500+ employees). Sales team reports losing 3 deals in Q4 where the prospect asked for "someone our size." Recommendation: Prioritize 2 enterprise case studies by end of Q2.
