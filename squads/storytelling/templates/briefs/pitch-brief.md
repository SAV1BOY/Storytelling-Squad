# Pitch Brief
> Use this template to prepare any pitch — investor, sales, partnership, or internal. Fill it in before building slides or writing a script so the narrative arc is clear.

## Instructions
Complete this brief with the pitch owner. Use the Pitch Anything framework (Oren Klaff) for frame control and the StoryBrand framework to keep the audience as hero. Time every section against the total pitch duration to ensure pacing.

## Example

### Pitch Overview
- **Pitch type:** Investor (Series A)
- **Presenter(s):** Dana Reeves, CEO; Marco Li, CTO
- **Date of pitch:** 2026-04-02
- **Time limit:** 20 minutes + 10 min Q&A

### Audience
- **Who is in the room?** 3 partners at Gradient Ventures — focused on AI/ML infrastructure.
- **What do they care about most?** Market size, defensibility, capital efficiency.
- **What frame do we want to set?** This is a category-defining infrastructure play, not just another SaaS tool.

### The Problem
- **The world as it is:** 70% of ML models never make it to production because deployment tooling is fragmented and brittle.
- **Why now?** Enterprise AI budgets doubled YoY but deployment success rates haven't improved since 2022.

### The Ask
- **What are you asking for?** $12M Series A at $60M pre-money.
- **Scarcity:** Round is 70% committed. Closing in 3 weeks.
