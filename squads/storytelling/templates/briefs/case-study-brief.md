# Case Study Brief
> Use this template to gather all inputs before writing a customer case study. Fill it in after the customer agrees to participate and before any drafting begins.

## Instructions
The brief owner interviews the customer success manager and the customer contact to populate all fields. Legal and the customer must approve participation before writing starts. Reference the Five Commandments framework to ensure the case study has narrative tension, not just a flat recounting of facts.

## Example

### Project Information
- **Brief ID:** CS-2026-017
- **Date Created:** 2026-02-10
- **Writer:** Jamie Torres
- **Customer Success Contact:** Alex Kim
- **Target Publish Date:** 2026-03-20
- **Status:** Drafting

### Customer Information
- **Company name:** BrightPath Education
- **Industry:** EdTech
- **Company size:** 200 employees, $30M ARR
- **Customer since:** 2024

### The Challenge
- **Situation before:** BrightPath's student engagement team manually tracked 14 different metrics across 3 spreadsheets, updated weekly by a part-time analyst.
- **Specific problem:** They couldn't identify at-risk students fast enough — by the time the data was ready, students had already dropped off.
- **Business impact:** 22% student churn rate, costing an estimated $1.2M annually.

### The Results
- **Primary quantitative result:** Student churn dropped from 22% to 11% within 6 months.
- **Key quote:** "We went from reacting to problems to predicting them. That changed everything." — Maria Santos, VP Student Success
