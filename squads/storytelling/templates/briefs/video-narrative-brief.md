# Video Narrative Brief
> Use this template to plan any video content — brand film, product explainer, testimonial, social clip, or ad. Fill in before scripting or storyboarding.

## Instructions
Complete with the video producer and storytelling lead. Hook strategy is critical: the first 3 seconds determine whether the viewer stays. Use the Story Spine for narrative structure and the ABT framework for concise messaging within time constraints.

## Example

### Video Overview
- **Project name:** "The 3-Day Turnaround" — Customer Story
- **Video type:** Testimonial
- **Target length:** 90 seconds

### Hook Strategy
- **Hook type:** Bold claim
- **Hook line:** "We went live in three days. Our last vendor took six months."
- **Why this stops the scroll:** Directly challenges the viewer's assumption about implementation timelines.

### Core Message
- **ABT:** BrightPath Education had growing student churn, BUT they couldn't identify at-risk students fast enough with manual tracking, THEREFORE they adopted our platform and cut churn in half within six months.
