# Story Brief
> Use this template to kick off any narrative project. Fill it in before writing begins to align stakeholders on audience, message, and desired outcome.

## Instructions
Complete every field below. If a field is unknown, mark it [TBD] and flag it for research. Share the completed brief with the storytelling lead and requesting squad before drafting starts. Reference the Story Grid framework for value shifts and the StoryBrand framework for clarity on the customer as hero.

## Example

### Project Information
- **Brief ID:** BRIEF-2026-03-042
- **Requester:** Sarah Chen / Growth Squad
- **Date Created:** 2026-03-01
- **Due Date:** 2026-03-15
- **Priority:** High
- **Status:** Approved

### Narrative Objective
- **What is the goal of this story?** Drive trial signups for the new analytics dashboard among mid-market SaaS leaders.
- **What single action should the audience take after hearing this?** Start a 14-day free trial.
- **How will we measure success?** Trial signup conversion rate from blog post (target: 4%).

### Audience
- **Primary audience:** VP of Product at B2B SaaS companies, 50-500 employees, data-curious but time-poor.
- **What do they currently believe?** "Analytics tools are expensive and take months to set up."
- **What do we want them to believe after?** "This tool gives me answers in days, not months."
- **What is their biggest fear or frustration related to this topic?** Making a big investment in a tool their team won't adopt.

### Core Message
- **One-sentence story summary:** A product leader drowning in spreadsheets discovered a dashboard that cut reporting time by 80% and finally got her team aligned on what matters.
- **Belief to change:** "Analytics is a heavy lift" → "Analytics can be lightweight and immediate."
- **Proof points:**
  1. 80% reduction in weekly reporting time (internal case study).
  2. NPS of 72 among current users.
  3. Quote from VP Product at Acme Corp: "We were live in 3 days."

### Tone and Voice
- **Brand voice attributes:** Confident, clear, slightly informal.
- **Emotional register:** Relief, empowerment.
- **Words to use:** Simple, fast, clarity, aligned, actionable.
- **Words to avoid:** Revolutionary, disrupt, synergy, leverage.

### Channel and Format
- **Primary channel:** Company blog, repurposed to LinkedIn.
- **Format:** Long-form article (1,200 words) with pull quotes.
- **Length / duration:** 1,200 words / 5-min read.
- **Supporting assets needed:** 1 hero image, 2 data charts, 1 customer headshot.
