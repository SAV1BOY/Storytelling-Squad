# Brand Story Brief
> Use this template when developing or refreshing a brand's core narrative. This brief captures positioning, archetype, enemy, promise, and proof — the building blocks of a brand story that resonates.

## Instructions
Work with brand leadership and marketing to fill every section. Use the StoryBrand SB7 framework and the brand archetype model as references. The completed brief becomes the source of truth for all downstream brand storytelling assets.

## Example

### Brand Identity
- **Brand name:** Trailhead Analytics
- **Tagline:** "See the path. Take the lead."
- **Category:** B2B Product Analytics
- **Date:** 2026-03-01
- **Version:** v1.0
- **Owner:** Marcus Reed / CMO

### Brand Positioning
- **For:** Product leaders at mid-market SaaS companies
- **Who need:** Clarity on which features drive retention
- **Our brand is:** A product analytics platform
- **That provides:** Actionable feature-level insights in minutes, not months
- **Unlike:** Legacy BI tools that require a data team to operate
- **We:** Deliver self-serve analytics designed for product people, not analysts

### Brand Archetype
- **Primary archetype:** Explorer
- **Secondary archetype:** Sage
- **Why this archetype fits:** Our customers are adventurous product leaders seeking new territory. They want knowledge as a tool for discovery.
- **Archetype voice traits:** Curious, confident, forward-looking

### The Enemy
- **External enemy:** Bloated analytics platforms that take 6 months to deploy and require a PhD to use.
- **Internal enemy:** The fear that "I'm not data-savvy enough to make the right call."
- **Philosophical enemy:** Product decisions should not be guesses.
- **Enemy statement:** "It's wrong that product leaders should have to wait months and hire specialists just to understand their own product."

### Brand Promise
- **Core promise:** You will understand what drives your product's growth within your first week.
- **Emotional payoff:** Confidence and control.
- **Functional payoff:** 80% faster time-to-insight versus legacy tools.
