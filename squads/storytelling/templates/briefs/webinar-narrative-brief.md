# Webinar Narrative Brief
> Use this template to plan any webinar where story is woven into education and offer. Fill in before scripting to ensure the teach-tension-offer arc is clear.

## Instructions
Complete with the webinar host and content lead. The core principle: teach generously, then create tension around what the audience still can't do alone, then present the offer as the resolution. Use the Sparkline framework to alternate between current reality and possibility.

## Example

### Webinar Overview
- **Title:** "The 3-Story Framework: How to Turn Customer Wins into Revenue"
- **Host:** Jenna Park, Head of Content
- **Guest:** David Muir, Customer Success Lead
- **Duration:** 50 minutes

### Teach Section
- **Key lesson 1:** How to identify a storyworthy customer moment (not just a satisfied customer).
- **Key lesson 2:** The 3-part structure for a compelling case study (Challenge → Turning Point → Transformation).
- **Key lesson 3:** Where to deploy customer stories for maximum pipeline impact.
- **Story integration:** Lesson 1 uses the BrightPath discovery story. Lesson 2 walks through writing it live. Lesson 3 shows before/after conversion data.

### Tension Section
- **Tension point:** "You now know the framework, but extracting stories from customers consistently requires a system — interview scripts, approval workflows, and a story bank."
- **Gap revealed:** DIY story collection is sporadic; a system makes it repeatable.

### Offer Section
- **Offer:** 30-day free trial of our Customer Story Engine (includes templates, interview scripts, approval tracker).
- **CTA:** "Start your free trial at [LINK]. Use code WEBINAR30 for an extended trial."
