# Presentation Brief
> Use this template to plan any deck or presentation — keynote, internal review, client meeting, or conference talk. Fill it in before building slides.

## Instructions
Complete this brief with the presenter and any collaborating squad members. Use the Sparkline framework to structure the flow between "what is" and "what could be." Every slide should earn its place — if it doesn't advance the story, cut it.

## Example

### Presentation Details
- **Title:** "Q1 Growth Story: What Worked, What Didn't, What's Next"
- **Presenter:** Priya Nair, Head of Growth
- **Date:** 2026-04-10
- **Duration:** 30 min + 10 min Q&A
- **Occasion:** Company all-hands

### Audience
- **Who:** Full company (~150 people), mixed functions.
- **Baseline knowledge:** They know the Q1 targets but not the detailed results.
- **Mood going in:** Curious, slightly anxious about performance.
- **Desired mood leaving:** Proud of wins, clear on next priorities, energized.

### Key Message
- **One thing:** We hit 92% of our growth target, and the experiments that fell short taught us exactly where to double down in Q2.

### Desired Action
- **Action:** Every team lead reviews their Q2 OKRs against the new growth priorities by Friday.
