# Public Narrative Brief
> Use this template when preparing a public narrative — for advocacy, movement building, campaigns, or leadership communications. Based on Marshall Ganz's Story of Self, Us, and Now framework.

## Instructions
Work with the speaker or campaign lead to fill in each section. Public narrative is about connecting personal experience to shared values to urgent action. Every section must answer "why" — why you, why us, why now. Reference the Public Narrative framework by Marshall Ganz.

## Template Body

### Narrative Overview
- **Brief ID:** [PN-YYYY-###]
- **Speaker / author:** [NAME]
- **Cause / campaign:** [WHAT THIS NARRATIVE SERVES]
- **Context:** [WHERE AND WHEN THIS WILL BE DELIVERED — rally, op-ed, speech, video]
- **Audience:** [WHO WILL HEAR THIS]
- **Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
- **Duration / length:** [MINUTES or WORD COUNT]

### The Cause
- **What is the issue?** [ISSUE — clear, specific]
- **Why does it matter?** [STAKES — who is affected and how]
- **What change are we seeking?** [DESIRED OUTCOME]
- **Who opposes this change and why?** [OPPOSITION — their framing]
- **Our framing:** [HOW WE FRAME THE ISSUE — values-based]

### Story of Self (Why You)
- **Choice point:** [A SPECIFIC MOMENT when you faced a choice that shaped your commitment to this cause]
- **What was the challenge?** [WHAT YOU FACED]
- **What values guided your choice?** [VALUES — e.g., justice, dignity, responsibility, compassion]
- **What did you do?** [ACTION YOU TOOK]
- **What did you learn?** [LESSON — why this moment matters to who you are today]
- **Emotional core:** [THE FEELING this story evokes — determination, hope, moral clarity]

### Story of Us (Why We)
- **Shared experience:** [A MOMENT OR EXPERIENCE your audience has in common]
- **Shared values:** [VALUES that bind this community together]
- **Shared challenge:** [WHAT "WE" face together that no individual can solve alone]
- **Community identity:** [WHO "WE" ARE — name the community]
- **Proof of collective strength:** [A TIME when "we" came together and made something happen]

### Story of Now (Why This Moment)
- **Urgent challenge:** [WHAT IS HAPPENING RIGHT NOW that demands action]
- **What is at stake if we don't act?** [CONSEQUENCE OF INACTION]
- **What is possible if we do act?** [VISION OF SUCCESS]
- **Specific ask:** [EXACT ACTION — sign, call, show up, donate, vote, share]
- **Deadline or urgency:** [WHY NOW — date, decision point, window closing]
- **Hope:** [WHY WE CAN WIN — evidence that action will matter]

## Example

### Narrative Overview
- **Speaker:** Amara Osei, Community Organizer
- **Cause:** Expanding after-school programs in underserved districts
- **Context:** City council public comment session
- **Duration:** 3 minutes

### Story of Self
- **Choice point:** At age 14, Amara was suspended for a fight. A teacher started an after-school coding club and invited her. That club became the first place she felt capable instead of "at risk."
- **Values:** Every kid deserves a place where they're seen as capable.
- **Emotional core:** The feeling of being believed in for the first time.

### Story of Us
- **Shared experience:** Every parent in this room has watched their child come home with nothing to do between 3 and 6 PM.
- **Shared values:** We believe our children deserve better than empty afternoons.
- **Shared challenge:** Our district has 3 after-school programs for 12,000 students.

### Story of Now
- **Urgent challenge:** The city budget vote is in 10 days. Line item 4.7 allocates $0 to new after-school programs.
- **Specific ask:** Sign our petition tonight and show up at the budget hearing on March 19.
- **Hope:** Last year, District 5 used this exact approach and won funding for 4 new programs.
