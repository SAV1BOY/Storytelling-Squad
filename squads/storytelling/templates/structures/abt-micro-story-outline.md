# ABT Micro-Story Outline
> Use this template for any situation that needs a concise, compelling narrative in 1-3 sentences. The ABT (And/But/Therefore) framework by Randy Olson is the DNA of story — it works for elevator pitches, social posts, email subject lines, presentation openings, and slide titles.

## Instructions
Fill in the three parts in order. The AND establishes context (agreement), the BUT introduces tension (contradiction), and the THEREFORE delivers resolution (consequence). Test your ABT by reading it aloud — if it doesn't create a "lean forward" moment at the BUT, the tension isn't strong enough.

### The ABT Formula

**AND (Context + Agreement)**
[SUBJECT] is/does [CONTEXT], AND [ADDITIONAL CONTEXT that establishes the normal state].

- **Who or what is this about?** [SUBJECT]
- **What is the baseline situation?** [NORMAL STATE — the audience nods along]
- **What additional context do they need?** [SETUP — builds the world]

**BUT (Tension + Contradiction)**

- **Why does this matter?** [STAKES — what's at risk]

**THEREFORE (Resolution + Consequence)**

- **What was done about it?** [ACTION TAKEN]
- **What was the result?** [OUTCOME — specific, measurable if possible]
- **What should the audience do?** [CTA — if applicable]

---

### Complete ABT Statement
> [SUBJECT] [CONTEXT], AND [ADDITIONAL CONTEXT]. BUT [TENSION]. THEREFORE [RESOLUTION].

---

### Anti-Patterns to Avoid

**AAA (And, And, And) — No tension:**
> "We built a product, and it has great features, and customers like it, and we're growing."
> Problem: No conflict. The audience tunes out.

**DHY (Despite, However, Yet) — Buried tension:**
> "Despite challenges, we managed to succeed."
> Problem: The tension is glossed over, not felt.

**ABT Test — Does it pass?**
- [ ] The AND makes the audience nod ("Yes, I know this")
- [ ] The BUT makes the audience lean in ("Oh, tell me more")
- [ ] The THEREFORE makes the audience satisfied or ready to act ("That makes sense")

---

### Variations

**ABT for a Customer Story:**
> [CUSTOMER] was [CONTEXT], AND [THEIR NORMAL], BUT [PROBLEM THEY FACED], THEREFORE [HOW THEY SOLVED IT WITH YOUR HELP].

**ABT for a Product:**
> [USERS] need [NEED], AND [CURRENT APPROACH], BUT [WHY IT FAILS], THEREFORE [YOUR SOLUTION + RESULT].

**ABT for a Pitch:**
> [MARKET] is [TREND], AND [OPPORTUNITY], BUT [GAP NO ONE HAS FILLED], THEREFORE [YOUR COMPANY + WHAT YOU DO].

**ABT for a Campaign:**
> [AUDIENCE] wants [DESIRE], AND [WHAT THEY'VE TRIED], BUT [WHY IT DOESN'T WORK], THEREFORE [YOUR OFFER].

## Example

**Customer story ABT:**
> BrightPath Education serves 50,000 students across 12 campuses, AND their student success team was dedicated and experienced. BUT they couldn't identify at-risk students until it was too late — churn hit 22%. THEREFORE they adopted predictive analytics and cut churn in half within six months.

**Product ABT:**
> Product teams need to understand which features drive retention, AND most use a mix of spreadsheets and BI tools. BUT by the time they get answers, the quarter is over. THEREFORE we built a self-serve analytics platform that delivers feature-level retention insights in minutes.

**Pitch ABT:**
> Enterprise AI spending has doubled year over year, AND companies are hiring ML engineers faster than ever. BUT 70% of ML models never make it to production because deployment tooling is fragmented. THEREFORE we built the deployment layer that takes models from notebook to production in one click.
