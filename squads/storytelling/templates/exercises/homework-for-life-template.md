# Homework for Life Template
> Use this template to capture one storyworthy moment each day. Based on Matthew Dicks' practice from "Storyworthy." The exercise trains you to notice small moments of change, surprise, or realization — the raw material of great stories.

## Instructions
Every night before bed, reflect on your day and identify one moment that was different from every other moment. It doesn't need to be dramatic — the best stories come from small, specific, human moments. Write one sentence maximum. Do this daily for at least 30 days to build the habit. After 30 days, review your entries — you'll have a story bank of real, personal material.

### Tips for Better Entries
- Write about **change** — a moment where something shifted (belief, mood, relationship, understanding)
- Be **specific** — "My daughter asked why the sky changes color" beats "Had a nice evening"
- Focus on **small moments** — the mundane contains the universal
- Write **one sentence** — don't draft a story, just capture the seed
- If nothing happened today, ask: "What surprised me? What did I learn? What did I feel that I didn't expect?"
