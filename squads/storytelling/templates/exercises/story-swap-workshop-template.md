# Story Swap Workshop Template
> Use this template to run a structured workshop where participants share, refine, and give feedback on stories. Works for team storytelling development, story mining sessions, and cross-functional narrative alignment. Runs 60-90 minutes.

## Instructions
The facilitator prepares the agenda and materials in advance. Send participants the prep assignment 3-5 days before the workshop. The workshop follows a Share-Feedback-Refine cycle. Enforce time limits strictly — they create focus. Psychological safety is essential: establish sharing rules before anyone speaks.

## Template Body

- **Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]
- **Duration:** [60 / 75 / 90 minutes]
- **Location / platform:** [ROOM or VIDEO TOOL]
- **Purpose:** [WHY — e.g., mine customer stories, practice pitches, develop brand narrative]

---

### Pre-Workshop Assignment (Send 3-5 Days Before)
> Participants, please come prepared with:
> 1. **One story** related to [THEME — e.g., "a customer moment that surprised you," "a time your product made a difference," "a failure that taught you something"]. Keep it to 2 minutes when spoken aloud.
> 2. **The story should include:** A specific moment (not a summary), a character (not a department), and a change (something was different after).
> 3. **Don't polish it.** Bring the raw version. We'll refine together.

---

### Agenda

**1. Opening and Rules (10 min)**

**Welcome script:**
> "Welcome to [WORKSHOP NAME]. Today's goal is to share stories, give each other feedback, and leave with stronger narratives. Here's how it works..."

**Sharing Rules (post visibly):**
1. **What's shared here stays here** — unless the teller gives permission to use their story.
2. **No judging, only building** — feedback is about making the story stronger, not evaluating the person.
3. **Specifics beat generics** — "I loved the detail about the sticky note" is better than "Great story."
4. **Time limits are sacred** — they help everyone get a turn.
5. **It's OK to be nervous** — that means you're about to grow.

**Quick warm-up (optional, 5 min):**
- [RUN ONE IMPROV DRILL — e.g., "Yes, And" Story Chain or One-Word Story]

---

**2. Story Sharing Round 1 (25-30 min)**

**Format:** Each participant tells their prepared story. Others listen without interrupting.

| Participant | Story Topic | Time Limit | Feedback Slot |
|-------------|-------------|------------|---------------|
| [NAME 1] | [TOPIC] | 2 min | 3 min |
| [NAME 2] | [TOPIC] | 2 min | 3 min |
| [NAME 3] | [TOPIC] | 2 min | 3 min |
| [NAME 4] | [TOPIC] | 2 min | 3 min |
| [NAME 5] | [TOPIC] | 2 min | 3 min |
| [NAME 6] | [TOPIC] | 2 min | 3 min |

**Feedback format (per story):**
After each story, the group responds using this structure:
1. **"I heard..."** — What was the core of the story? (Tests clarity)
2. **"I felt..."** — What emotion did it evoke? (Tests emotional impact)
3. **"I wanted more of..."** — Where should the teller expand or add detail?
4. **"The moment that hooked me was..."** — Identify the strongest beat.

---

**3. Reflection and Pattern Spotting (10 min)**

**Group discussion prompts:**
- What themes are emerging across our stories?
- Which stories surprised you?
- Which stories could we use for [SPECIFIC PURPOSE — sales, marketing, recruiting]?
- Where are the gaps — what stories are we missing?

**Capture:**
| Story | Teller | Potential Use | Priority |
|-------|--------|---------------|----------|
| [STORY SUMMARY] | [NAME] | [USE — case study, pitch, social, internal] | [HIGH/MED/LOW] |
| [STORY] | [NAME] | [USE] | [Priority] |
| [STORY] | [NAME] | [USE] | [Priority] |

---

**4. Refinement Round (20-25 min)**

**Format:** Pick 2-3 stories with the highest potential. Each teller retells with group coaching.

**Refinement process per story (8 min each):**
1. **Retell (2 min):** Teller retells incorporating feedback from Round 1.
2. **Group coaching (4 min):** Group helps sharpen:
   - The opening hook — how to start stronger
   - The turning point — how to make the change clearer
   - The ending — how to land it with impact
3. **Final take (2 min):** Teller tells it one more time with all refinements.

**Coaching prompts for the group:**
- "What if you started with [specific moment] instead of the background?"
- "The turning point felt like it was at [moment] — can you make that bigger?"
- "What did you feel in that moment? Can you say that out loud in the story?"
- "What's the one sentence version of this story?"

---

**5. Closing and Next Steps (5-10 min)**

**Debrief questions:**
1. What's one thing you learned about storytelling today?
2. What story (not your own) stuck with you most?
3. What will you do differently in your next presentation, pitch, or conversation?

**Action items:**
| Action | Owner | Deadline |
|--------|-------|----------|
| [Write up and polish story for [USE]] | [NAME] | [DATE] |
| [Schedule interview with customer from [STORY]] | [NAME] | [DATE] |
| [Add refined stories to story bank] | [NAME] | [DATE] |
| [Schedule next Story Swap] | [FACILITATOR] | [DATE] |

---

### Facilitator Notes
- **Room setup:** Chairs in a circle, no tables between people (reduces barriers).
- **Materials needed:** Timer (visible), whiteboard or shared doc for capture, name tents.
- **Energy management:** If energy dips, insert a quick improv drill as a reset.
- **Quiet participants:** Use the "pair share" format (2 people share with each other) before group sharing to warm up.
- **Recording:** Ask permission before recording. Recordings are for reference, not distribution.

## Example

**Workshop:** Q1 Customer Story Mining — Content + Sales teams (6 people)
**Best moment:** A sales rep told a story about a customer who almost churned, then stayed after a single conversation where the rep listened for 20 minutes without pitching. The content team immediately recognized it as the opening for the Q2 case study they'd been struggling to start.
**Outcome:** 3 stories identified for immediate use. 1 customer interview scheduled. Next Story Swap set for April 15.
