# Story Spine Exercise
> Use this exercise to quickly draft a narrative arc using Kenn Adams' Story Spine. The fill-in-the-blank structure forces a complete story with setup, escalation, climax, and resolution. Works for brainstorming, workshops, improv, and rapid story prototyping.

## Instructions
Fill in each blank sequentially without overthinking. The first pass should take under 5 minutes. The structure itself does the heavy lifting — trust the prompts. After completing, read aloud and refine. Use this to prototype stories before investing in full drafts.

## Template Body

### Exercise Metadata
- **Participant:** [NAME]
- **Story subject:** [WHAT THIS STORY IS ABOUT — customer, product, idea, brand]
- **Purpose:** [Brainstorm / Workshop / Prototype / Warm-up]
- **Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]

---

### The Story Spine

**Once upon a time...** [ESTABLISH THE WORLD — who, where, when, and what was "normal"]
> [YOUR ANSWER]

**Every day...** [DESCRIBE THE ROUTINE — what life was like before the change]
> [YOUR ANSWER]

**But one day...** [THE INCITING INCIDENT — what disrupted the routine]
> [YOUR ANSWER]

**Because of that...** [FIRST CONSEQUENCE — what happened as a result]
> [YOUR ANSWER]

**Because of that...** [SECOND CONSEQUENCE — escalation, things get harder or more complex]
> [YOUR ANSWER]

**Because of that...** [THIRD CONSEQUENCE — the stakes reach their peak]
> [YOUR ANSWER]

**Until finally...** [THE CLIMAX — the decisive moment, the turning point]
> [YOUR ANSWER]

**And ever since then...** [THE NEW NORMAL — how the world is different now]
> [YOUR ANSWER]

**And the moral is...** [THE LESSON — what this story teaches or proves]
> [YOUR ANSWER]

---

### Refinement

**Read your Story Spine aloud. Then answer:**

1. **Is the "But one day" moment surprising enough?** [YES / NO — if no, what would make it more disruptive?]
2. **Do the "Because of that" beats escalate?** [YES / NO — each should be harder/more complex than the last]
3. **Is "Until finally" a real turning point?** [YES / NO — it should feel earned, not convenient]
4. **Does "Ever since then" show genuine change?** [YES / NO — the world should be different from "Once upon a time"]
5. **Is the moral earned by the story?** [YES / NO — it should emerge naturally, not feel tacked on]

---

### Variations

**Business Story Spine:**
- Once upon a time... [COMPANY/TEAM was in THIS situation]
- Every day... [THEY DID THIS — the old way]
- But one day... [THIS HAPPENED — market shift, customer complaint, insight]
- Because of that... [THEY TRIED THIS]
- Because of that... [IT LED TO THIS]
- Because of that... [THINGS ESCALATED TO THIS]
- Until finally... [THEY ACHIEVED/DISCOVERED THIS]
- Ever since then... [THE NEW APPROACH/RESULT]

**Customer Story Spine:**
- Once upon a time... [CUSTOMER had THIS problem]
- Every day... [THEY DEALT WITH IT by doing THIS]
- But one day... [SOMETHING MADE IT WORSE / THEY FOUND US]
- Because of that... [THEY TRIED OUR SOLUTION]
- Because of that... [THEY SAW EARLY RESULTS]
- Because of that... [THEY WENT ALL IN]
- Until finally... [THE TRANSFORMATION — metric, outcome]
- Ever since then... [THEIR NEW REALITY]

## Example

**Once upon a time,** Maria ran student success at BrightPath Education, serving 50,000 students.

**Every day,** she spent Monday mornings wrestling three spreadsheets to find at-risk students.

**But one day,** student churn hit 22%, and the CEO said: "Fix this or we cut the program."

**Because of that,** Maria started searching for a better way and found a predictive analytics platform.

**Because of that,** she ran a trial and discovered 40 at-risk students her spreadsheets had missed.

**Because of that,** the team redesigned their highest-churn course module in one week.

**Until finally,** six months later, churn dropped from 22% to 11%, saving an estimated $600K.

**And ever since then,** Maria's team makes every decision with real-time data, not Monday morning guesswork.

**And the moral is:** The right tool doesn't just save time — it changes what's possible.
