# StoryBrand Landing Pages: Swipe Analysis

## Purpose
Landing pages that effectively use Donald Miller's StoryBrand framework to guide
the visitor through a narrative that positions them as the hero and the brand as
the guide.

---

## The StoryBrand Landing Page Formula

### Section Sequence
1. **Header:** Clear one-liner (problem + solution + result)
2. **Stakes:** What's at risk if the visitor doesn't act
3. **Value proposition:** Three benefits (not features)
4. **Guide introduction:** Empathy + Authority
5. **The plan:** Three clear steps
6. **Explanatory paragraph:** The fuller story
7. **CTA (direct):** Primary action
8. **CTA (transitional):** Lower-commitment action
9. **Junk drawer:** FAQ, details, fine print

## Example 1: SaaS Project Management Tool

### Header Section
**Headline:** "Stop losing projects to chaos."
**Subhead:** "One platform to plan, track, and deliver — so your team can stop firefighting and start building."
**CTA Button:** "Start Free Trial"
**Transitional CTA:** "Watch a 2-Minute Demo"

### Stakes Section
> "Every missed deadline costs more than time. It costs trust — with your clients,
> your team, and yourself. Last year, project delays cost mid-size companies an
> average of $98,000 in lost revenue and rework."

### Three Benefits
1. **See everything in one place.** No more toggling between five tools to get the full picture.
2. **Know what's behind schedule before it's a crisis.** Automated alerts flag at-risk tasks before they slip.
3. **Deliver on time and keep your promises.** Teams using [Product] deliver 34% more projects on deadline.

### Guide Section
**Empathy:** "We know what it's like to dread the Monday morning status meeting — wondering what fell through the cracks over the weekend."
**Authority:** "We've helped 2,400+ teams deliver over 50,000 projects on time. Our framework is based on 12 years of project delivery research."

### The Plan
1. **Start a free trial** — Set up your first project in under 10 minutes.
2. **Import your team** — Everyone sees their tasks, deadlines, and dependencies.
3. **Deliver with confidence** — Watch project visibility replace project anxiety.

## StoryBrand Landing Page Checklist

1. **Can the visitor understand what you offer within 5 seconds?**
2. **Is the customer the hero?** (Not the brand.)
3. **Is there a clear problem named?** (External, internal, and philosophical.)
4. **Is the guide positioned with both empathy and authority?**
5. **Is there a simple plan (3 steps)?**
6. **Is there a clear direct CTA?**
7. **Is there a transitional CTA for people not ready to commit?**
8. **Are stakes articulated?** (What does the visitor lose by not acting?)
9. **Is success painted vividly?** (What does life look like after?)
10. **Is the page free of jargon and unnecessary complexity?**

---

## Common Mistakes
- **The brand is the hero.** ("We're the leading…" "Our award-winning…") Make the customer the protagonist.
- **No clear plan.** Visitors need to see the path before they'll walk it.
- **Multiple CTAs competing.** One primary CTA. One transitional. That's it.
- **Stakes are missing.** Without stakes, there's no reason to act now.
- **Too much text.** A StoryBrand page should be scannable in 30 seconds.
