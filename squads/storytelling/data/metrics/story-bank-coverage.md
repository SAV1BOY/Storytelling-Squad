# Story Bank Coverage Analysis
> Tracking coverage gaps across audience segments, journey stages, channels, and story types

## Purpose
This document maps what the Story Bank has against what the organization needs. A well-stocked
Story Bank means every team has the right story for every situation. Gaps in coverage mean
teams default to generic messaging or wing it. Updated quarterly after the narrative audit.

## Critical Gaps (Priority Order)

1. **Customer stories for video and ad channels** — Zero customer stories adapted for video or ad formats. These are high-impact channels with no narrative assets.
   - Action: Adapt top 2 customer stories (STY-2026-001, STY-2026-005) for 60s video and paid social ad formats.
   - Owner: Story Writer + Ops
   - Target: End of Q2 2026

2. **Onboarding stories for customers and employees** — No stories designed for the onboarding journey. New customers and employees receive procedural content only.
   - Action: Create 2 customer onboarding stories and 2 employee onboarding stories.
   - Owner: Story Strategist
   - Target: Mid Q2 2026

3. **Retention/renewal stories** — No stories for the retention stage. Customer success team has no narrative assets for renewal conversations.
   - Action: Create 3 customer success stories focused on long-term value and growth.
   - Owner: Story Writer + CS team
   - Target: End of Q2 2026

4. **Culture stories beyond the outage narrative** — Only 1 culture story in the bank. Need diversity of culture narratives: values, team moments, decisions.
   - Action: Run 3 internal story discovery sessions to capture culture moments.
   - Owner: Story Strategist
   - Target: Ongoing through Q2 2026

5. **Non-healthcare vertical stories** — Zero stories from logistics or retail verticals. Pipeline expansion is planned for Q2.
   - Action: Identify 2 logistics customer candidates and begin case study extraction.
   - Owner: Story Ops + Sales
   - Target: End of Q2 2026
