# Content Narrative Metrics
> Tracking engagement, shares, and narrative effectiveness across content channels

## Purpose
This document measures how narrative-driven content performs compared to informational content
across all publishing channels. It tracks whether storytelling techniques actually improve
content outcomes and identifies which narrative approaches work best per channel.

## Key Metrics Definitions
- **Narrative Engagement Rate**: Engagement on story-driven content vs. informational content
- **Share Multiplier**: How many times more likely narrative content is to be shared
- **Completion Rate**: Percentage of audience that finishes the content
- **Comment Quality Score**: Depth and relevance of audience comments (1-10, editorial rated)
- **Content-to-Pipeline**: Content pieces that generate attributed pipeline within 30 days

## Measurement Notes
- Engagement rate = (clicks + comments + shares + saves) / impressions
- Content-to-pipeline attribution: 30-day window, first-touch and multi-touch models averaged
- Comment quality scored by Story Editor on 1-10 rubric monthly
- Social metrics pulled from native platform analytics
- Email metrics from marketing automation platform
