# Deck Engagement Metrics
> Tracking attention by section, drop-off patterns, and narrative effectiveness in presentations

## Purpose
This document measures how presentation audiences engage with story-driven decks. It tracks
where attention peaks, where it drops, which sections drive questions, and which sections
correlate with positive outcomes. Updated after each significant presentation.

## Key Metrics Definitions
- **Section Attention Score**: Facilitator-rated audience engagement per section (1-10)
- **Drop-Off Points**: Sections where audience visibly disengages (phone checks, side conversations)
- **Question Triggers**: Which sections generate the most audience questions
- **Win Correlation**: Which sections are most present in pitches that achieve their objective

## Cross-Deck Patterns

### Sections That Consistently Perform Well
1. **Customer-specific opening** (personalized "Their World"): 8.2 avg attention
2. **Customer story with metrics**: 8.3 avg attention
3. **Vision/what-could-be slides**: 8.5 avg attention
4. **Live demo moments**: 7.8 avg attention (when kept under 90 seconds)

### Sections That Consistently Underperform
1. **Company credentials/about-us**: 6.4 avg attention — reframe as Guide, not resume
2. **Product architecture diagrams**: 6.1 avg attention — too technical for most audiences
3. **Market size slides**: 6.7 avg attention — expected, not engaging
4. **Team slides**: 6.9 avg attention — needs narrative treatment, not headshot grid

## Improvement Actions
- Redesign the Guide section in DK-2026-002 to lead with customer quote, not company credential
- Replace product architecture diagram with a patient journey visualization
- Add a "surprise proof" slide after the market size section to re-engage
- Test removing the team headshot grid and instead weaving team credibility into the origin story

## Measurement Notes
- Attention scores are facilitator-observed (1-10) during live presentations
- Drop-off rate based on observable disengagement behaviors
- Question rate categorized as Low (0-1), Medium (2-3), High (4+) per section
- Win correlation requires minimum 10 presentations — DK-2026-002 data is directional
