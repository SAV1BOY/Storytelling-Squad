# Channel Performance Metrics

Per-channel story performance metrics for comparing narrative effectiveness across distribution channels.

## Purpose

The same story performs differently depending on where it is published. Channel performance metrics enable the Storytelling Squad to understand which channels amplify which story types, optimize format and length for each channel, and allocate distribution effort where it delivers the most impact.

## Channels Tracked

| Channel | Primary Metric | Secondary Metrics |
|---|---|---|
| Blog | Time on page | Scroll depth, shares, organic traffic, conversion rate |
| Email | Click-through rate | Open rate, reply rate, unsubscribe rate |
| Social (LinkedIn) | Engagement rate | Impressions, shares, comments, saves, follower growth |
| Social (X) | Engagement rate | Impressions, retweets, replies, link clicks |
| Landing page | Conversion rate | Bounce rate, time on page, scroll depth |
| Sales deck | Win rate influence | Deal velocity, usage by sales reps |
| Ad (paid) | Click-through rate | Cost per click, conversion rate, ROAS |
| Video | Completion rate | Views, average watch time, click-through |
| Podcast | Listen-through rate | Downloads, average listen duration |
| Webinar | Attendance rate | Registration-to-attendance ratio, engagement score, pipeline generated |

## Metric Definitions

### 1. Channel Engagement Index

- **Definition**: Normalized engagement score per channel, enabling cross-channel comparison.
- **Formula**: Channel-specific engagement metrics normalized to a 0-10 scale using channel benchmarks.
- **Granularity**: Per channel, per story.
- **Update frequency**: Weekly.

### 2. Channel Conversion Rate

- **Definition**: Story-attributed conversion rate specific to each channel.
- **Formula**: `(conversions from story on channel / impressions on channel) * 100`
- **Granularity**: Per channel, per story, per audience segment.
- **Target**: Varies by channel (see benchmarks below).
- **Update frequency**: Weekly.

### 3. Channel Fit Score

- **Definition**: How well a story's format, length, and tone match the channel's requirements.
- **Measurement**: Automated check against Voice Calibration Registry channel defaults.
- **Scoring**: 0-10 based on compliance with channel guidelines.
- **Update frequency**: At publication time.

### 4. Cross-Channel Lift

- **Definition**: Additional engagement or conversion observed when a story is present on multiple channels simultaneously.
- **Formula**: `(multi-channel performance - single-channel performance) / single-channel performance * 100`
- **Granularity**: Per story, per channel combination.
- **Update frequency**: Monthly.

### 5. Channel ROI

- **Definition**: Return on investment for story distribution on each channel.
- **Formula**: `(revenue attributed to story on channel - cost of distribution) / cost of distribution`
- **Granularity**: Per channel.
- **Update frequency**: Monthly.

## Channel Benchmarks

| Channel | Engagement Benchmark | Conversion Benchmark | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blog | 4+ min time on page | 2-4% CTA click | Long-form stories perform best |
| Email | 25-35% open, 3-6% CTR | 1-3% conversion | Subject line (hook) is critical |
| LinkedIn | 2-4% engagement rate | 0.5-1% link CTR | Native text posts outperform links |
| X | 1-3% engagement rate | 0.3-0.8% link CTR | Thread format for longer stories |
| Landing page | 60%+ scroll depth | 5-10% form fill | Proof-heavy stories convert best |
| Sales deck | 70%+ rep adoption | Track deal influence | Must be easy to customize |
| Paid ad | 1-2% CTR | Varies by objective | Hook quality determines performance |
| Video | 40%+ completion (2 min) | Track CTA clicks | First 5 seconds are decisive |

## Notes

- Channel performance should inform repurposing decisions. A story that performs well on blog but poorly in email may need a different hook or format for email.
- Cross-channel lift data helps justify multi-channel distribution of high-performing stories.
- Channel benchmarks should be recalibrated quarterly as platform algorithms and audience behavior shift.
- Sales deck metrics are the hardest to track. Rely on CRM integration and periodic sales team surveys.
