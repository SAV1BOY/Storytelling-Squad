# Pitch Conversion Metrics
> Tracking win rates, objection patterns, timing, and narrative element attribution for pitches

## Purpose
This document tracks pitch effectiveness with focus on how narrative elements correlate with
conversion outcomes. It helps identify which story structures, proof points, and delivery
techniques drive results. Updated after every pitch delivery with full debrief data.

## Key Metrics Definitions
- **Win Rate**: Percentage of pitches that achieve their stated objective
- **Objection Rate**: Average number of objections raised per pitch
- **Recovery Score**: How effectively objections are handled narratively (1-10)
- **Time-to-Decision**: Average days from pitch delivery to decision
- **Hook Effectiveness**: Percentage of audiences visibly engaged within first 15 seconds

|---------|-------------------|-------|
| Demo over 3 minutes | 68% | Audience disengages after 90 seconds |
| No customer story | 55% | Claims without proof fall flat |
| Generic CTA ("let's talk") | 72% | Vague next steps kill momentum |
| Defensive objection response | 48% | Story-based responses outperform 3:1 |

|---------------|-------------|------------|----------|--------|
| Hook/Opening | 10% | 8% | -2% | Underspent — hooks are rushed |
| Problem Setup | 20% | 25% | +5% | Overspent — dwelling too long on pain |
| Solution/Demo | 25% | 32% | +7% | Significantly overspent — demo bloat |
| Proof/Evidence | 20% | 15% | -5% | Underspent — need more proof time |
| CTA/Close | 15% | 12% | -3% | Rushed closing — practice needed |

## Improvement Actions
1. Preempt the "different from existing tools" objection within the pitch narrative (Step 2 of pitch)
2. Cut demo time to maximum 90 seconds — rehearse this specifically
3. Expand proof section with a second customer reference (currently only Meridian)
4. Add a "trust bridge" story for the startup credibility objection — the outage story works
5. Script a stronger, more specific CTA with an exact next step and date

## Measurement Notes
- Win rate = pitches achieving stated objective / total pitches delivered
- Recovery score = presenter self-assessment + facilitator observation average
- Hook effectiveness = facilitator observation of audience engagement in first 15 seconds
- Time-to-decision starts at pitch delivery, ends at yes/no (excludes ghosted prospects)
