# Story Performance Metrics
> Tracking retention, CTA lift, and copy lift across all published stories

## Purpose
This document tracks the performance of individual stories and story types across channels.
Updated monthly with data from analytics, CRM, and content platforms. Use this to identify
what's working, what's underperforming, and where to invest storytelling resources.

## Key Metrics Definitions
- **Retention Rate**: Percentage of audience that consumed 75%+ of the story content
- **CTA Lift**: Conversion rate improvement when a story is present vs. absent
- **Copy Lift**: Engagement improvement when narrative copy replaces informational copy
- **Share Rate**: Percentage of viewers who shared the content organically
- **Time on Content**: Average time spent engaging with the story

## Key Findings This Period
1. Culture and transparency stories generate 2x the engagement of customer stories
2. Customer stories drive higher conversion despite lower engagement
3. Specific detail density correlates with retention (r=0.72 across sample)
4. Stories using mid-scene hooks retain 23% more readers than question-hook stories

## Measurement Notes
- Engagement rate = (clicks + comments + shares) / impressions
- Conversion rate = attributed conversions / total story viewers
- Attribution window: 7-day post-view, 1-day post-click
- Sample sizes are small in Q1; treat trends as directional, not definitive
