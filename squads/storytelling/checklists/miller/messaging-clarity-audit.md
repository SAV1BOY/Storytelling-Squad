# Messaging Clarity Audit
> Quality gate for verifying the message is understood in 5 seconds, free of jargon, with benefit clearly stated

## Purpose
Ensure all brand messaging passes the clarity threshold: a first-time visitor or listener can understand what is offered, how it helps them, and what to do next within five seconds of exposure. Confusion is the enemy of conversion.

## Prerequisites
- Primary messaging assets identified (website header, email subject, ad copy, pitch opening)
- Target audience persona documented
- StoryBrand BrandScript or equivalent messaging framework completed

## Checklist

### Five-Second Comprehension
- [ ] **Header Passes the 5-Second Test** — A stranger reading the website header or opening line can answer "What does this company do?" within five seconds
- [ ] **Benefit Is Immediate** — The primary benefit to the customer is communicated in the first sentence, not buried in supporting copy
- [ ] **No Decoding Required** — The message does not require the reader to interpret, infer, or click further to understand the core offering
- [ ] **Visual Hierarchy Supports Clarity** — If visual, the most important message is the largest, most prominent element; the eye goes there first

### Jargon Elimination
- [ ] **No Industry Jargon** — Technical terms, acronyms, and insider language have been replaced with plain language
- [ ] **No Corporate Speak** — Phrases like "synergy," "leverage," "holistic solution," or "paradigm shift" are absent
- [ ] **No Assumed Knowledge** — The message does not assume the reader already knows what the product category is or why it matters
- [ ] **Grandparent Test** — A non-expert adult could read the message and explain the offering to someone else

### Benefit Clarity
- [ ] **Customer Outcome Stated** — The message tells the customer what their life looks like after using the product/service, not just what the product does
- [ ] **Features Translated to Benefits** — Every feature mentioned is connected to a specific customer benefit; no orphan features
- [ ] **Single Primary Message** — The communication leads with one clear message, not three competing ideas; supporting points are subordinate
- [ ] **Emotional and Functional Benefit Present** — Both how the customer feels and what they practically gain are addressed

### Action Clarity
- [ ] **Next Step Obvious** — The reader knows exactly what to do after consuming the message (click, call, sign up, visit)
- [ ] **No Friction in Action Path** — The call to action is visible, accessible, and requires minimal steps to execute
- [ ] **Coherence Across Touchpoints** — The same clear message is consistent across website, email, social, and sales materials

## Scoring
| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Pass | All 15 items checked; the message is instantly clear and actionable |
| Conditional | 11-14 items checked; clarity is close but jargon or competing messages remain |
| Fail | Fewer than 11 items, or the 5-second test fails |

## Next Steps
- Run the 5-second test with five people outside the organization using usabilityhub.com or equivalent
- Replace every piece of jargon with plain language and A/B test conversion rates
- If multiple messages compete, choose one and move the rest to supporting sections
