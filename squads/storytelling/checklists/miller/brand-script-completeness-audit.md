# Brand Script Completeness Audit
> Quality gate for verifying all 7 SB7 elements are filled, congruent with each other, and actionable

## Purpose
Ensure the completed BrandScript is not just filled in but internally consistent, customer-centered, and directly translatable into marketing assets. A completed but incongruent BrandScript produces confused messaging.

## Prerequisites
- StoryBrand BrandScript worksheet filled out
- SB7 Audit completed
- Target customer persona and competitive positioning documented

## Checklist

### Completeness
- [ ] **All Seven Fields Populated** — Character, Problem (3 levels), Guide (empathy + authority), Plan, CTA, Failure, and Success sections are all filled in with specific content
- [ ] **No Placeholder Language** — Every field contains final, usable language, not notes like "TBD" or "something about trust"
- [ ] **Sufficient Detail** — Each field has enough specificity to directly generate marketing copy without additional interpretation

### Congruence
- [ ] **Character and Problem Aligned** — The character's defined want directly connects to the stated problem; they are not describing different people or situations
- [ ] **Guide Addresses the Problem** — The empathy statement reflects understanding of the specific problem identified, not generic compassion
- [ ] **Plan Solves the Problem** — Each step in the plan directly advances the customer from the problem state toward the success state
- [ ] **Failure Is the Problem Unchecked** — The failure scenario is a believable consequence of the stated problem remaining unsolved
- [ ] **Success Resolves All Three Problem Levels** — The success outcome addresses the external, internal, and philosophical problems, not just one
- [ ] **CTA Fits the Plan** — The call to action is the logical first step of the plan, not a disconnected request

### Actionability
- [ ] **Website Copy Derivable** — The BrandScript can generate a complete website wireframe (header, stakes, value prop, plan, CTA, testimonials) without additional strategic work
- [ ] **Email Sequences Derivable** — The BrandScript provides enough material for a nurture email series that guides the customer through the SB7 journey
- [ ] **Sales Script Derivable** — A salesperson could use the BrandScript to structure a conversation: identify problem, position as guide, present plan, call to action
- [ ] **Elevator Pitch Derivable** — The one-liner and elevator pitch can be pulled directly from the BrandScript fields

### Quality of Content
- [ ] **Customer Language Used** — The BrandScript uses words and phrases the customer actually says, not internal brand language
- [ ] **Differentiation Present** — The BrandScript clearly distinguishes this brand from competitors; swapping in a competitor's name would not work
- [ ] **Emotional Truth Present** — The internal problem and philosophical problem tap into genuine human emotions, not manufactured urgency

## Scoring
| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Pass | All 16 items checked; the BrandScript is complete, congruent, and ready for deployment |
| Conditional | 12-15 items checked; BrandScript is complete but congruence gaps exist between fields |
| Fail | Fewer than 12 items, or fields are incomplete or internally contradictory |

## Next Steps
- Read the BrandScript aloud from Character through Success as a continuous narrative; note where it breaks
- Have three team members independently generate a website header from the BrandScript; if they produce very different results, the BrandScript lacks clarity
- Update the BrandScript quarterly as customer language and market positioning evolve
