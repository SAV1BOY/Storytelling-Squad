# StoryBrand SB7 Audit
> Quality gate for verifying all seven StoryBrand framework elements are present and functional

## Purpose
Ensure the brand narrative follows Donald Miller's SB7 framework: a Character with a Problem meets a Guide who gives them a Plan and calls them to Action that helps them avoid Failure and achieve Success. Every element must be clear, specific, and customer-focused.

## Prerequisites
- Target customer persona documented
- Brand positioning established
- Complete marketing narrative, website copy, or brand script drafted

## Checklist

### 1. Character (The Customer)
- [ ] **Character Defined** — The customer is the hero, not the brand; the narrative centers on their desire, not the company's achievements
- [ ] **Want Is Specific** — The character wants something concrete and identifiable; the desire is not vague or abstract

### 2. Problem (Three Levels)
- [ ] **External Problem Stated** — A tangible, visible problem the customer faces is clearly named (e.g., "leaky roof," "declining revenue")
- [ ] **Internal Problem Identified** — The frustration, self-doubt, or emotional struggle the external problem causes is articulated (e.g., "feeling incompetent," "overwhelmed")
- [ ] **Philosophical Problem Framed** — A "should" statement captures the injustice (e.g., "Nobody should have to struggle with X just to achieve Y")

### 3. Guide (The Brand)
- [ ] **Empathy Expressed** — The brand demonstrates understanding of the customer's pain before offering solutions; "We understand what it's like to..."
- [ ] **Authority Demonstrated** — The brand shows competence through credentials, results, testimonials, or experience without bragging

### 4. Plan
- [ ] **Simple Plan Provided** — A 3-4 step process is laid out that makes the customer's path clear and achievable
- [ ] **Plan Reduces Confusion** — Each step is concrete enough that the customer knows exactly what to do next
- [ ] **Agreement Plan or Process Plan Present** — Either a process plan (steps to buy/engage) or an agreement plan (promises that address fears) is documented

### 5. Call to Action
- [ ] **Direct CTA Present** — A clear, unambiguous primary call to action exists ("Buy Now," "Schedule a Call," "Start Free Trial")
- [ ] **Transitional CTA Available** — A lower-commitment option exists for those not ready for the direct CTA (lead magnet, free resource, newsletter)

### 6. Failure (Stakes)
- [ ] **Failure Painted Clearly** — The narrative articulates what happens if the customer does nothing; the negative consequences are specific and real
- [ ] **Failure Creates Urgency** — The cost of inaction is vivid enough to motivate action without being manipulative or fear-mongering

### 7. Success
- [ ] **Success Described Vividly** — The positive outcome is painted in concrete, aspirational terms the customer can visualize and desire
- [ ] **Transformation Shown** — The narrative shows who the customer becomes, not just what they get; identity transformation is more powerful than feature delivery
- [ ] **Success Addresses All Three Problem Levels** — The success resolves the external, internal, and philosophical problems identified earlier

### Integration
- [ ] **All Seven Elements Present** — No element of the SB7 is missing; each is identifiable in the narrative
- [ ] **Customer Is Always the Hero** — At no point does the brand position itself as the hero; it remains the guide throughout
- [ ] **Clarity Over Cleverness** — The messaging is clear enough that a distracted reader grasps the value in a single pass

## Scoring
| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Pass | All 19 items checked; the SB7 is complete, clear, and customer-centered |
| Conditional | 15-18 items checked; framework is present but some elements need sharpening |
| Fail | Fewer than 15 items, or core elements (Character, Problem, Guide) missing |

## Next Steps
- Fill out the official StoryBrand BrandScript worksheet if not yet done
- Test the narrative on five people outside the company; ask them to restate the offer in their own words
- If the brand is accidentally the hero, rewrite every "we" statement as a "you" statement
