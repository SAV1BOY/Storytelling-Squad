# Social Media Story Quality Checklist
> Quality gate for validating that social media content tells a complete micro-story native to the platform

## Purpose
Gates the narrative quality of social media posts, threads, carousels, reels, and stories. Use before publishing any social content that aims to do more than broadcast information. Ensures the content is a complete micro-narrative with a thumb-stopping opening, platform-native structure, and shareability, not just a truncated version of longer content.

## Prerequisites
- A draft social media post, thread, carousel, or short-form video script exists
- The target platform is specified (LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, etc.)
- The audience segment for this platform is understood
- The content objective (engagement, traffic, conversion, brand awareness) is defined
- Platform-specific constraints (character limits, aspect ratios, duration) are known

## Checklist

### Micro-Story Completeness
- [ ] **Complete narrative in miniature** — The post contains a beginning (setup/context), middle (tension/insight), and end (resolution/takeaway), however compressed
- [ ] **Single point delivered** — The post communicates one idea, one lesson, or one moment; it does not try to compress an essay into a post
- [ ] **Transformation or change present** — Something shifts between the start and end of the post: a belief, a situation, a piece of understanding
- [ ] **Takeaway is clear** — The reader finishes knowing exactly what they were supposed to learn, feel, or do; no "what was the point?" reaction
- [ ] **Story is self-contained** — The post does not require clicking a link, reading a previous post, or having insider context to make sense

### Thumb-Stopping Opening
- [ ] **First line hooks** — The opening line (or first frame of video) stops the scroll with a bold claim, surprising fact, emotional statement, or provocative question
- [ ] **First line is not generic** — The opening avoids "I've been thinking about..." "Hot take:" "Unpopular opinion:" and other formulaic openers unless genuinely used in a fresh way
- [ ] **Visual hook present (if applicable)** — For visual platforms, the first image, frame, or graphic is arresting enough to pause a fast-scrolling thumb
- [ ] **Opening creates an information gap** — The first 1-2 lines introduce enough tension or curiosity that the reader expands the post or keeps watching

### Platform-Native Design
- [ ] **Format matches platform conventions** — The content uses the platform's native formats: threads for X, carousels for LinkedIn/Instagram, duets for TikTok, etc.
- [ ] **Length is platform-appropriate** — The content respects platform norms: LinkedIn posts under 1300 characters for feed visibility, tweets within limits, videos at optimal platform duration
- [ ] **Tone matches platform culture** — The voice feels natural on the platform: professional on LinkedIn, conversational on X, visual-first on Instagram, raw on TikTok
- [ ] **Engagement mechanic included** — The post includes a natural prompt for interaction: a question, a "tag someone who," a poll, or a "save this for later" worthy list

### Shareability and Virality
- [ ] **Post passes the "screenshot test"** — The content is valuable or resonant enough that someone would screenshot it and share it in a DM or group chat
- [ ] **Identity signal present** — Sharing or liking the post would say something positive about the sharer's identity, values, or expertise
- [ ] **Emotional trigger present** — The post evokes a specific emotion that drives sharing: inspiration, outrage, recognition ("this is so me"), humor, or awe
- [ ] **Post works without the caption (visual platforms)** — For Instagram, TikTok, and visual-first platforms, the visual content tells the story even if the caption is not read

### Brand and Strategy Alignment
- [ ] **Voice is consistent with brand** — The post sounds like the same person or brand as other content in the feed; no jarring tone shifts
- [ ] **Post serves the content strategy** — The post fits within a larger content plan; it is not random or reactive without purpose
- [ ] **CTA is natural, not forced** — If a call to action exists, it flows from the content rather than being bolted on ("If this resonated, you'll love our newsletter" vs. "CLICK THE LINK IN BIO")

## Scoring
Must pass **15/20 minimum**. The following items are CRITICAL and must all pass:
- Complete narrative in miniature
- First line hooks
- Format matches platform conventions
- Post passes the "screenshot test"

Any CRITICAL failure requires revision before publishing.

## Next Steps
- **On Pass:** Schedule the post for optimal timing. Prepare response strategy for comments and engagement.
- **On Fail:** Rewrite from the audience's perspective: what would make THEM stop, read, feel, and share? Reference the Hook and Open Loop checklist (hook-and-open-loop-quality.md) for opening fixes. Study the top 5 performing posts in the same niche on the same platform and identify structural patterns. If the content was adapted from a longer piece, start over as a native social post rather than compressing the original.
