# Public Narrative Quality Checklist
> Quality gate for validating that a public narrative mobilizes action through the Self/Us/Now framework

## Purpose
Gates the effectiveness of public narratives designed to inspire collective action: campaign speeches, movement messaging, organizational rallying cries, nonprofit appeals, community calls to action, or social change communications. Based on Marshall Ganz's public narrative framework. Use when the goal is to move a group to act together around shared values.

## Prerequisites
- A draft public narrative exists (speech, letter, video script, or campaign message)
- The speaker/narrator's identity and credibility are established
- The audience community is identified
- A specific collective action is defined
- The values driving the narrative are articulated

## Checklist

### Story of Self
- [ ] **Personal origin moment present** — The narrator shares a specific moment from their own life that explains why they care about this cause
- [ ] **Values revealed through experience** — The story of self demonstrates the narrator's core values through a lived experience, not through abstract declarations
- [ ] **Choice point highlighted** — The personal story includes a moment where the narrator made a significant choice that aligned with their values
- [ ] **Emotional authenticity present** — The personal narrative conveys genuine emotion (hope, anger, love, determination) without manipulation
- [ ] **Self story is brief and purposeful** — The personal narrative takes no more than 20% of the total content; it serves as a bridge to the collective story

### Story of Us
- [ ] **Shared identity established** — The narrative names and defines the "us": who this community is and what binds them together
- [ ] **Shared values articulated** — At least one core value that the community holds is named and demonstrated through a collective experience or history
- [ ] **Shared challenge identified** — The narrative describes a threat, injustice, or opportunity that affects the entire community, not just individuals
- [ ] **Community stories included** — The narrative references specific people, events, or traditions from the community's shared experience
- [ ] **Belonging is inclusive** — The "us" is defined in a way that invites the audience in rather than creating exclusive in-group dynamics

### Story of Now
- [ ] **Urgent choice framed** — The narrative presents a specific, time-bound moment of decision: the audience must choose to act or accept the consequence of inaction
- [ ] **Action is specific and achievable** — The call to action names a concrete behavior the audience can take today or this week, not a vague aspiration
- [ ] **Cost of inaction is real** — The narrative articulates what will happen if the community does not act, with specific and credible consequences
- [ ] **Hope is grounded** — The narrative demonstrates that success is possible by pointing to evidence, precedent, or early momentum, not just optimism
- [ ] **Moral present** — The narrative concludes with a clear moral imperative: this is what we must do because of who we are and what we believe

### Integration and Delivery
- [ ] **Self-Us-Now flow is seamless** — The transition from personal to collective to urgent feels natural, not like three separate speeches stitched together
- [ ] **Emotional arc builds** — The emotional intensity increases from self (warmth, vulnerability) to us (solidarity, pride) to now (urgency, determination)
- [ ] **Audience is activated, not just informed** — The narrative is designed to produce immediate action (signing, showing up, donating, calling) not just agreement

## Scoring
Must pass **14/18 minimum**. The following items are CRITICAL and must all pass:
- Personal origin moment present
- Shared values articulated
- Action is specific and achievable
- Self-Us-Now flow is seamless

Any CRITICAL failure requires narrative revision.

## Next Steps
- **On Pass:** Move to delivery preparation and distribution. Rehearse for authenticity, not polish.
- **On Fail:** Revise using the Ganz public narrative structure (squads/storytelling/checklists/ganz/). If the Story of Self is weak, interview the narrator for a deeper personal connection. If the Story of Us is thin, research the community's shared history. If the Story of Now lacks urgency, find the genuine deadline or tipping point. Consult the Public Narrative Agent for structural guidance.
