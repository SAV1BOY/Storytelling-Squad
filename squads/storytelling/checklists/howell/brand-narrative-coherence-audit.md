# Brand Narrative Coherence Audit
> Quality gate for verifying the narrative is consistent across channels and aligned with brand positioning

## Purpose
Ensure the brand story maintains its core identity, message, and emotional tone across every channel and touchpoint. Incoherent narratives fragment the brand, confuse audiences, and dilute trust.

## Prerequisites
- Brand positioning document (mission, vision, values, personality, positioning statement)
- Inventory of all active channels (website, social media, email, sales decks, PR, advertising)
- Sample content from each channel collected for review

## Checklist

### Core Narrative Consistency
- [ ] **Origin Story Consistent** — The brand's founding story or origin narrative is told the same way (facts, sequence, emotional tone) across all channels
- [ ] **Value Proposition Unified** — The core promise to the customer is the same everywhere, even if expressed in channel-appropriate language
- [ ] **Hero/Guide Roles Consistent** — The brand is always the guide and the customer always the hero; no channel accidentally positions the brand as the hero
- [ ] **Tone of Voice Stable** — The brand's personality (formal/casual, serious/playful, authoritative/approachable) is recognizable across channels

### Channel Alignment
- [ ] **Adapted Not Copied** — Content is adapted for each channel's format and audience expectations, not duplicated verbatim across platforms
- [ ] **No Contradictory Messages** — No channel communicates a benefit, claim, or positioning that contradicts another channel
- [ ] **Visual Identity Aligned** — Visual storytelling (imagery, color, typography) reinforces the same narrative across digital, print, and in-person
- [ ] **Messaging Hierarchy Preserved** — The primary message leads in every channel; secondary messages do not accidentally become primary on any platform

### Positioning Alignment
- [ ] **Competitive Positioning Maintained** — The narrative consistently positions the brand in the same competitive space; it does not claim different positions on different channels
- [ ] **Target Audience Consistent** — The narrative speaks to the same core audience persona across channels, with channel-specific adjustments in style but not in who is addressed
- [ ] **Brand Values Demonstrated** — Stated brand values are not just listed but demonstrated through stories, examples, and proof on every channel

### Temporal Coherence
- [ ] **Past, Present, and Future Aligned** — The brand's narrative about where it came from, where it is, and where it is going forms a coherent timeline
- [ ] **Campaign Narratives Connect to Master Narrative** — Individual campaigns extend or illustrate the core brand story, not introduce disconnected sub-brands
- [ ] **No Abandoned Narratives** — Previous brand stories or commitments are either continued or deliberately and transparently retired, not quietly dropped

## Scoring
| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Pass | All 14 items checked; the brand tells one coherent story everywhere |
| Conditional | 10-13 items checked; mostly coherent but 1-2 channels drift from the core narrative |
| Fail | Fewer than 10 items, or channels actively contradict each other |

## Next Steps
- Create a brand narrative one-pager that serves as the single source of truth for all channels
- Audit each channel quarterly by comparing its current messaging to the master narrative document
- If contradictions exist, prioritize the channel with the largest audience impact for correction first
