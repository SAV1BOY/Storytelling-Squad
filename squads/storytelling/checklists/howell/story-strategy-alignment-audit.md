# Story-Strategy Alignment Audit
> Quality gate for verifying the narrative connects to business outcomes, not just emotional engagement

## Purpose
Ensure the story is not just emotionally compelling but strategically purposeful. Every narrative must connect to measurable business outcomes (revenue, retention, alignment, conversion, fundraising) or it risks being entertainment without impact.

## Prerequisites
- Business objectives documented with measurable KPIs
- Target audience and their decision-making context defined
- Narrative draft completed
- Marketing or communication strategy documented

## Checklist

### Strategic Connection
- [ ] **Business Objective Named** — The specific business outcome the story is intended to drive is documented (e.g., increase trial signups by 15%, secure Series B funding, align leadership team on strategy)
- [ ] **Story Maps to Objective** — A clear logic chain connects the narrative's content to the desired business outcome; the connection is not assumed, it is articulated
- [ ] **Success Metric Defined** — A measurable indicator exists to evaluate whether the story achieved its business purpose (conversion rate, NPS change, deal close rate, employee engagement score)
- [ ] **ROI Justifiable** — The investment in crafting and deploying the story (time, money, attention) is proportional to the expected business return

### Audience-Outcome Fit
- [ ] **Audience Can Act** — The target audience has the authority, budget, or influence to produce the desired business outcome; the story is not told to the wrong people
- [ ] **Decision Journey Mapped** — The point in the audience's decision journey where the story intervenes is identified (awareness, consideration, decision, retention)
- [ ] **Story Addresses Decision Barriers** — The narrative specifically addresses the objections, fears, or information gaps that prevent the audience from taking the desired action

### Narrative-Strategy Congruence
- [ ] **Story Reinforces Brand Positioning** — The narrative advances the brand's strategic positioning, not just any emotionally appealing message
- [ ] **Story Fits the Channel Strategy** — The narrative is designed for the channels where the target audience will encounter it, not repurposed generically
- [ ] **Story Supports the Sales Process** — If applicable, the narrative equips sales teams with useful language, proof points, or frameworks they can use in conversations
- [ ] **Story Builds Long-Term Equity** — The narrative contributes to long-term brand equity (trust, differentiation, loyalty), not just short-term engagement

### Accountability
- [ ] **Measurement Plan Exists** — A plan for measuring the story's business impact is documented before deployment, not created after the fact
- [ ] **Feedback Loop Planned** — A mechanism exists to capture audience response and feed it back into strategy refinement
- [ ] **Timeline for Evaluation Set** — A specific date is set for evaluating the story's performance against the defined success metric
- [ ] **Story Will Be Iterated** — The narrative is treated as a living asset that will be refined based on performance data, not a one-and-done artifact

## Scoring
| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Pass | All 15 items checked; the story is both emotionally compelling and strategically rigorous |
| Conditional | 11-14 items checked; story is strategically connected but measurement or audience fit needs tightening |
| Fail | Fewer than 11 items, or the story has no documented connection to business outcomes |

## Next Steps
- If the strategy connection is weak, work backward from the business objective to define what the audience must think, feel, or do after encountering the story
- If measurement is absent, define one leading indicator and one lagging indicator before deployment
- Schedule a post-deployment review at the defined evaluation date
