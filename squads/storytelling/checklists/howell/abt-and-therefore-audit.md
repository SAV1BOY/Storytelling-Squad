# ABT (And, But, Therefore) Audit
> Quality gate for verifying ABT structure is clear with proper setup, tension, and resolution

## Purpose
Ensure the narrative follows the ABT (And, But, Therefore) framework: "And" establishes the agreeable context, "But" introduces the tension or complication, and "Therefore" delivers the resolution or action. This structure is the DNA of compelling narrative at every scale.

## Prerequisites
- Narrative draft, pitch, or messaging document complete
- Core message or argument identified
- Understanding of Randy Olson's ABT framework as applied to business storytelling

## Checklist

### "And" (Agreement/Setup)
- [ ] **Context Established** — The opening "And" section lays out the situation, background, or shared reality the audience agrees with
- [ ] **Audience Nods Along** — The setup is something the audience already knows, believes, or experiences; it creates alignment, not argument
- [ ] **Setup Is Concise** — The "And" section provides enough context to ground the audience but does not belabor the obvious; it moves quickly
- [ ] **No Premature Tension** — The setup does not introduce conflict too early; the audience needs to feel the stability before it is disrupted

### "But" (Tension/Complication)
- [ ] **"But" Creates Genuine Tension** — The complication introduces a real problem, contradiction, or obstacle that disrupts the agreement established in the "And"
- [ ] **Tension Is Specific** — The "But" names a concrete challenge, not a vague sense that something is wrong
- [ ] **Audience Feels the Problem** — The tension is relevant to the audience's own experience or concerns; they recognize the complication personally
- [ ] **Only One Primary "But"** — The narrative introduces one central tension, not multiple competing problems that dilute focus

### "Therefore" (Resolution/Action)
- [ ] **"Therefore" Follows Logically** — The resolution or proposed action is a direct, logical response to the tension introduced by the "But"
- [ ] **Resolution Is Specific and Actionable** — The "Therefore" proposes a clear course of action, solution, or conclusion, not a vague aspiration
- [ ] **Resolution Addresses the Tension** — The "Therefore" directly solves or engages with the problem raised by the "But," not a different problem
- [ ] **Forward Momentum Created** — The "Therefore" propels the audience toward a next step, decision, or changed understanding

### Structural Integrity
- [ ] **ABT Order Maintained** — The three elements appear in sequence: context first, then tension, then resolution; the order is not scrambled
- [ ] **ABT Operates at Multiple Levels** — The overall narrative follows ABT, and individual sections or paragraphs also use the ABT structure
- [ ] **No "AAA" Trap** — The narrative does not list agreeable facts without ever introducing tension (the "And, And, And" problem)
- [ ] **No "DHY" Trap** — The narrative does not pile on disconnected facts and complications without resolution (the "Despite, However, Yet" problem)

## Scoring
| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Pass | All 16 items checked; the ABT structure drives the narrative with clarity and momentum |
| Conditional | 12-15 items checked; ABT is present but one element is weak or misplaced |
| Fail | Fewer than 12 items, or the narrative falls into AAA or DHY patterns |

## Next Steps
- Distill the narrative to a single ABT sentence: "[Context], but [tension], therefore [resolution]"
- If the "But" is weak, ask "What would happen if we did nothing?" to sharpen the tension
- Test the ABT sentence on five people; if they do not lean in at the "But," the tension is insufficient
