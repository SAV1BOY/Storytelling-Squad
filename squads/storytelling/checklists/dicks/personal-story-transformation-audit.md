# Personal Story Transformation Audit
> Quality gate for verifying a specific, credible, personally felt transformation with a universal lesson

## Purpose
Ensure the personal story contains a genuine transformation that is specific enough to be believable, personal enough to be authentic, and universal enough that the audience sees their own life reflected in it.

## Prerequisites
- Personal story drafted
- Five-second moment identified
- Intended audience and context defined

## Checklist

### Specific Transformation
- [ ] **Transformation Is Named** — The storyteller can articulate what changed: a belief, a behavior, a relationship, a self-understanding, or a worldview
- [ ] **Before State Is Concrete** — Who the protagonist was before the transformation is shown through specific actions, thoughts, or habits, not generalizations
- [ ] **After State Is Concrete** — Who the protagonist became is equally specific; the audience can see the difference in behavior, not just a stated feeling
- [ ] **Change Is Bounded** — The transformation is a single, defined shift, not a vague "I grew as a person" or "I learned a lot"

### Credibility
- [ ] **Transformation Is Proportional** — The magnitude of change is appropriate to the events of the story; the audience believes this experience could produce this shift
- [ ] **Struggle Is Real** — The story shows genuine difficulty, doubt, or resistance before the transformation; it is not a smooth, easy epiphany
- [ ] **Imperfection Shown** — The storyteller includes unflattering details about themselves; the story does not present them as secretly perfect all along
- [ ] **No Exaggeration** — The facts, emotions, and outcomes are truthful; embellishment for dramatic effect does not cross into dishonesty

### Personally Felt
- [ ] **Emotion Is Present** — The storyteller's emotional experience during the transformation is communicated through detail and delivery, not labels ("I was sad")
- [ ] **Vulnerability Demonstrated** — The story includes a moment where the storyteller is exposed, uncertain, or at risk; they do not remain safely behind a wall
- [ ] **Stake Is Personal** — What mattered to the storyteller personally (not professionally, not abstractly) is at the center of the story
- [ ] **Storyteller Is Changed, Not Just Informed** — The transformation affected how the storyteller lives, not just what they know; it changed behavior, not just belief

### Universal Lesson
- [ ] **Audience Sees Themselves** — The specific details of the story are personal, but the underlying truth applies to the audience's own experience
- [ ] **Lesson Is Implied, Not Stated** — The universal takeaway emerges from the story without the storyteller delivering a moral; the audience draws their own connection
- [ ] **Multiple Audiences Can Connect** — The story resonates across different demographics, not only people with identical life experiences
- [ ] **Lesson Is Earned** — The universal truth feels discovered through the story, not imported from outside and pasted on

## Scoring
| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Pass | All 16 items checked; the transformation is specific, credible, felt, and universal |
| Conditional | 12-15 items checked; transformation is present but credibility or universality needs strengthening |
| Fail | Fewer than 12 items, or no identifiable transformation exists |

## Next Steps
- If the transformation is vague, answer: "What did I believe before that I no longer believe?" or "What do I do now that I never did before?"
- If credibility is weak, add more struggle and imperfection; audiences trust flawed narrators
- If the lesson is too explicit, cut the concluding moral and let the last scene carry the meaning
