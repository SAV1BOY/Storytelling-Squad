# Homework for Life Audit
> Quality gate for verifying regular story moment capture is happening, moments are documented, and the reservoir is growing

## Purpose
Ensure the storyteller or team is practicing Matthew Dicks' "Homework for Life" discipline: ending each day by identifying and recording the most storyworthy moment. This practice builds a reservoir of raw material and trains the storytelling muscle.

## Prerequisites
- Understanding of the Homework for Life practice from "Storyworthy"
- A capture system in place (spreadsheet, journal, app, notebook)
- Commitment from individuals or team to maintain the practice

## Checklist

### Regular Capture
- [ ] **Daily Practice Established** — The storyteller documents at least one moment from each day, or a minimum of 5 entries per week
- [ ] **Capture Happens Same Day** — Moments are recorded on the day they occur or the following morning, not batched weekly from fading memory
- [ ] **Consistent Format Used** — Each entry includes a date, a one-sentence description of the moment, and optionally the emotional charge or theme
- [ ] **Practice Sustained Over Time** — The capture habit has been maintained for at least 30 consecutive days, demonstrating commitment beyond initial enthusiasm

### Moment Quality
- [ ] **Moments Are Specific** — Entries describe concrete events ("my daughter asked if the moon was lonely") not vague summaries ("had a good day")
- [ ] **Moments Capture Change** — The best entries identify a moment where something shifted: a realization, a decision, a surprise, a connection, a loss
- [ ] **Variety of Sources** — Moments come from diverse areas of life (work, family, solitude, strangers, routines, surprises), not just one domain
- [ ] **Small Moments Included** — The capture practice values small, quiet moments as much as dramatic events; the mundane is mined for meaning

### Reservoir Growing
- [ ] **Running Total Tracked** — The total number of captured moments is known and growing; the storyteller can report their count
- [ ] **Reservoir Is Searchable** — Moments are organized in a way that allows retrieval by theme, emotion, or time period when a story is needed
- [ ] **Patterns Emerging** — Over time, recurring themes, characters, or preoccupations become visible in the collected moments
- [ ] **Moments Are Being Used** — At least some captured moments have been developed into full stories, pitches, or presentations; the reservoir feeds output

### Team Application (if applicable)
- [ ] **Team Participation Active** — If this is a team practice, multiple members are contributing and sharing moments regularly
- [ ] **Story Sharing Sessions Scheduled** — Regular opportunities exist for team members to share developed moments and receive feedback
- [ ] **Quality Over Compliance** — The practice is motivated by genuine curiosity about daily experience, not treated as a checkbox obligation

## Scoring
| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Pass | All applicable items checked; the reservoir is growing and feeding creative output |
| Conditional | 10-12 items checked; practice is active but inconsistent or moments lack specificity |
| Fail | Fewer than 10 items, or the practice has lapsed for more than 2 consecutive weeks |

## Next Steps
- If the practice has lapsed, restart with a commitment to just one sentence per day for 7 days
- If moments are vague, add sensory prompts: "What did I see, hear, or feel that I had never noticed before?"
- Schedule a monthly review of the reservoir to identify the strongest moments for development into full stories
