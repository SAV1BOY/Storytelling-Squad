# Investor Pitch Quality
> Quality gate for verifying problem, market, solution, traction, team, and ask are all present with narrative

## Purpose
Ensure the investor pitch covers all essential elements (problem, market, solution, traction, team, ask) while weaving them into a compelling narrative rather than presenting them as a disconnected slide sequence. Investors fund stories they believe in, not just spreadsheets.

## Prerequisites
- Investor pitch deck or presentation drafted
- Financial model and key metrics prepared
- Target investor profile and thesis understood

## Checklist

### Problem
- [ ] **Problem Is Visceral** — The problem is described in terms that make the investor feel the pain, not just understand it intellectually
- [ ] **Problem Is Large** — The problem affects a market large enough to justify venture-scale investment; the scope is quantified
- [ ] **Problem Is Current** — The problem is pressing now, not hypothetical or emerging; evidence of current urgency is provided

### Market
- [ ] **Market Size Credible** — TAM, SAM, and SOM are presented with bottom-up logic, not just top-down estimates from research reports
- [ ] **Market Timing Justified** — The narrative explains why now is the right time: technology shift, regulatory change, behavioral trend, or market gap

### Solution
- [ ] **Solution Is Clear** — What the product or service does is explained in one sentence without jargon
- [ ] **Solution Fits the Problem** — The connection between the stated problem and the offered solution is direct and obvious
- [ ] **Differentiation Articulated** — Why this solution is better than alternatives (including doing nothing) is specific, not generic claims of innovation

### Traction
- [ ] **Traction Is Quantified** — Key metrics (revenue, users, growth rate, retention, partnerships) are presented with specific numbers
- [ ] **Traction Tells a Story** — The metrics are presented as a trajectory, not a snapshot; the growth narrative shows momentum and direction
- [ ] **Credibility Markers Present** — Customer logos, testimonials, awards, or partnerships provide social proof beyond raw numbers

### Team
- [ ] **Team-Problem Fit Shown** — The narrative explains why this specific team is uniquely positioned to solve this problem; relevant experience and unfair advantages are highlighted
- [ ] **Key Roles Covered** — The founding or leadership team covers essential functions (technical, commercial, operational) or a plan to fill gaps is acknowledged

### Ask
- [ ] **Ask Is Specific** — The funding amount, use of funds, and expected milestones are clearly stated
- [ ] **Ask Is Justified** — The requested amount connects to specific milestones that will de-risk the investment and create value
- [ ] **Return Narrative Present** — The investor can see the path to returns: growth trajectory, potential exit scenarios, or comparable valuations

### Narrative Integration
- [ ] **Story Arc Present** — The pitch follows a narrative arc (problem creates tension, solution provides resolution, traction proves possibility, team inspires confidence) rather than a list of topics
- [ ] **Emotional Engagement** — The pitch produces an emotional response beyond intellectual interest; the investor wants to be part of this story
- [ ] **Memorable** — The pitch contains at least one element (story, statistic, demo, vision) the investor will remember and retell to partners

## Scoring
| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Pass | All 19 items checked; the pitch is both a compelling narrative and a rigorous business case |
| Conditional | 15-18 items checked; elements are present but narrative integration or traction needs strengthening |
| Fail | Fewer than 15 items, or core elements (problem, solution, ask) are missing or unsupported |

## Next Steps
- If the pitch is a slide sequence without narrative, restructure around the story: "Here is a world with a problem, here is what we built, here is proof it works, here is where we are going, and here is how you can be part of it"
- If traction is light, lead with the problem intensity and team strength; acknowledge traction stage honestly
- Practice the pitch with a hostile audience and time it; investor meetings are shorter than you think
