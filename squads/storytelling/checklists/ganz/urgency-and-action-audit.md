# Urgency and Action Audit
> Quality gate for verifying real urgency, specific action, and achievable next steps in mobilization narratives

## Purpose
Ensure the narrative creates genuine urgency that leads to specific, achievable action. False urgency breeds cynicism; vague calls to action breed inaction. This audit verifies that the urgency is real and the requested action is within the audience's capacity.

## Prerequisites
- Mobilization narrative or campaign communication drafted
- Specific action and timeline defined
- Audience capacity and constraints understood

## Checklist

### Real Urgency
- [ ] **Urgency Is Genuine** — The time pressure is based on a real deadline, window of opportunity, or escalating consequence, not manufactured fear
- [ ] **Consequence of Inaction Specific** — The narrative explains what happens if action is not taken, with concrete details rather than vague doom
- [ ] **Urgency Is Proportional** — The level of urgency matches the actual stakes; the narrative does not cry wolf or inflate danger
- [ ] **Urgency Is Current** — The reason to act now is connected to present circumstances, not hypothetical futures or stale threats

### Specific Action
- [ ] **Action Is Named** — The narrative requests one clear, specific action: sign, call, attend, donate, share, organize, vote, commit
- [ ] **Action Is Singular** — One primary action is requested, not a list of five things the audience could do; focus drives action
- [ ] **Action Is Described Concretely** — The audience knows the what, where, when, and how of the action; no interpretation required
- [ ] **Action Connects to Outcome** — The narrative explains how this specific action contributes to the larger goal; the audience sees the causal chain

### Achievable Next Step
- [ ] **Step Is Within Capacity** — The requested action is something the audience can actually do given their time, resources, skills, and authority
- [ ] **Barrier to Action Low** — Obstacles to taking the step (complexity, cost, fear, inconvenience) have been anticipated and minimized
- [ ] **First Step Is Small** — Even if the ultimate goal is large, the immediate ask is manageable and does not overwhelm
- [ ] **Success Is Visible** — The audience can see or confirm that they have completed the action; it is not ambiguous whether they have acted

### Integrity
- [ ] **No Manipulation** — The urgency and action request respect the audience's autonomy; they are not guilt-tripped, shamed, or deceived into acting
- [ ] **Follow-Through Planned** — After the audience takes the requested action, there is a clear path for continued engagement; one-and-done is avoided
- [ ] **Accountability Present** — The narrative's authors are accountable for what happens with the action taken; trust is maintained through transparency

## Scoring
| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Pass | All 15 items checked; urgency is real, action is clear, and the next step is achievable |
| Conditional | 11-14 items checked; urgency or action is present but specificity or achievability needs work |
| Fail | Fewer than 11 items, or urgency is manufactured or action is vague |

## Next Steps
- If urgency feels manufactured, identify the real deadline or cost and build the narrative around it
- If the action is too complex, break it into smaller steps and request only the first one
- Test the CTA with five people from the target audience and observe whether they can take the action without additional instruction
