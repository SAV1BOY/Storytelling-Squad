# Public Narrative: Self, Us, Now Audit
> Quality gate for verifying all three parts of public narrative are present, coherent, and connected

## Purpose
Ensure the public narrative follows Marshall Ganz's three-part framework: Story of Self (why the speaker is called to lead), Story of Us (why the community shares this purpose), and Story of Now (why action is urgent). All three parts must be present and flow into each other to move people from empathy to solidarity to action.

## Prerequisites
- Public narrative draft or outline completed
- Speaker's personal connection to the cause documented
- Community identity and shared values identified
- Specific action or campaign defined

## Checklist

### Story of Self
- [ ] **Personal Experience Shared** — The speaker shares a specific moment or experience that called them to this cause; it is not an abstract commitment
- [ ] **Values Revealed Through Story** — The speaker's core values emerge from the narrative, not from a list of stated beliefs
- [ ] **Vulnerability Present** — The Story of Self includes genuine risk, doubt, or struggle; it is not a hero narrative
- [ ] **Choice Moment Identified** — The story includes a moment where the speaker chose to act despite uncertainty, demonstrating agency

### Story of Us
- [ ] **Community Defined** — The "us" is clearly identified: who belongs to this group and what binds them together
- [ ] **Shared Experience Named** — The community's shared challenge, history, or aspiration is articulated with specificity
- [ ] **Shared Values Activated** — The Story of Us connects to values the community holds in common, creating solidarity
- [ ] **Inclusive, Not Exclusive** — The "us" is defined by shared values and purpose, not by excluding others; identity is built through affirmation

### Story of Now
- [ ] **Urgency Is Real** — The reason to act now is genuine and specific: a deadline, a threat, an opportunity window, a turning point
- [ ] **Challenge Named** — The obstacle or injustice requiring action is stated clearly and specifically
- [ ] **Specific Action Requested** — The audience knows exactly what they are being asked to do; the CTA is concrete and achievable
- [ ] **Hope Present** — Despite the urgency, the narrative communicates that action can make a difference; despair is not the final note

### Coherence and Connection
- [ ] **Self Flows into Us** — The transition from personal story to community story feels natural; the speaker's experience connects to the community's experience
- [ ] **Us Flows into Now** — The community's shared values and identity naturally lead to the urgent call for action; the connection is logical and emotional
- [ ] **All Three Parts Present** — No part of the public narrative is missing; Self without Us is self-indulgent, Us without Now is sentimental, Now without Self is impersonal
- [ ] **Emotional Arc Is Complete** — The narrative moves from empathy (Self) to solidarity (Us) to urgency and agency (Now)

## Scoring
| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Pass | All 16 items checked; the public narrative moves from personal truth to collective action |
| Conditional | 12-15 items checked; parts are present but transitions or specificity need strengthening |
| Fail | Fewer than 12 items, or any of the three parts is entirely missing |

## Next Steps
- If Story of Self is missing vulnerability, ask: "What scared me? What did I almost not do?"
- If Story of Us feels abstract, collect specific stories from community members and weave them in
- If Story of Now lacks urgency, identify the specific deadline or tipping point that makes delay costly
