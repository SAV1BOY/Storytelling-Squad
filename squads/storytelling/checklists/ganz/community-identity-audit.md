# Community Identity Audit
> Quality gate for verifying "us" is defined, inclusive, and identity is built through shared values

## Purpose
Ensure the narrative defines a community ("us") that is compelling, inclusive, and built on shared values rather than exclusion of others. The Story of Us must create belonging through affirmation, not through opposition alone.

## Prerequisites
- Community or audience identified
- Shared values and experiences documented
- Public narrative framework (Story of Self, Us, Now) in progress or completed

## Checklist

### "Us" Defined
- [ ] **Community Is Named** — The "us" is explicitly defined: who is part of this community and what makes them a group
- [ ] **Boundaries Are Clear** — The community's scope is defined enough that members know they belong; it is not so vague as to mean everyone or no one
- [ ] **Shared Experience Anchors Identity** — The community is bound by a specific shared experience, challenge, history, or aspiration, not just demographics
- [ ] **Identity Is Active** — Membership in the community is defined by what people do or value, not just who they are by birth or circumstance

### Inclusive Identity
- [ ] **Defined by Affirmation, Not Opposition** — The "us" is primarily defined by what the community stands for, not who or what it is against
- [ ] **No Unnecessary Exclusion** — The community boundaries do not exclude potential members who share the values but differ in background, identity, or experience
- [ ] **Welcoming to Newcomers** — The narrative makes it possible for someone new to the community to see themselves joining, not just observing from outside
- [ ] **Diversity Acknowledged** — The community's internal diversity is acknowledged as a strength, not smoothed over into false homogeneity

### Built Through Shared Values
- [ ] **Values Are the Foundation** — The community identity is rooted in shared values (justice, creativity, service, excellence, courage) not just shared grievance
- [ ] **Values Are Demonstrated in Stories** — The shared values are illustrated through specific stories of community members living those values
- [ ] **Values Create Pride** — Members feel pride in belonging to a community defined by these values; the identity is aspirational as well as descriptive
- [ ] **Values Guide Action** — The shared values point toward specific behaviors and commitments, not just feelings of belonging

### Narrative Effectiveness
- [ ] **Community Story Is Retellable** — The Story of Us can be told by any community member, not just the leader or narrator
- [ ] **Community Story Evolves** — The "us" narrative has room to grow and incorporate new chapters, members, and experiences over time
- [ ] **Connection Between Self and Us** — The speaker's personal story connects to the community's story in a way that feels authentic, not forced
- [ ] **"Us" Leads to "Now"** — The community identity naturally creates the foundation for urgent collective action

## Scoring
| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Pass | All 16 items checked; the community identity is clear, inclusive, values-driven, and action-oriented |
| Conditional | 12-15 items checked; community is defined but inclusivity or values depth needs work |
| Fail | Fewer than 12 items, or the community is defined primarily through exclusion or opposition |

## Next Steps
- If "us" is vague, interview 5 community members and ask: "What do we share? What makes us 'us'?"
- If identity is oppositional, rewrite the Story of Us leading with shared values and aspirations, not shared enemies
- If newcomers feel excluded, add onboarding language or welcoming stories that lower the barrier to belonging
