# Tension and Novelty Audit
> Quality gate for verifying tension is maintained, novelty is present, and hot cognition is activated

## Purpose
Ensure the pitch maintains attention through the dual engines of tension (unresolved conflict or uncertainty) and novelty (new, unexpected, or pattern-breaking information). When both are present, the audience operates in "hot cognition" - engaged, alert, and emotionally processing rather than analytically dismissing.

## Prerequisites
- Pitch or presentation drafted
- Audience's baseline state anticipated (bored, skeptical, distracted, analytical)
- Understanding of Klaff's concepts of hot cognition vs. cold cognition

## Checklist

### Tension Maintained
- [ ] **Tension Introduced Early** — Within the first 2 minutes, an unresolved question, conflict, or stakes gap is introduced that creates audience anticipation
- [ ] **Tension Is Not Resolved Prematurely** — Key questions or uncertainties are held open long enough to maintain engagement; the payoff is delayed strategically
- [ ] **Multiple Tension Loops** — The pitch opens and closes multiple smaller tension loops rather than relying on a single thread; when one resolves, another opens
- [ ] **Tension Is Felt Physically** — The audience leans in, holds breath, or shows visible engagement; tension is embodied, not just intellectual

### Novelty Present
- [ ] **Unexpected Element Included** — At least one piece of information, perspective, or approach in the pitch is genuinely new to the audience
- [ ] **Pattern Broken** — The pitch breaks a pattern the audience expects (format, timing, content type, tone) at least once to reset attention
- [ ] **Fresh Framing of Familiar Ideas** — Even known information is presented through an unfamiliar lens, analogy, or context that makes it feel new
- [ ] **No Predictability** — The audience cannot guess the next slide, sentence, or direction; the pitch maintains an element of unpredictability

### Hot Cognition Activated
- [ ] **Emotional Before Analytical** — The pitch leads with emotionally engaging content (stories, stakes, visual contrast) before presenting analytical content (data, logic, process)
- [ ] **Croc Brain Engaged** — The opening is simple, visual, novel, and high-contrast enough to pass the reptilian brain filter and avoid the "boring, ignore it" response
- [ ] **Cold Cognition Avoided** — Dense data, long lists, complex charts, and monotonous delivery are either eliminated or strategically placed after emotional engagement is established
- [ ] **Desire Created Before Details** — The audience wants the solution before the pitcher explains the solution; wanting precedes understanding

### Sustained Engagement
- [ ] **No Dead Spots** — Every section of the pitch contains either tension, novelty, or both; there are no stretches where neither is present
- [ ] **Variety of Engagement Tactics** — The pitch uses multiple methods to maintain attention: story, data surprise, visual reveal, audience interaction, status play
- [ ] **Energy Arc Managed** — The pitch's energy follows a designed arc; it does not start strong and fade, or start flat and never recover
- [ ] **Audience Attention Recoverable** — If attention dips (phone check, sidebar conversation), a novelty or tension moment within the next 60 seconds can recapture it

## Scoring
| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Pass | All 16 items checked; the pitch is a sustained experience of tension and discovery |
| Conditional | 12-15 items checked; tension or novelty is present but inconsistent across the pitch |
| Fail | Fewer than 12 items, or the pitch operates primarily in cold cognition |

## Next Steps
- Map the pitch minute by minute and label each section as T (tension), N (novelty), B (both), or D (dead); eliminate D sections
- If cold cognition dominates, restructure to lead with story or stakes before data
- Add one novelty moment per 3-minute segment of the pitch
