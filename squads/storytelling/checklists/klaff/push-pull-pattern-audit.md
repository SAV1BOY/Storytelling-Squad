# Push/Pull Pattern Audit
> Quality gate for verifying desire/withdrawal alternation creates dynamic engagement throughout the pitch

## Purpose
Ensure the pitch employs the push/pull dynamic: creating desire (pull) then withdrawing slightly (push), maintaining the audience in a state of active pursuit rather than passive reception. This pattern prevents the pitcher from chasing the deal and instead positions the audience as the pursuer.

## Prerequisites
- Pitch drafted with key moments identified
- Understanding of Klaff's push/pull technique
- Awareness of the audience's motivations and decision triggers

## Checklist

### Pull (Creating Desire)
- [ ] **Value Clearly Demonstrated** — The pitch shows compelling value that makes the audience want what is being offered
- [ ] **Scarcity or Exclusivity Signaled** — The opportunity is framed as limited, selective, or time-bound, increasing its perceived desirability
- [ ] **Social Proof Provided** — Evidence that others want or have benefited from this opportunity increases the pull
- [ ] **Emotional Connection Made** — The audience feels a personal connection to the opportunity, not just intellectual interest

### Push (Creating Withdrawal)
- [ ] **Pitcher Does Not Chase** — At no point does the pitcher appear desperate, eager for approval, or willing to compromise to close the deal
- [ ] **Qualification Inserted** — The pitcher suggests this opportunity may not be right for everyone; the audience must qualify themselves
- [ ] **Indifference Projected** — The pitcher communicates that the outcome will be acceptable either way; this is not the pitcher's only option
- [ ] **Slight Withdrawal Present** — At least one moment in the pitch where the pitcher pulls back or suggests the audience may not be ready creates pursuit energy

### Alternation Pattern
- [ ] **Pattern Alternates** — Pull and push moments alternate throughout the pitch rather than clustering all pull at the start and all push at the end
- [ ] **Frequency Is Natural** — The push/pull rhythm feels conversational and genuine, not mechanical or manipulative
- [ ] **Intensity Escalates** — Later pull moments are more compelling and later push moments are bolder than earlier ones, building toward the close
- [ ] **Final Pull Is Strongest** — The pitch ends on a strong pull (compelling opportunity, clear next step) not on a push (indifference)

### Authenticity
- [ ] **Push Is Not Hostility** — Withdrawal moments come across as confidence and selectivity, not as rudeness or contempt
- [ ] **Pull Is Not Desperation** — Desire-creating moments are grounded in genuine value, not hype, exaggeration, or manufactured urgency
- [ ] **Dynamic Feels Natural** — The push/pull pattern feels like a confident conversation between equals, not a scripted negotiation tactic
- [ ] **Audience Ends in Pursuit Mode** — By the close, the audience is asking "How do we move forward?" rather than the pitcher asking "So, what do you think?"

## Scoring
| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Pass | All 16 items checked; the push/pull dynamic creates active audience pursuit |
| Conditional | 12-15 items checked; pattern is present but timing or authenticity needs refinement |
| Fail | Fewer than 12 items, or the pitch is all pull (desperate) or all push (hostile) |

## Next Steps
- Map each section of the pitch as Pull or Push and verify the alternation pattern
- If the pitch is all pull, add one qualification moment: "This may not be for everyone, and that's fine"
- If the pitch is all push, add one compelling value demonstration that makes the audience lean in
- Practice the push moments until they feel confident, not scripted
