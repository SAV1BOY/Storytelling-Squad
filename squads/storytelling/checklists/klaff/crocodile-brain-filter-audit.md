# Crocodile Brain Filter Audit
> Quality gate for verifying the message is simple, visual, novel, high-contrast, and emotionally engaging enough to pass the croc brain

## Purpose
Ensure the pitch's opening and core message pass through the audience's primitive "crocodile brain" filter. Klaff teaches that every message is first processed by the most primitive part of the brain, which filters for threat, novelty, and relevance. Messages that are complex, abstract, or boring are killed before they reach higher cognition.

## Prerequisites
- Pitch or presentation opening drafted
- Core message distilled
- Understanding of Klaff's crocodile brain concept

## Checklist

### Simple
- [ ] **Core Message in One Sentence** — The pitch's central idea can be expressed in a single, clear sentence that requires no background knowledge
- [ ] **No Cognitive Overload** — The opening does not present more than one idea at a time; complexity is introduced gradually after engagement is established
- [ ] **Jargon Eliminated** — Technical terms, acronyms, and insider language are absent from the opening and minimized throughout
- [ ] **Structure Is Obvious** — The audience always knows where they are in the pitch; the path forward is clear

### Visual
- [ ] **Mental Images Created** — The pitch uses language that produces pictures in the audience's mind: concrete nouns, action verbs, spatial language
- [ ] **Abstract Concepts Made Tangible** — Any abstract idea (market opportunity, risk, growth) is translated into a visual metaphor or concrete example
- [ ] **Visual Aids Support, Not Replace** — If slides are used, they are image-driven, not text-driven; they amplify the spoken message

### Novel
- [ ] **Something New Presented** — The pitch contains at least one piece of information, insight, or perspective the audience has not encountered before
- [ ] **Familiar Made Unfamiliar** — Even known concepts are reframed in a way that makes the audience see them differently
- [ ] **Pattern Disruption** — The pitch does something unexpected in form or content within the first 3 minutes to signal "this is different"

### High-Contrast
- [ ] **Before/After Contrast** — The pitch presents a clear contrast between the current state (problem) and the future state (solution) that is dramatic and visceral
- [ ] **Binary Choices Presented** — At key moments, the pitch frames decisions as clear contrasts (this vs. that, now vs. later, us vs. them) rather than nuanced spectrums
- [ ] **Stakes Are Black and White** — The consequences of action vs. inaction are presented with enough contrast to trigger a response

### Emotionally Engaging
- [ ] **Emotion Precedes Logic** — The first thing the audience feels is an emotion (curiosity, concern, excitement, recognition), not an analytical assessment
- [ ] **Threat or Opportunity Signaled** — The croc brain is tuned to detect threats and opportunities; the pitch clearly signals one or both
- [ ] **Personal Relevance Established** — Within 60 seconds, the audience understands why this pitch matters to them specifically, not abstractly

## Scoring
| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Pass | All 16 items checked; the message sails through the croc brain filter into higher cognition |
| Conditional | 12-15 items checked; most elements pass but complexity or abstraction creates friction |
| Fail | Fewer than 12 items, or the opening is complex, abstract, and emotionally flat |

## Next Steps
- Have someone unfamiliar with the topic listen to the first 60 seconds and report what they understood and felt
- Replace every abstract noun in the opening with a concrete image or example
- If the pitch fails the simplicity check, cut content until the core message is inescapable
