# Archetype Alignment Audit
> Quality gate for verifying the brand archetype is identified, consistently embodied, and behaviorally authentic

## Purpose
Ensure the narrative's central character or brand voice operates from a coherent Jungian archetype that is demonstrated through action and behavior, not merely declared as a label.

## Prerequisites
- Brand archetype formally identified (e.g., Hero, Sage, Outlaw, Caregiver)
- Complete narrative draft or story outline
- Reference document listing the archetype's core traits, shadow traits, and motivations

## Checklist

### Identification
- [ ] **Archetype Explicitly Named** — The chosen archetype is documented with a rationale for why it fits the brand or character
- [ ] **Archetype Justified by Context** — The archetype choice connects to the audience's needs and the market position, not just internal preference
- [ ] **Shadow Side Acknowledged** — The archetype's shadow or negative expression is identified and a strategy exists to avoid or address it

### Consistency
- [ ] **Voice Matches Archetype** — The tone, vocabulary, and cadence of the narrative are consistent with the archetype's communication style
- [ ] **Behavior Matches Archetype** — The character or brand takes actions that an audience would expect from this archetype, not contradictory ones
- [ ] **Visual and Sensory Alignment** — Any imagery, metaphors, or sensory language reinforces the archetype rather than undermining it
- [ ] **No Archetype Drift** — The narrative does not accidentally shift into a different archetype mid-story without intentional purpose

### Depth Beyond Labels
- [ ] **Shown Not Told** — The archetype is demonstrated through at least three specific behaviors or decisions, not stated in exposition
- [ ] **Archetype Under Pressure** — The narrative shows how the archetype responds to conflict or adversity, revealing its core nature
- [ ] **Audience Recognition Test** — A reader unfamiliar with the archetype label could independently describe the character in archetypal terms
- [ ] **Emotional Resonance Present** — The archetype evokes a predictable emotional response (trust for Caregiver, aspiration for Hero, etc.)

### Behavioral Integrity
- [ ] **Decisions Align with Archetype Motivation** — Every major decision the character makes can be traced to the archetype's core motivation
- [ ] **Relationships Reflect Archetype Role** — How the character relates to others (allies, antagonists, audience) fits the archetype pattern
- [ ] **Growth Stays Within Archetype** — Character development deepens the archetype rather than abandoning it; a Hero becomes a wiser Hero, not suddenly a Jester
- [ ] **No Performative Archetype** — The archetype feels lived-in and authentic, not like a costume worn for marketing purposes

## Scoring
| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Pass | All 15 items checked; archetype feels inevitable and organic |
| Conditional | 11-14 items checked; minor inconsistencies that can be resolved in revision |
| Fail | Fewer than 11 items, or archetype is only labeled but never demonstrated |

## Next Steps
- If archetype drift is detected, map each scene to the archetype's motivation and revise deviations
- If the archetype feels performative, add scenes where the character acts on archetype values when no one is watching
- Cross-reference with Mythic Symbolism Audit to ensure symbols reinforce the archetype
