# Mythic Symbolism Audit
> Quality gate for verifying symbols are universal, culturally appropriate, and serve the story

## Purpose
Ensure that mythic symbols, metaphors, and imagery in the narrative tap into universal human experience without relying on cliches, cultural insensitivity, or decorative flourishes that add no meaning.

## Prerequisites
- Complete narrative draft with all symbolic and metaphorical language identified
- Cultural context of the target audience documented
- List of all recurring images, metaphors, and symbols extracted from the draft

## Checklist

### Universality
- [ ] **Symbols Tap Universal Experience** — Each major symbol connects to a widely shared human experience (journey, transformation, light/dark, ascent/descent) rather than niche references
- [ ] **No Cliche Reliance** — Symbols avoid overused tropes (e.g., "light at the end of the tunnel," "rising phoenix") unless deliberately subverted or renewed with fresh context
- [ ] **Fresh Expression of Timeless Themes** — Familiar archetypal images are presented through a specific, concrete lens that makes them feel new
- [ ] **Accessibility Without Dumbing Down** — Symbols work on a surface level for casual audiences and reward deeper reading for attentive ones

### Cultural Appropriateness
- [ ] **Cultural Origins Respected** — Any symbol drawn from a specific culture is used with understanding of its original meaning, not stripped of context
- [ ] **No Unintentional Offense** — Symbols have been reviewed for potential misreadings across the primary audience's cultural backgrounds
- [ ] **Inclusive Symbolism** — The symbolic language does not inadvertently exclude or alienate segments of the intended audience
- [ ] **Local Adaptation Considered** — If the narrative will cross cultural boundaries, symbols have been evaluated for translatability

### Story Service
- [ ] **Every Symbol Earns Its Place** — Each symbol can be removed as a thought experiment; if nothing is lost, the symbol is decorative and should be cut or strengthened
- [ ] **Symbols Advance Theme** — Metaphors and images connect directly to the narrative's controlling idea or theme, not just to atmosphere
- [ ] **Symbolic Consistency** — Symbols introduced early reappear or evolve later; no orphan symbols that appear once and vanish
- [ ] **Metaphors Do Not Contradict** — Mixed metaphors are absent; the symbolic system is internally coherent

### Craft and Execution
- [ ] **Sensory Grounding** — Symbols are anchored in concrete sensory detail (sight, sound, texture) rather than abstract language
- [ ] **Layered Meaning** — At least two major symbols operate on multiple levels (literal, emotional, thematic) simultaneously
- [ ] **Subtlety Over Declaration** — Symbols are woven into action and description rather than explained through narration or dialogue
- [ ] **Emotional Resonance Tested** — At least one reader or reviewer has confirmed the symbols evoke the intended emotional response

## Scoring
| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Pass | All 16 items checked; symbolism feels organic and resonant |
| Conditional | 12-15 items checked; minor issues with consistency or cultural review pending |
| Fail | Fewer than 12 items, or culturally inappropriate symbols present |

## Next Steps
- Remove or replace any decorative symbols that fail the removal thought experiment
- Commission cultural sensitivity review for any symbols flagged in the appropriateness section
- Map symbols to the controlling idea and ensure every major symbol reinforces it
