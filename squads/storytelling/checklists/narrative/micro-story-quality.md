# Micro-Story Quality
> Quality gate for verifying a complete narrative exists in small format with beginning, middle, and end

## Purpose
Ensure that short-form stories (social media posts, testimonial snippets, ad copy, email hooks, 30-second pitches) contain a complete narrative arc despite their brevity. Compression is not an excuse for incompleteness; a micro-story must still transform.

## Prerequisites
- Micro-story drafted (under 300 words or 60 seconds spoken)
- Target channel and format defined
- Core message or transformation identified

## Checklist

### Complete Narrative Arc
- [ ] **Beginning Present** — The micro-story establishes a starting state, character, or situation in the opening line or two
- [ ] **Middle Present** — A complication, tension, or turning point disrupts the starting state, creating movement
- [ ] **End Present** — The micro-story resolves with a new state, insight, or outcome that is different from the beginning
- [ ] **Transformation Visible** — Even in compressed form, something has changed between the first and last sentence

### Compression Craft
- [ ] **No Shortcuts on Story** — The narrative is compressed through economy of language, not by skipping essential beats (setup, tension, resolution)
- [ ] **Every Word Earns Its Place** — There are no filler words, throat-clearing phrases, or decorative language; the word count is at minimum
- [ ] **Specificity Over Generality** — Concrete details (a name, a number, a place, an action) replace abstract language; specifics compress more meaning into fewer words
- [ ] **Single Thread** — The micro-story follows one character through one event with one takeaway; no subplots or tangents

### Emotional Impact
- [ ] **Emotion in Compressed Space** — The micro-story produces an emotional response despite its brevity; the audience feels something
- [ ] **Hook in First Line** — The opening sentence creates curiosity, surprise, or recognition that pulls the reader into the remaining content
- [ ] **Landing Is Clean** — The final line or phrase delivers the payoff without explanation or moral; it trusts the reader to get it
- [ ] **Resonance Beyond the Story** — The micro-story suggests a larger truth or insight that the audience carries away

### Format Fitness
- [ ] **Length Fits Platform** — The micro-story respects the constraints of its channel (character limits, attention spans, visual context)
- [ ] **Standalone Comprehension** — The micro-story makes sense without requiring the audience to click through, read a thread, or know the backstory
- [ ] **Shareable** — The micro-story is compelling enough that the audience would forward, repost, or quote it

## Scoring
| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Pass | All 15 items checked; the micro-story is a complete, compressed, emotionally resonant narrative |
| Conditional | 11-14 items checked; story is present but compression or emotional impact needs tightening |
| Fail | Fewer than 11 items, or the micro-story is a statement or claim, not a narrative |

## Next Steps
- If the arc is incomplete, identify which beat is missing (setup, tension, or resolution) and add it in one sentence
- If the story is too long, cut context and start at the moment of tension rather than the backstory
- Test with three people: can they retell the micro-story after hearing it once?
