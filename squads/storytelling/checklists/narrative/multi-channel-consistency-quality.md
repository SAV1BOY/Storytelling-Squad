# Multi-Channel Consistency Quality
> Quality gate for verifying the same core story is adapted (not copied) for each channel

## Purpose
Ensure the brand's core narrative maintains its essence across every channel while being genuinely adapted for each platform's format, audience expectations, and consumption context. Copying the same content everywhere is lazy; telling a different story everywhere is incoherent.

## Prerequisites
- Core narrative documented (brand script, key messages, controlling idea)
- Channel inventory with format specifications for each
- Audience profile per channel documented

## Checklist

### Core Story Preserved
- [ ] **Controlling Idea Consistent** — The fundamental message, theme, or value proposition is the same across all channels
- [ ] **Character Roles Maintained** — The customer is always the hero and the brand always the guide across every touchpoint
- [ ] **Emotional Tone Recognizable** — The brand's emotional signature (inspiring, reassuring, provocative, warm) is consistent across channels
- [ ] **Key Proof Points Present** — Core evidence (statistics, testimonials, case studies) appears in appropriate form across channels, not just on one

### Genuine Adaptation
- [ ] **Format Fits Channel** — Each channel's version respects the platform's native format: video for YouTube, carousel for Instagram, long-form for blog, concise for email
- [ ] **Length Calibrated** — Content length matches channel expectations: 30 seconds for social video, 800 words for email, 2,000 for blog
- [ ] **Tone Adjusted Within Range** — Tone shifts slightly per channel (more casual on social, more formal in PR) while staying within the brand's tonal range
- [ ] **CTA Channel-Appropriate** — The call to action fits the channel: "swipe up" on Instagram, "click here" in email, "visit" in a podcast ad

### No Incoherence
- [ ] **No Contradictory Claims** — No channel makes a promise, claim, or positioning statement that another channel contradicts
- [ ] **Timeline Consistent** — Any dates, milestones, or chronology mentioned are consistent across channels
- [ ] **Visual Consistency** — Imagery, colors, and visual treatment are recognizably from the same brand across channels
- [ ] **Customer Experience Coherent** — A person encountering the brand on multiple channels within a single day would not feel confused or encounter conflicting messages

### Strategic Integration
- [ ] **Channels Complement Each Other** — Each channel adds something unique to the overall narrative; they build on each other rather than duplicating
- [ ] **Journey Across Channels Planned** — The path a customer takes across channels (discover on social, research on website, convert via email) is designed, not accidental
- [ ] **Measurement Spans Channels** — The effectiveness of the narrative is measured holistically, not just per channel in isolation

## Scoring
| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Pass | All 15 items checked; the narrative is one story told well across many platforms |
| Conditional | 11-14 items checked; mostly consistent but some channels feel disconnected or copied |
| Fail | Fewer than 11 items, or channels actively contradict each other or content is copy-pasted |

## Next Steps
- Create a channel-by-channel adaptation guide showing how the core narrative translates to each platform
- If content is being copied, assign channel-specific adaptation as a deliberate step in the content creation process
- Audit all active channels quarterly for consistency by comparing to the core narrative document
