# Webinar Narrative Quality Checklist
> Quality gate for validating that a webinar integrates teaching, tension, and offer into a cohesive narrative experience

## Purpose
Gates the narrative quality of webinars, masterclasses, and live online presentations that combine education with a commercial or action-oriented objective. Use after the webinar script or outline is drafted and before rehearsal. Ensures the webinar delivers genuine value while building toward its offer, without the teaching and selling feeling disconnected.

## Prerequisites
- A webinar script, outline, or slide deck exists
- The topic, audience, and offer (or CTA) are defined
- The webinar duration is set (typically 45-90 minutes)
- Teaching content has been developed
- The offer, product, or desired action is clearly specified

## Checklist

### Teaching Arc
- [ ] **Teaching delivers genuine value** — The educational content is useful on its own, even if the audience never buys or takes the offered action; it is not a thinly veiled sales pitch
- [ ] **Content organized around 3-5 key insights** — The teaching portion is structured into distinct, memorable takeaways, not a firehose of information
- [ ] **Each insight builds on the previous** — Teaching points are sequenced so each one requires or enriches the one before; no random topic jumping
- [ ] **Quick wins included** — At least one teaching point gives the audience something they can apply immediately during or right after the webinar
- [ ] **Knowledge gap created** — The teaching reveals enough to be valuable but also makes the audience aware of what they still do not know, naturally positioning the offer

### Tension Arc
- [ ] **Problem established in first 10 minutes** — The webinar opens by framing the audience's pain, challenge, or aspiration with specificity and urgency
- [ ] **Stakes raised throughout** — As teaching progresses, the consequences of not solving the problem become clearer and more personal
- [ ] **Story woven through teaching** — At least one running narrative (founder story, case study, or audience-surrogate journey) threads through the educational content
- [ ] **Emotional variation present** — The webinar alternates between intellectual (learning) and emotional (story, humor, urgency) beats to maintain engagement
- [ ] **Engagement hooks at drop-off points** — Interactive elements (polls, questions, stories, reveals) are placed at 10-15 minute intervals where attention naturally dips

### Offer Arc
- [ ] **Transition to offer is natural** — The shift from teaching to presenting the offer feels like a logical next step, not an abrupt pivot ("Now let me tell you about...")
- [ ] **Offer solves the gap teaching created** — The product, service, or action directly addresses the knowledge or capability gap the teaching revealed
- [ ] **Offer is presented as a story** — The offer section includes at least one transformation story (customer result, case study) rather than just features and pricing
- [ ] **Objections addressed within narrative** — Common reasons for not taking action are woven into the offer section through stories or FAQ-style beats
- [ ] **Scarcity or urgency is real** — Any limited-time or limited-availability claims are genuine and specific (actual deadline, actual capacity limit)
- [ ] **CTA is repeated and clear** — The specific action (register, purchase, book a call) is stated at least 3 times in the final third of the webinar with a clear URL or next step

### Production and Flow
- [ ] **Total runtime respects the audience** — The webinar fits within its advertised time window; teaching and offer are proportioned (typically 60-70% teaching, 30-40% offer and Q&A)
- [ ] **Opening hook in first 2 minutes** — The webinar begins with a compelling reason to stay: a bold promise, a surprising stat, or a provocative question
- [ ] **Slide design supports narrative** — Slides reinforce the spoken narrative rather than duplicating it; no text-heavy slides that the presenter reads aloud

## Scoring
Must pass **14/19 minimum**. The following items are CRITICAL and must all pass:
- Teaching delivers genuine value
- Transition to offer is natural
- Offer solves the gap teaching created
- Opening hook in first 2 minutes

Any CRITICAL failure requires webinar restructuring.

## Next Steps
- **On Pass:** Move to rehearsal. Time each section independently. Test technical setup. Prepare for Q&A using improv-readiness-quality.md.
- **On Fail:** Restructure using a three-act framework: Act 1 (problem + promise), Act 2 (teaching + story), Act 3 (offer + proof + CTA). Reference Duarte's Sparkline (squads/storytelling/checklists/duarte/) for presentation narrative structure and the pitch framework (squads/storytelling/checklists/pitch/) for offer positioning. If the teaching and offer feel disconnected, the content topic may need to be reframed to bridge them naturally.
