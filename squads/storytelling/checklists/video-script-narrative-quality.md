# Video Script Narrative Quality Checklist
> Quality gate for validating that a video script is structured for retention, emotional impact, and visual storytelling

## Purpose
Gates the narrative quality of video scripts before production begins. Use for any video format: YouTube content, social video, brand films, explainers, testimonials, course content, or ads. Ensures the script is designed around the retention curve, hooks immediately, uses visual storytelling rather than talking-head narration, and delivers its payoff before the CTA.

## Prerequisites
- A complete video script or detailed outline exists
- The target platform and its conventions are known (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, website)
- The target video length is defined
- The audience and objective are clear
- Visual references or storyboard concepts are available or in progress

## Checklist

### Retention Curve Design
- [ ] **Hook in first 3 seconds** — The video opens with a visually or verbally arresting moment: a bold claim, a surprising image, a question, or action in progress; no logos, intros, or "Hey guys"
- [ ] **Re-hook at 30-second mark** — A second engagement spike is designed near the 30-second point to catch viewers who survived the opening but are deciding whether to stay
- [ ] **Pattern interrupts every 60-90 seconds** — For videos over 2 minutes, visual or tonal shifts (new location, graphic, pace change, humor, story beat) are scripted at regular intervals
- [ ] **No front-loading of context** — Background information is woven in as needed, not dumped at the start; the video begins in media res or with a promise
- [ ] **Retention dips anticipated** — Points in the script where attention is likely to flag (technical explanations, transitions, slower moments) have been identified and reinforced with visual or narrative hooks

### Visual Storytelling
- [ ] **Show, don't tell enforced** — At least 50% of the key information is communicated through visuals (demonstrations, graphics, B-roll, actions) rather than narration alone
- [ ] **Script includes visual direction** — The script specifies what the viewer sees, not just what they hear; visual cues are written alongside dialogue or narration
- [ ] **Talking-head time limited** — If the video includes a speaker on camera, unbroken talking-head segments do not exceed 30 seconds before a visual change
- [ ] **Visual metaphors used** — At least one abstract concept is illustrated visually rather than verbally (a growing chart, a physical demonstration, a comparison shot)
- [ ] **B-roll is purposeful** — Every B-roll or cutaway shot specified in the script serves the narrative; no filler footage for its own sake

### Narrative Structure
- [ ] **Story arc present** — The video follows a narrative structure: setup (problem/context) > confrontation (tension/journey) > resolution (payoff/answer)
- [ ] **Payoff delivered before CTA** — The primary value (answer, revelation, transformation, lesson) is delivered before the audience is asked to subscribe, click, or buy
- [ ] **Single through-line maintained** — The video pursues one topic or story; tangents that do not serve the main thread are cut
- [ ] **Emotional beats scripted** — Moments designed to produce specific emotions (surprise, empathy, humor, inspiration) are identified and noted in the script

### Audio and Pacing
- [ ] **Narration pacing varies** — The script indicates pace changes: faster for excitement, slower for emphasis, pauses for impact
- [ ] **Script reads aloud naturally** — The narration has been read at intended pace; it sounds conversational, not written-for-the-page
- [ ] **Music and sound design noted** — The script includes cues for music shifts, sound effects, or silence that support the emotional arc
- [ ] **Runtime verified** — The script has been timed (read aloud with visual beat pauses) and fits the target duration within a 10% margin

### Platform Optimization
- [ ] **Format matches platform** — Aspect ratio, length, caption requirements, and style norms for the target platform are accounted for in the script
- [ ] **Thumb-stop moment in first frame** — The opening visual, if frozen as a thumbnail, would compel a viewer to click or watch
- [ ] **Captions scripted** — Key text overlays or captions are written into the script for platforms where many viewers watch without sound

## Scoring
Must pass **15/20 minimum**. The following items are CRITICAL and must all pass:
- Hook in first 3 seconds
- Show, don't tell enforced
- Payoff delivered before CTA
- Script reads aloud naturally

Any CRITICAL failure requires script revision before production.

## Next Steps
- **On Pass:** Move to storyboarding and production. Brief the production team with the annotated script. Schedule a table read.
- **On Fail:** Restructure the script. Reference the Hook and Open Loop checklist (hook-and-open-loop-quality.md) for opening fixes, the Structure and Pacing checklist (structure-and-pacing-quality.md) for arc issues, and platform-specific retention research for hook patterns. Storyboard the key moments before rewriting the narration. Watch 3 high-performing videos in the same niche and reverse-engineer their retention strategies.
