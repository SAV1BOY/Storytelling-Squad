# Founder Story Quality Checklist
> Quality gate for validating that a founder narrative is authentic, compelling, and strategically purposeful

## Purpose
Gates the quality of a founder's personal origin story. Use when crafting or revising an About the Founder page, pitch deck origin slide, keynote personal narrative, media interview prep, or any content where the founder's personal journey carries strategic weight. Ensures the story is not a resume recital but a genuine narrative that builds trust and authority.

## Prerequisites
- A draft founder narrative exists (written or recorded)
- The founder has been interviewed or has provided raw material
- The business context and target audience are defined
- The strategic purpose of the founder story is clear (fundraising, brand building, recruiting, media)

## Checklist

### Origin Moment
- [ ] **Specific origin moment identified** — The story pinpoints a single, concrete moment or event that sparked the journey, not a vague "I always wanted to..."
- [ ] **Origin is sensory and vivid** — The founding moment includes at least two sensory details (what was seen, heard, felt) that place the audience in the scene
- [ ] **Origin connects to a real problem** — The founding moment arose from encountering a genuine pain, gap, or injustice, not from a market analysis
- [ ] **Timeline is clear** — The audience can follow when key events happened relative to each other

### Personal Motivation and Vulnerability
- [ ] **Personal "why" is explicit** — The story explains why this person cares about this problem in a way that goes beyond financial opportunity
- [ ] **Vulnerability is present** — The founder reveals at least one moment of doubt, fear, failure, or personal cost that makes the story human
- [ ] **Vulnerability is strategic** — The vulnerable moment is relevant to the narrative arc and builds trust, not pity; it demonstrates resilience
- [ ] **Sacrifices named** — The story mentions what the founder gave up, risked, or endured to pursue this path
- [ ] **Emotional honesty present** — The narrative includes genuine feelings (frustration, excitement, fear, pride) rather than corporate-neutral language

### Mission Connection
- [ ] **Personal story connects to company mission** — The founder's personal experience logically leads to the company's reason for existing
- [ ] **Bridge from personal to universal** — The story transitions from "my problem" to "our problem" to "your problem," making the audience part of the mission
- [ ] **Mission is forward-looking** — The story does not only look backward; it connects the origin to a future vision the audience can join

### Authority and Credibility
- [ ] **Relevant expertise established** — The narrative demonstrates why this founder is uniquely qualified to solve this problem (experience, insight, access, obsession)
- [ ] **Results or traction mentioned** — At least one proof point demonstrates that the founder's vision is working, not just aspirational
- [ ] **Authority shown through action, not claimed** — Credibility comes from what the founder did, built, or discovered, not from self-labeling ("I'm a thought leader")
- [ ] **Humility balanced with confidence** — The founder acknowledges what they learned from others or what remains unsolved, while showing conviction in the direction

## Scoring
Must pass **12/16 minimum**. The following items are CRITICAL and must all pass:
- Specific origin moment identified
- Personal "why" is explicit
- Vulnerability is present
- Personal story connects to company mission

Any CRITICAL failure requires the founder story to be revised or re-interviewed.

## Next Steps
- **On Pass:** Integrate founder story into target format (pitch deck, about page, keynote). Run the relevant format-specific checklist.
- **On Fail:** Conduct a deeper founder interview using Dicks' "Homework for Life" technique (squads/storytelling/checklists/dicks/) to surface the real origin moment. Reference Campbell's Hero's Journey (squads/storytelling/checklists/campbell/) for structuring the personal quest. Avoid the instinct to make the story "more impressive"; focus on making it more true and specific.
