# Logline Quality Audit
> Quality gate for verifying the logline contains irony, mental image, audience clarity, stakes, and primal appeal

## Purpose
Ensure the logline is a compelling, concise distillation of the story that sells the concept in a single sentence. A strong logline makes people want to hear more; a weak one gets ignored.

## Prerequisites
- Story concept or draft completed
- Target audience identified
- Core conflict and protagonist defined

## Checklist

### Irony and Hook
- [ ] **Irony Present** — The logline contains an inherent contradiction, tension, or unexpected juxtaposition that provokes curiosity
- [ ] **Hook Is Immediate** — A reader grasps why this story is interesting within the first reading; no re-reads required
- [ ] **Not Generic** — The logline could not describe a hundred other stories; it is specific to this narrative

### Mental Image
- [ ] **Compelling Mental Image** — The logline evokes a vivid scene, situation, or character in the reader's mind without needing additional explanation
- [ ] **Movie Poster Test** — You can imagine a poster, thumbnail, or headline image based on the logline alone

### Audience Clarity
- [ ] **Target Audience Implied** — The logline's language, tone, and subject matter signal who this story is for without stating it explicitly
- [ ] **Broad Enough to Attract** — While the audience is clear, the logline does not feel so niche that it repels adjacent audiences
- [ ] **Age and Tone Appropriate** — Nothing in the logline's framing would confuse the audience about the story's maturity or genre

### Cost and Stakes
- [ ] **Stakes Are Clear** — The logline communicates what the protagonist stands to lose if they fail
- [ ] **Cost of Inaction Implied** — The audience understands why the protagonist cannot simply walk away from the problem
- [ ] **Ticking Clock or Pressure Present** — Urgency is embedded in the logline through deadline, escalation, or irreversibility

### Primal Feeling
- [ ] **Primal Drive Activated** — The logline taps into a fundamental human emotion or motivation (survival, love, justice, freedom, belonging)
- [ ] **Emotional Response Testable** — At least three people from the target audience confirm the logline makes them want to know what happens next
- [ ] **Gut Reaction Positive** — The logline produces a visceral "I want to see/read/hear that" response, not just intellectual interest

### Craft
- [ ] **Under 35 Words** — The logline is concise; every word earns its place
- [ ] **Active Protagonist** — The logline features a hero who does something, not a passive subject to whom things happen
- [ ] **No Spoilers** — The logline reveals the setup and stakes but not the resolution

## Scoring
| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Pass | All 17 items checked; the logline stops people in their tracks |
| Conditional | 13-16 items checked; concept is strong but phrasing needs sharpening |
| Fail | Fewer than 13 items, or irony and stakes are both missing |

## Next Steps
- If irony is missing, flip the protagonist's situation or expectation and rebuild
- Test the logline with five people outside the project; record their immediate reactions
- Refine word count by eliminating adjectives and qualifiers that do not add meaning
