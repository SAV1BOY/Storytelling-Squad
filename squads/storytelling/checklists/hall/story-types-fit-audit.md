# Story Types Fit Audit
> Quality gate for verifying the correct story type is chosen for context with justified rationale

## Purpose
Ensure the narrative uses the right type of business story (Value Story, Founder Story, Customer Story, Purpose Story) for the specific communication context. Using the wrong type wastes the audience's attention and misses the strategic opportunity.

## Prerequisites
- Communication objective defined
- Target audience and their decision stage identified
- Understanding of Kindra Hall's four story types from "Stories That Stick"

## Checklist

### Story Type Identification
- [ ] **Type Explicitly Named** — The story is categorized as one of the four types: Value Story (shows the product/service value), Founder Story (origin/motivation), Customer Story (social proof), or Purpose Story (mission/why)
- [ ] **Type Matches the Objective** — The chosen type aligns with what the communication needs to achieve (Value for conversion, Founder for trust, Customer for proof, Purpose for alignment)
- [ ] **Alternative Types Considered** — At least one other story type was evaluated and rejected with a documented reason

### Value Story Criteria (if applicable)
- [ ] **Product/Service Value Shown** — The story demonstrates the value of the offering through a specific example, not a feature list
- [ ] **Transformation Is Visible** — The audience sees a before-and-after that makes the value tangible and desirable

### Founder Story Criteria (if applicable)
- [ ] **Origin Is Authentic** — The founding story is truthful, specific, and reveals genuine motivation, not a polished corporate myth
- [ ] **Connection to Customer Built** — The founder's struggle or insight connects to the customer's own experience or values

### Customer Story Criteria (if applicable)
- [ ] **Real Customer Featured** — The story features an actual customer with verifiable details, not a composite or fictional example
- [ ] **Customer Is Relatable** — The featured customer resembles the target audience in situation, challenges, or aspirations

### Purpose Story Criteria (if applicable)
- [ ] **Mission Connected to Action** — The purpose story does not just inspire; it connects the mission to a specific behavior or commitment
- [ ] **Purpose Is Authentic** — The stated purpose is genuine and demonstrated through the organization's actions, not just its marketing

### Context Fit
- [ ] **Audience Stage Appropriate** — The story type fits where the audience is in their journey (awareness, consideration, decision, loyalty)
- [ ] **Channel Appropriate** — The story type works for the delivery channel (website, pitch meeting, social media, keynote, email)
- [ ] **Timing Right** — The story is deployed at a moment when the audience is receptive to this type of narrative (e.g., not a Founder Story when the audience wants proof)
- [ ] **Length Appropriate** — The story's depth and length match the context; a 10-minute Founder Story is wrong for a social media post

## Scoring
| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Pass | All applicable items checked (core items + type-specific items); the right story is in the right place |
| Conditional | Most items checked but type-context fit is imperfect or alternative types were not considered |
| Fail | Wrong story type for the objective, or story type is unidentifiable |

## Next Steps
- If the type does not fit, re-evaluate the communication objective and select the type that directly serves it
- If multiple types are needed, create a story portfolio with one of each type for different touchpoints
- Test the story with audience proxies and ask what they took away; if the takeaway mismatches the objective, the type may be wrong
