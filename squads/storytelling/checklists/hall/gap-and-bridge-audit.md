# Gap and Bridge Audit
> Quality gate for verifying Normal-Explosion-New Normal structure with surprising gap and satisfying bridge

## Purpose
Ensure the story follows the three-part structure Kindra Hall describes: Normal (the status quo), Explosion (the event that changes everything), and New Normal (the transformed state). The gap created by the explosion must be surprising, and the bridge to the new normal must be satisfying.

## Prerequisites
- Complete story draft
- Three-part structure mapped
- Transformation identified

## Checklist

### Normal (Status Quo)
- [ ] **Normal Is Established** — The audience understands the protagonist's world, habits, beliefs, or situation before the disruption
- [ ] **Normal Is Specific** — The status quo is described with concrete details, not generalities; the audience can picture this world
- [ ] **Normal Contains the Seed** — Something in the normal state hints at or enables the explosion that is coming, even if the audience does not notice it yet
- [ ] **Normal Is Relatable** — The audience recognizes elements of their own experience in the protagonist's normal world

### Explosion (The Gap)
- [ ] **Explosion Is Identifiable** — A single event, moment, or discovery creates the rupture between normal and new normal; it is not gradual
- [ ] **Gap Is Surprising** — The explosion defies the audience's expectations; it is not the obvious next thing that would happen
- [ ] **Gap Is Significant** — The explosion is large enough to make the old normal impossible to sustain; there is no going back
- [ ] **Gap Creates Emotional Response** — The audience reacts to the explosion: shock, delight, fear, curiosity, empathy, or concern

### New Normal (The Bridge)
- [ ] **New Normal Shown** — The story does not end at the explosion; it shows the transformed state the protagonist inhabits afterward
- [ ] **Bridge Is Satisfying** — The journey from explosion to new normal feels complete and earned; the audience is not left with unresolved dissonance
- [ ] **New Normal Is Different** — The new normal is qualitatively different from the old normal; the transformation is real and visible
- [ ] **New Normal Contains the Lesson** — The audience can extract meaning or insight from the contrast between old normal and new normal

### Structural Craft
- [ ] **Proportions Are Balanced** — The Normal section is long enough to ground the audience but not so long it delays the explosion; roughly 30/20/50 or justified variation
- [ ] **Transitions Are Clean** — The shift from Normal to Explosion and from Explosion to New Normal is clear; the audience is not confused about which phase they are in
- [ ] **All Three Parts Present** — No phase is missing; stories that end at the explosion or skip the normal state are incomplete

## Scoring
| Rating | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| Pass | All 15 items checked; the three-part arc is clear, surprising, and satisfying |
| Conditional | 11-14 items checked; structure is present but the gap or bridge needs strengthening |
| Fail | Fewer than 11 items, or a phase is entirely missing |

## Next Steps
- If the explosion is predictable, look for a less obvious angle or an earlier moment of disruption
- If the new normal is underdeveloped, extend it with specific evidence of transformation
- Map the three phases visually and check proportions; adjust pacing if one phase dominates inappropriately
