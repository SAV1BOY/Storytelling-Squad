# Narrative Word Choices

## Purpose
A curated lexicon organized by emotional tone and narrative function. Use these words
intentionally to shift the reader's emotional state.

---

## Power Words
Words that convey authority, certainty, and impact.

### Verbs
command, shatter, ignite, forge, seize, launch, conquer, master, crush, dominate,
transform, revolutionize, unleash, propel, accelerate, demolish, obliterate, claim,
build, architect, engineer, dismantle, reclaim, own

### Nouns
authority, edge, leverage, dominance, breakthrough, force, momentum, empire, arsenal,
fortress, milestone, pinnacle, summit, weapon, foundation, backbone, powerhouse

### Adjectives
unstoppable, relentless, fierce, unshakable, formidable, proven, decisive, bold,
audacious, fearless, unyielding, iron-clad, battle-tested, razor-sharp

---

## Soft / Empathy Words
Words that create warmth, connection, and emotional safety.

### Verbs
embrace, nurture, cradle, welcome, comfort, soothe, listen, understand, hold, care,
cherish, protect, honor, appreciate, acknowledge, recognize, support, uplift

### Nouns
warmth, kindness, compassion, grace, tenderness, empathy, belonging, comfort, haven,
sanctuary, home, heart, soul, bond, trust, gentleness, patience

### Adjectives
gentle, tender, warm, soft, kind, compassionate, generous, thoughtful, quiet,
patient, steady, faithful, genuine, heartfelt, sincere, loving

---

## Urgency Words
Words that compress time and demand immediate attention.

### Verbs
rush, hurry, act, seize, grab, claim, secure, lock in, reserve, don't wait,
don't miss, move, sprint, leap, decide, commit, start, begin now

### Nouns
deadline, countdown, window, opportunity, moment, tipping point, last chance,
threshold, breaking point, expiration, clock, race

### Adjectives / Adverbs
now, today, immediately, urgent, limited, final, closing, fleeting, vanishing,
fast-approaching, time-sensitive, once-only, critical, last

### Phrases
- "Before midnight tonight"
- "Only [N] spots remaining"
- "The window is closing"
- "This won't be available again until [date]"
- "Every day you wait costs you [specific amount]"

---

## Curiosity Words
Words that open loops and create information gaps.

### Verbs
discover, uncover, reveal, unlock, decode, explore, investigate, peek, glimpse,
question, wonder, stumble upon, notice, suspect

### Nouns
secret, mystery, puzzle, clue, key, hidden, truth, surprise, anomaly, riddle,
paradox, twist, revelation, discovery, insider

### Adjectives
surprising, unexpected, counterintuitive, hidden, little-known, overlooked,
forgotten, strange, unusual, curious, unlikely, shocking, eye-opening

### Phrases
- "What most people don't realize is…"
- "The part nobody talks about"
- "Here's what we found when we looked closer"
- "There's a reason this works — and it's not what you think"
- "The answer was hiding in plain sight"

---

## Trust / Credibility Words
Words that build confidence and reduce perceived risk.

### Verbs
prove, verify, confirm, validate, guarantee, demonstrate, certify, ensure,
back, support, document, test, measure, audit, track

### Nouns
proof, evidence, data, results, track record, guarantee, certification, study,
research, testimonial, case study, benchmark, standard, credential

### Adjectives
proven, verified, certified, documented, tested, reliable, consistent,
transparent, accountable, trusted, reputable, established, peer-reviewed

---

## Transformation Words
Words that signal change, growth, and before/after dynamics.

### Verbs
transform, evolve, shift, convert, elevate, upgrade, reimagine, reinvent,
redesign, rewrite, reshape, rebuild, transcend, bloom, emerge, rise, awaken

### Nouns
transformation, metamorphosis, evolution, breakthrough, turning point,
milestone, rebirth, renaissance, pivot, leap, shift, awakening, ascent

### Adjectives
transformed, renewed, reimagined, elevated, evolved, reborn, revitalized,
unrecognizable (in a good way), redefined, liberated, unleashed

### Phrases
- "From [painful before] to [desirable after]"
- "That was the turning point"
- "Everything looked different on the other side"
- "What used to take [X] now takes [Y]"
- "They didn't just improve. They became something new."

---

## Sensory Words
Words that activate the five senses and create vivid mental images.

### Visual
gleaming, shadowed, crimson, pale, vivid, blinding, flickering, translucent,
stark, hazy, luminous, muted, sharp, blurred, radiant, dim, golden

### Auditory
whisper, roar, hum, crackle, buzz, thunder, murmur, echo, chime, rustle,
clatter, silence, ring, snap, hiss, drone, thud

### Tactile
smooth, rough, sharp, soft, cold, warm, sticky, slippery, gritty, velvety,
prickly, heavy, light, firm, brittle, supple, damp

### Olfactory
fresh, musty, acrid, sweet, smoky, earthy, floral, metallic, pungent, crisp,
stale, fragrant, rotten, clean, woody, salty

### Gustatory
bitter, sweet, sour, salty, savory, rich, bland, tangy, spicy, sharp,
smooth, crisp, ripe, raw, burnt, honeyed

---

## Words by Narrative Function

| Function | Words |
|----------|-------|
| **Opening a scene** | imagine, picture this, there was, once, I remember, the day |
| **Building tension** | but, however, except, until, then, suddenly, without warning |
| **Releasing tension** | finally, at last, and then, relief, breathe, safe, home |
| **Inserting proof** | according to, research shows, in a study of, data from, X% |
| **Transitioning** | meanwhile, on the other hand, but here's the thing, fast-forward |
| **Closing** | and that's why, the lesson, what this means, so the next time |
