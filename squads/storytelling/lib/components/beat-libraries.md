# Beat Libraries by Type

## Purpose
A beat is the smallest unit of story change — a single shift in value, emotion, or information. This library catalogs reusable beats organized by narrative context so storytellers can assemble sequences rapidly.

---

## Pitch Beats

### 1. The World As-Is Beat
- **Function:** Establish the status quo the audience recognizes
- **Trigger:** Audience nods in agreement
- **Example:** "Every morning, 10,000 sales reps open their CRM and dread what they see."

### 2. The Crack Beat
- **Function:** Introduce the first sign that the status quo is breaking
- **Trigger:** Curiosity — audience leans in
- **Example:** "But last quarter, one team stopped using their CRM entirely — and closed 40% more deals."

### 3. The Stakes Beat
- **Function:** Make the audience feel what's at risk if nothing changes
- **Trigger:** Tension — fear of loss
- **Example:** "Companies that ignore this shift will lose their best reps within 18 months."

### 4. The Mechanism Beat
- **Function:** Reveal the unique insight or method that resolves the tension
- **Trigger:** Relief mixed with intrigue
- **Example:** "We discovered that pipeline velocity depends on three conversation patterns, not data entry."

### 5. The Proof Beat
- **Function:** Provide evidence that the mechanism works
- **Trigger:** Credibility — trust increases
- **Example:** "Acme Corp deployed this in Q2. Rep retention went to 94%. Pipeline grew 3.1x."

## Case Study Beats

### 1. The Situation Beat
- **Function:** Paint the client's world before intervention
- **Key elements:** Industry, size, specific pain, emotional weight

### 2. The Turning Point Beat
- **Function:** The moment the client decided to act
- **Key elements:** Catalyst event, internal champion, risk tolerance

### 3. The Implementation Beat
- **Function:** Show the journey, not just the destination
- **Key elements:** Timeline, obstacles overcome, team dynamics

### 4. The Result Beat
- **Function:** Deliver quantified outcomes
- **Key elements:** Metrics before/after, timeline to results, unexpected benefits

### 5. The Reflection Beat
- **Function:** Client in their own words on what changed
- **Key elements:** Direct quote, emotional shift, future outlook

## Brand Story Beats

### 1. Origin Wound Beat
- Founder's personal frustration or injustice that sparked the company

### 2. Epiphany Beat
- The moment of insight that became the company's core thesis

### 3. First Believer Beat
- The first customer, investor, or partner who validated the vision

### 4. Trial by Fire Beat
- The existential threat the company survived

### 5. Mission Crystallization Beat
- The moment the brand's purpose became clear and unshakable

---

## Video Narrative Beats

### 1. Cold Open Beat (0-3 seconds)
- Pattern interrupt — visual or verbal shock that stops the scroll
- Must create an open loop the viewer needs closed

### 2. Context Beat (3-10 seconds)
- Rapidly orient the viewer: who, where, why this matters to them

### 3. Escalation Beat (10-30 seconds)
- Raise stakes through conflict, contrast, or revelation

### 4. Payoff Beat (30-45 seconds)
- Deliver the transformation, proof, or insight promised by the hook

### 5. Echo Beat (final 5 seconds)
- Callback to the opening that reframes the entire narrative

---

## Assembly Rules

1. **Never skip the Stakes Beat** — without stakes, no beat sequence has tension
2. **Proof Beats must follow Mechanism Beats** — claims without evidence erode trust
3. **Every beat sequence needs at least one emotional beat** — logic informs, emotion moves
4. **Vary beat length** — monotonous rhythm kills attention
5. **Test beat order** — the same beats rearranged can double or halve impact
