# Deck Scoring Rubric

## Purpose
A rubric for evaluating presentation decks as narrative vehicles. A deck is not a document — it is a visual story structure. This rubric assesses whether the deck amplifies the narrative or undermines it. Great decks are felt before they are read.

---

## Scoring Categories

### 1. Opening Slide Impact (0-12 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 10-12 | Opening slide creates an immediate emotional or intellectual response. Visual or text hook that stops the audience's internal monologue. No logo slide, no agenda slide, no "About Us" slide. |
| 7-9 | Opening has some impact but defaults to convention — title + subtitle format with a relevant image. |
| 4-6 | Opens with standard corporate template. Logo, company name, date. Functional but forgettable. |
| 0-3 | Opens with an agenda or table of contents. The audience is already disengaging. |

### 2. Visual Storytelling (0-15 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 13-15 | Images, graphics, and layout tell the story independently. Slides could be understood without narration. Visual metaphors reinforce the narrative. Consistent visual language throughout. |
| 10-12 | Good visual design. Images support the narrative. Some slides rely too heavily on text. |
| 7-9 | Clean design but visuals are decorative, not narrative. Stock photos without story function. |
| 4-6 | Text-heavy slides with minimal visual support. Design is professional but uninspired. |
| 0-3 | Wall-of-text slides. Clip art. Inconsistent design. The deck fights the narrative. |

### 3. Contrast Rhythm (0-15 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 13-15 | Clear Duarte sparkline — "what is" and "what could be" slides alternate with intentional rhythm. Contrast is visual (dark/light), verbal (problem/solution), and emotional (tension/relief). |
| 10-12 | Some contrast present. Problem and solution sections are distinct. Rhythm could be more deliberate. |
| 7-9 | Sections exist but the deck feels monotone. All slides have the same energy level. |
| 4-6 | No contrast. The deck is a continuous flow of information without variation. |
| 0-3 | Counter-productive rhythm — the deck peaks too early or has no peaks at all. |

### 4. Data Presentation (0-12 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 10-12 | Data is visualized for impact, not information density. One number per slide when possible. Charts tell a story — the audience sees the insight without reading a legend. Annotations guide interpretation. |
| 7-9 | Data is clear and well-visualized. Minor clutter or density issues on 1-2 slides. |
| 4-6 | Data is present but poorly visualized. Multiple metrics competing for attention. Audience has to work to extract the meaning. |
| 0-3 | Data slides are spreadsheets projected on screen. Tiny fonts, dense tables, no visual hierarchy. |

### 5. Slide Economy (0-12 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 10-12 | Every slide earns its place. No redundant slides. One idea per slide. The deck is the minimum length needed to tell the story. Removing any slide would break the narrative. |
| 7-9 | Mostly lean. 2-3 slides could be consolidated or removed. |
| 4-6 | 20-30% of slides are filler — repeating points, unnecessary transitions, or content that should be an appendix. |
| 0-3 | Bloated deck. Could be cut in half without losing anything essential. |

### 6. Text Discipline (0-10 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 9-10 | Maximum 6 words per headline. Body text is rare and purposeful. The deck is designed to be spoken to, not read. Font sizes are large and confident. |
| 7-8 | Mostly disciplined. 1-2 slides have more text than ideal. Headlines are clear. |
| 5-6 | Mixed — some slides are clean, others have paragraphs. Audience reads ahead of speaker. |
| 3-4 | Text-heavy throughout. Speaker is competing with the slides for attention. |
| 0-2 | Full paragraphs on most slides. Audience is reading, not listening. |

### 7. Narrative Completeness (0-12 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 10-12 | Deck contains all five elements: hook, conflict/problem, mechanism/solution, proof, and CTA. The narrative arc is complete — beginning, middle, end. The audience knows exactly what to think, feel, and do. |
| 7-9 | Most elements present. One element is weak or missing. Arc is present but incomplete. |
| 4-6 | 2-3 narrative elements missing. Deck feels like a brochure, not a story. |
| 0-3 | No narrative structure. Collection of information slides without arc. |

### 8. Closing Power (0-12 points)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 10-12 | Final slide(s) create emotional resonance and a clear call to action. Callback to the opening creates closure. The audience knows exactly what happens next. Last impression is the strongest. |
| 7-9 | Clear CTA. Ending is solid but lacks emotional punch. No callback. |
| 4-6 | Ends with a contact slide or generic "Thank You." Functional but forgettable. |
| 0-3 | Deck fades out. No clear ending, no CTA, no emotional close. |

---

## Total Score Interpretation

| Score | Rating | Recommendation |
|-------|--------|---------------|
| 90-100 | Exceptional | Presentation-ready. Archive as exemplar. |
| 80-89 | Strong | Minor visual or structural refinements needed. |
| 70-79 | Good | Functional deck. Narrative improvements will elevate it significantly. |
| 60-69 | Adequate | Needs both visual and narrative work. Redesign weakest sections. |
| 50-59 | Below Standard | Significant rebuild required. Start with narrative structure, then design. |
| Below 50 | Does Not Meet Standard | Start over. Define the narrative arc before opening the design tool. |

---

## Pre-Delivery Checklist

Before presenting any deck, verify:
- [ ] Opening slide creates an emotional or intellectual response (not a logo)
- [ ] No slide has more than 20 words of visible text
- [ ] At least 3 slides use contrast (what is / what could be)
- [ ] Every data slide has a clear "so what" annotation
- [ ] The final slide has a specific, singular CTA
- [ ] The deck can be presented in the allotted time minus 20% (buffer for Q&A)
